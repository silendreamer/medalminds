#### Organelle Quick Reference
| Organelle | Key Function | Unique Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Nucleus | DNA storage, transcription | Double membrane + NPCs |
| Rough ER | Protein synthesis for secretion | Ribosomes on surface |
| Smooth ER | Lipid synthesis, detox, Ca²⁺ | No ribosomes |
| Golgi | Sorting, modification, packaging | Cis/trans polarity |
| Mitochondria | ATP synthesis | Own circular DNA, 70S ribosomes |
| Chloroplast | Photosynthesis | Own circular DNA, 70S ribosomes |
| Lysosome | Intracellular digestion | Low pH (~5), 50 hydrolases |
| Peroxisome | Fatty acid oxidation, H₂O₂ detox | No surrounding membrane on enzymes |
| Ribosome | Protein synthesis | 80S (eukaryote) or 70S (prokaryote) |
| Vacuole | Storage, turgor (plants) | Large in plant cells |

#### 15 Must-Know Cell Biology Facts
1. Cell theory: all cells come from pre-existing cells (Virchow).
2. Endosymbiotic theory: mitochondria from α-proteobacteria; chloroplasts from cyanobacteria.
3. Evidence: own DNA, 70S ribosomes, double membrane, binary fission.
4. Rough ER → Golgi (via COPII vesicles) → plasma membrane or lysosomes.
5. Mannose-6-phosphate = lysosomal targeting signal.
6. Microtubules: 25 nm, α/β-tubulin; actin: 7 nm; IFs: 8–12 nm.
7. Kinesin = plus-end directed (away from nucleus); dynein = minus-end directed.
8. Taxol stabilizes microtubules; vinblastine/vincristine prevent polymerization.
9. Lysosome pH = ~5, maintained by V-type ATPase proton pump.
10. Tay-Sachs: hexosaminidase A deficiency; GM₂ ganglioside accumulates.
11. mtDNA = 37 genes; maternally inherited.
12. NPCs transport proteins in (NLS) and RNA out (NES) of nucleus.
13. Dynamic instability: MT growth and catastrophe driven by GTP hydrolysis on tubulin.
14. Svedberg unit (S) = sedimentation coefficient — larger = sediments faster.
15. Prokaryote ribosomes: 70S (30S + 50S); eukaryote: 80S (40S + 60S).

2. Describe three structural or biochemical features that distinguish smooth ER from rough ER.
3. Explain why drugs targeting 70S ribosomes (like chloramphenicol) can be used as antibiotics without harming human cells.
