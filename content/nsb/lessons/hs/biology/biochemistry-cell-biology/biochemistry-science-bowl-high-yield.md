#### Top 20 High-Yield Facts
1. Water's polarity comes from oxygen being more electronegative than hydrogen.
2. Hydrogen bonds form between δ+ hydrogen and δ− oxygen of adjacent water molecules.
3. Water's high specific heat = 4.18 J/g·°C; high heat of vaporization = 2,260 J/g.
4. pH = −log[H⁺]; each unit = 10× concentration difference.
5. Buffers work best within ±1 pH unit of their pKa.
6. Blood pH = 7.35–7.45; buffered by bicarbonate (H₂CO₃/HCO₃⁻).
7. Carbohydrate formula: (CH₂O)n.
8. Starch = α-1,4 (and α-1,6 in amylopectin) glycosidic bonds; cellulose = β-1,4.
9. Phospholipids are amphipathic — hydrophilic head, hydrophobic tails.
10. Cholesterol is precursor to all steroid hormones and bile salts.
11. Peptide bonds form between carboxyl of one amino acid and amino of the next.
12. Four levels of protein structure: primary (sequence) → secondary (α-helix/β-sheet) → tertiary (3D shape) → quaternary (subunit assembly).
13. Disulfide bonds (between cysteines) stabilize tertiary/quaternary structure.
14. Denaturation disrupts secondary/tertiary/quaternary structure — peptide bonds remain intact.
15. DNA: A pairs with T (2 H-bonds); G pairs with C (3 H-bonds).
16. RNA uses ribose and uracil instead of deoxyribose and thymine.
17. rRNA is the catalytic component of ribosomes; mRNA carries code; tRNA carries amino acids.
18. ATP hydrolysis releases ~30.5 kJ/mol; product is ADP + Pᵢ.
19. Purines (2 rings) = adenine and guanine; pyrimidines (1 ring) = cytosine, thymine, uracil.
20. Sickle cell disease: single amino acid change (Glu→Val) in β-globin causes hemoglobin polymerization.

#### Common Traps
- "Strongest bond in biology" → covalent bonds (NOT hydrogen bonds); hydrogen bonds are INDIVIDUALLY weak but collectively strong.
- "Cellulose and starch are both glucose polymers" → TRUE, but β vs. α linkage makes cellulose indigestible.
- "Denaturation breaks peptide bonds" → FALSE; denaturation only disrupts non-covalent interactions (and disulfide bonds in some cases).
- "DNA has more H-bonds per base pair than RNA" → Neither DNA nor RNA forms interstrand H-bonds with itself (RNA is single-stranded). Within DNA: G-C has 3, A-T has 2.

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "This type of bond, formed between the partial positive hydrogen and partial negative oxygen of adjacent water molecules, gives water its high surface tension and specific heat." *(Answer: hydrogen bond)*
2. "This property of phospholipids, meaning they have both a hydrophilic head and hydrophobic tail, drives spontaneous bilayer formation." *(Answer: amphipathic)*
3. "This level of protein structure describes the three-dimensional folding of a single polypeptide, stabilized by R-group interactions." *(Answer: tertiary structure)*
4. "This nucleotide base, found only in RNA and not in DNA, pairs with adenine." *(Answer: uracil)*

#### Review Questions
1. A Science Bowl question asks: "This chemical bond, formed by dehydration synthesis between amino acids, links the carboxyl group of one to the amino group of the next." What is the answer?
2. What is Chargaff's rule and what does it reveal about DNA structure?
3. Name three properties of water that result directly from hydrogen bonding.
