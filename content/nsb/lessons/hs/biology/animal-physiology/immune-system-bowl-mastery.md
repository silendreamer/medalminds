#### Bowl Toss-up Patterns
"For 10 points, what type of white blood cells produce antibodies?" → **B cells** (plasma cells are differentiated B cells)

"For 10 points, what class of antibody is most abundant in blood and is the only antibody that crosses the placenta?" → **IgG**

"For 10 points, what type of T cell directly kills infected cells by releasing perforin and granzymes?" → **cytotoxic T cell** (CD8+ T cell)

"For 10 points, what surface molecule on all nucleated cells presents intracellular peptides to CD8+ cytotoxic T cells?" → **MHC class I**

"For 10 points, what pathogen targets CD4+ helper T cells and eventually causes acquired immunodeficiency syndrome?" → **HIV** (human immunodeficiency virus)

#### High-Yield Facts
- B cells mature in **bone marrow**; T cells mature in **thymus**
- IgM: first antibody in primary response (pentamer)
- IgA: secretory (mucus, breast milk, saliva)
- Dendritic cells bridge innate and adaptive immunity
- Herd immunity threshold for measles: ~95%

#### Review Questions
1. Name all five antibody classes and their primary functions or locations.
2. What is the difference between the primary and secondary immune response in terms of speed and antibody concentration?
3. Explain why HIV infection eventually leads to susceptibility to a wide range of pathogens, including those that healthy immune systems easily control.
