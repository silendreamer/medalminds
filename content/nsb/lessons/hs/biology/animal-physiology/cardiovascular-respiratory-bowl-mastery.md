#### Bowl Toss-up Patterns
"For 10 points, what structure in the right atrium spontaneously depolarizes and sets the heart rate?" → **SA node** (sinoatrial node; pacemaker)

"For 10 points, what molecule in red blood cells carries oxygen and contains iron in its heme group?" → **hemoglobin**

"For 10 points, what effect describes the rightward shift of the oxygen dissociation curve in the presence of increased CO2 or decreased pH, causing hemoglobin to release oxygen more readily?" → **Bohr effect**

"For 10 points, what equation gives cardiac output as a product of two variables?" → **CO = stroke volume × heart rate**

"For 10 points, what gas, produced during anaerobic metabolism, inhibits muscle contraction by dropping pH?" → Trick: lactic **acid** / **lactate** (produces H+; lactic acid drops pH → fatigue)

#### High-Yield Facts
- Lub = AV valves close (S1); Dub = semilunar valves close (S2)
- Alveoli: ~300 million; surface area ~70 m²; lined with surfactant
- CO2: 70% as bicarbonate, 23% carbaminohemoglobin, 7% dissolved
- CO binds Hb 200× more than O2
- Right ventricle → pulmonary circuit; left ventricle → systemic circuit

#### Review Questions
1. A bowl question asks: "What is the name for the temporary oxygen debt that persists after exercise and represents the extra oxygen consumed to restore homeostasis?" → What is the answer?
2. Trace the electrical signal through the heart from the SA node to ventricular contraction, naming each structure.
3. Why do premature infants often lack surfactant, and what physiological problem does this cause?
