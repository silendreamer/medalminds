#### Bowl Toss-up Patterns
"For 10 points, what enzyme in saliva begins the digestion of starch by breaking it into maltose?" → **salivary amylase**

"For 10 points, what substance, produced by the liver and stored in the gallbladder, emulsifies dietary fats in the duodenum?" → **bile**

"For 10 points, what finger-like projections of the small intestinal mucosa increase surface area for nutrient absorption?" → **villi**

"For 10 points, what inactive precursor to trypsin is secreted by the pancreas and activated by enterokinase in the duodenum?" → **trypsinogen**

"For 10 points, what are the finger-like extensions of the plasma membrane on intestinal cells that form the 'brush border'?" → **microvilli**

#### High-Yield Facts
- Pepsin works in **acid** (stomach pH 1.5–2); requires HCl for activation
- Bile: emulsifier (not enzyme); produced in **liver**, stored in **gallbladder**
- Lipids absorbed into **lacteals** (lymphatic capillaries) as chylomicrons
- Lactase deficiency → lactose intolerance → gas, bloating, diarrhea
- Liver: only site of **urea cycle** (converts toxic ammonia to urea for excretion)
