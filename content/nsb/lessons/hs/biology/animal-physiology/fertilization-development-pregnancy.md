#### Fertilization and Early Development
Fertilization: sperm penetrates secondary oocyte in fallopian tube → acrosome reaction (hydrolytic enzymes digest zona pellucida) → sperm-egg fusion → **cortical reaction** (release of enzymes hardens zona pellucida, preventing polyspermy) → secondary oocyte completes meiosis II → pronuclei fuse → zygote (2n). **Cleavage**: rapid mitotic divisions; cells (blastomeres) get smaller; no growth; 8-cell stage (morula) → **blastocyst** (inner cell mass = embryo; trophoblast = placenta) → implants in endometrium (day 6–10). **hCG** secretion by trophoblast maintains corpus luteum → progesterone continues.

**Gastrulation**: blastocyst reorganizes into three germ layers: **ectoderm** (skin, nervous system), **mesoderm** (muscle, bone, circulatory system, kidneys), **endoderm** (digestive tract lining, lungs, liver, pancreas). **Neurulation**: ectoderm folds to form neural tube → brain and spinal cord. Neural tube defects (spina bifida, anencephaly) linked to folic acid deficiency.

#### Placenta and Extraembryonic Membranes
**Placenta**: fetal and maternal blood do not mix; nutrients, O2, and antibodies (IgG) pass from mother to fetus; CO2 and waste pass to mother. **Amnion**: fluid-filled sac cushions fetus (amniotic fluid). **Chorion** forms placenta with uterine wall. **Yolk sac** (nutrient in birds/reptiles; site of early blood cell formation in humans). **Allantois** (waste storage in birds/reptiles; forms umbilical blood vessels in humans).
