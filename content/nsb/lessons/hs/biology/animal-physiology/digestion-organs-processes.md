#### Overview of Digestion
**Mechanical digestion**: physical breakdown without changing chemical structure — chewing, churning in stomach, peristalsis. **Chemical digestion**: enzymatic hydrolysis of polymers into monomers — polysaccharides → monosaccharides, proteins → amino acids, lipids → fatty acids + glycerol. Alimentary canal: mouth → pharynx → esophagus → stomach → small intestine (duodenum, jejunum, ileum) → large intestine (colon) → rectum → anus. Accessory glands: salivary glands, liver, gallbladder, pancreas.

#### Key Digestive Enzymes and Sites
- **Mouth**: salivary amylase (starch → maltose); lingual lipase (minor fat digestion)
- **Stomach**: pepsin (protein → peptides); HCl (pH 1.5–2; activates pepsin, kills bacteria); gastric lipase
- **Duodenum**: pancreatic amylase, lipase, protease (trypsin, chymotrypsin, carboxypeptidase); bile (from liver/gallbladder — emulsifies fats, not an enzyme)
- **Small intestine brush border**: maltase, sucrase, lactase (disaccharides → monosaccharides); peptidases (dipeptides → amino acids)
- **Large intestine**: water and electrolyte reabsorption; gut bacteria (microbiome); no enzymes secreted

#### Absorption in the Small Intestine
Surface area maximized by: **circular folds** → **villi** (finger-like projections) → **microvilli** (brush border on each villus cell) → ~600× increase in area. Monosaccharides: absorbed by cotransport with Na+ (SGLT1) → capillaries → portal vein → liver. Amino acids: similar Na+ cotransport. Fatty acids and glycerol: absorbed into intestinal cells → reassembled into triglycerides → packaged with cholesterol and proteins into **chylomicrons** → enter **lacteals** (lymphatic vessels in villi) → lymph system → thoracic duct → blood.
