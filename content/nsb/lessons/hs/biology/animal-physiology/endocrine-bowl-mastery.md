#### Bowl Toss-up Patterns
"For 10 points, what anterior pituitary hormone stimulates the adrenal cortex to release cortisol?" → **ACTH** (adrenocorticotropic hormone)

"For 10 points, what endocrine gland produces calcitonin, T3, and T4?" → **thyroid gland**

"For 10 points, what hormone from the posterior pituitary stimulates uterine contractions and milk letdown?" → **oxytocin**

"For 10 points, what hormone from the parathyroid gland raises blood calcium by stimulating osteoclast activity?" → **PTH** (parathyroid hormone)

"For 10 points, what second messenger, produced from ATP by adenylyl cyclase, mediates the effects of many peptide hormones?" → **cAMP** (cyclic AMP)

#### High-Yield Facts
- Anterior pituitary hormones: FSH, LH, TSH, ACTH, GH, prolactin (tropic hormones)
- Posterior pituitary: ADH (vasopressin) and oxytocin — produced in hypothalamus
- Steroids synthesized from **cholesterol**
- Epinephrine from **adrenal medulla** (fast); cortisol from **adrenal cortex** (slow)
- Melatonin from **pineal gland** → regulates circadian rhythms

1. Name the four hormones released by the adrenal gland, which layer produces each, and the general function of each.
2. A bowl question asks: "What structure connects the hypothalamus to the anterior pituitary via a blood vessel network?" → What is the answer?
3. Why does blocking the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system lower blood pressure?
