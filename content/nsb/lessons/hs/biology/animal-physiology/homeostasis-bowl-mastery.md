#### Bowl Toss-up Patterns
"For 10 points, what hormone, released from the posterior pituitary, increases water reabsorption in the collecting duct of the nephron?" → **ADH** (antidiuretic hormone, vasopressin)

"For 10 points, what steroid hormone from the adrenal cortex promotes sodium reabsorption in the distal convoluted tubule?" → **aldosterone**

"For 10 points, what hormone, released from alpha cells of the islets of Langerhans, raises blood glucose by stimulating glycogenolysis in the liver?" → **glucagon**

"For 10 points, what type of feedback loop is used in most homeostatic mechanisms, where the response opposes the original stimulus?" → **negative feedback**

"For 10 points, what are the functional units of the kidney?" → **nephrons**

#### High-Yield Facts
- Insulin from **beta** cells; glucagon from **alpha** cells
- ADH targets **collecting duct**; aldosterone targets **DCT and collecting duct**
- Ectotherms = temperature from environment; endotherms = metabolic heat
- Blood pH: 7.35–7.45 (slightly basic); deviation = acidosis/alkalosis

#### Review Questions
1. A bowl question asks: "What type of cells in the islets of Langerhans secrete insulin?" → What is the answer?
2. What effect does a high-protein, low-carbohydrate diet have on glucagon vs. insulin secretion?
3. Why is the loop of Henle essential for producing concentrated urine?
