#### Heart Anatomy
Four chambers: right atrium (receives deoxygenated blood from vena cava) → right ventricle (pumps to pulmonary artery → lungs) → left atrium (receives oxygenated blood from pulmonary veins) → left ventricle (pumps to aorta → body). Left ventricular wall is thicker (higher pressure needed for systemic circulation). **Valves**: atrioventricular (AV) valves — tricuspid (right) and bicuspid/mitral (left) — prevent backflow from ventricles to atria; semilunar valves — pulmonary and aortic — prevent backflow from arteries to ventricles. Heart sounds ("lub-dub"): AV valve closure (S1) and semilunar valve closure (S2).

#### The Cardiac Cycle
**Diastole**: heart relaxes; blood fills atria and ventricles passively; ~70% of ventricular filling. **Atrial systole**: atria contract; complete ventricular filling (~30% more). **Ventricular systole**: ventricles contract; AV valves close (S1); pressure builds; semilunar valves open; blood ejected into pulmonary artery and aorta; semilunar valves close (S2) when pressure falls. Cardiac output (CO) = Stroke volume (SV) × Heart rate (HR). At rest: CO ≈ 70 mL/beat × 70 beats/min ≈ 5 L/min = entire blood volume.

#### Intrinsic Conduction System
**SA node** (sinoatrial node; "pacemaker"): spontaneously depolarizes ~70 times/min; located in right atrium; initiates each heartbeat. Signal spreads through atria → **AV node** (atrioventricular node): delays signal ~0.1 sec (allows ventricles to fill) → **Bundle of His** → **Purkinje fibers**: spread signal rapidly through ventricular walls from apex upward → coordinated ventricular contraction. ECG (electrocardiogram) detects: P wave (atrial depolarization), QRS complex (ventricular depolarization), T wave (ventricular repolarization).
