#### The Biological Species Concept
Ernst Mayr's biological species concept (BSC): a species is a group of actually or potentially interbreeding populations that are reproductively isolated from other such groups. Strength: operationally defines species by what keeps them separate (gene pools). Weakness: cannot be applied to asexual organisms (bacteria), fossils, or populations that never encounter each other (allopatric populations). Alternative concepts: morphological species concept (defined by physical traits — used for fossils and asexual organisms), phylogenetic species concept (smallest diagnosable cluster sharing a common ancestor), ecological species concept (defined by ecological role/niche).

#### Reproductive Isolating Mechanisms
Prezygotic (prevent mating or fertilization): **habitat isolation** (live in different microhabitats), **temporal isolation** (breed at different times/seasons), **behavioral isolation** (different courtship displays/calls — most important in animals), **mechanical isolation** (incompatible reproductive structures), **gametic isolation** (sperm cannot fertilize egg). Postzygotic (occur after mating): **hybrid inviability** (hybrid embryo doesn't survive), **hybrid sterility** (hybrid survives but is sterile — mules from horse × donkey), **hybrid breakdown** (F1 fertile but F2 offspring are sterile or inviable).

#### The Process of Speciation
Speciation requires the evolution of reproductive isolation. Gene flow between populations homogenizes allele frequencies and prevents divergence. Isolation + time + divergent selection → speciation. Reproductive isolation can evolve through geographic separation (allopatric speciation) or within a single area (sympatric speciation). The degree of reproductive isolation is a continuum — partially isolated populations exist on the speciation continuum.

#### Review Questions
1. A horse and donkey can mate to produce a mule, but mules are sterile. What type of reproductive isolating mechanism does this represent?
2. Why can't the biological species concept be applied to bacteria?
3. Two bird populations on adjacent islands have slightly different songs. What type of reproductive isolation is developing, and what category (prezygotic or postzygotic) does it represent?
