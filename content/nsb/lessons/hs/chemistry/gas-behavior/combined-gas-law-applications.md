#### Weather Balloons
A weather balloon is launched with 1.0 m³ of helium at ground level (1.0 atm, 20°C). At 30 km altitude, P = 0.010 atm and T = −45°C.
- T₁ = 293 K, T₂ = 228 K
- V₂ = V₁ × (P₁/P₂) × (T₂/T₁) = 1.0 × (1.0/0.010) × (228/293) = **77.8 m³**

The balloon expands 78-fold! Balloons are designed to burst at altitude (when expanded too large) and the instrument package floats down by parachute.

#### Scuba Tank Calculations
A 12 L scuba tank at 20°C contains air at 200 atm. How many liters of air (at 1 atm, 37°C body temperature) can a diver breathe?
Using combined law: V₂ = V₁ × (P₁/P₂) × (T₂/T₁) = 12 × (200/1) × (310/293) = **2469 L**

At a breathing rate of ~15 L/min, the tank lasts ~165 min at the surface — much less at depth (higher pressure means more molecules per breath needed).

#### Industrial Gas Cylinders
Oxygen cylinders used in hospitals are compressed to ~2000 psi (136 atm) at room temperature. If the cylinder is accidentally heated in a fire (say to 500°C), the pressure increases by Gay-Lussac's Law: P₂ = 136 × (773/293) = **358 atm** — why cylinders can become rockets if the valve fails in a fire.
