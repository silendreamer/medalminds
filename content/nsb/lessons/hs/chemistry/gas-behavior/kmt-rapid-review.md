#### KMT Postulate Map
| Observable | KMT explanation |
|---|---|
| Gases are compressible | Large empty space between particles |
| Pressure increases with T | Faster particles hit walls harder |
| All gases mix | Random motion, no intermolecular forces |
| Heavier gases effuse slower | Same KE → lower speed for higher mass |
| Real gas deviates at high P | Particle volume becomes significant |

#### Conceptual Questions
1. Two sealed rigid containers hold the same number of moles of gas at the same temperature — one is H₂, one is CO₂. Which exerts higher pressure? Explain using KMT.
2. Which postulate of KMT allows us to write PV = nRT without a correction for volume of particles?
3. How does the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution change when a gas is heated? Describe in words.

#### Review Questions
1. [Bowl-style] For 10 points — according to kinetic molecular theory, average kinetic energy of gas particles is proportional to what thermodynamic variable?
2. [Bowl-style] For 10 points — what law states that lighter gas molecules effuse faster than heavier ones?
3. A 4 g sample of H₂ and a 4 g sample of He are at the same temperature. Do they have the same average molecular kinetic energy? Do they have the same average molecular speed?
