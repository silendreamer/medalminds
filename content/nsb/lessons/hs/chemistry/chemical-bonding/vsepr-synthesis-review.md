#### Shape Prediction Drill
For each molecule, give: (a) electron geometry, (b) molecular geometry, (c) polar or nonpolar.
- BeCl₂: 2 domains, linear, linear, nonpolar
- SO₃: 3 domains (if we count double bond as one domain), trigonal planar, trigonal planar, nonpolar
- PF₅: 5 domains, trig. bipyramidal, trig. bipyramidal, nonpolar
- ClF₃: 5 domains (3 bonds + 2 lone pairs), trig. bipyramidal, T-shaped, polar
- ICl₅: 6 domains (5 bonds + 1 lone pair), octahedral, square pyramidal, polar

#### Review Questions
1. Give the molecular geometry and polarity for: NF₃, BrF₄⁻, H₂Se.
2. Why do lone pairs occupy equatorial positions (rather than axial) in trigonal bipyramidal electron geometries?
3. A molecule with formula AX₂E₂ is always bent. Explain why, using lone pair repulsion.
