#### Toss-Up Stems
"For 10 points, this type of chemical bond forms by the electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions, typically between a metal and a nonmetal." → **ionic bond**

"For 10 points, two nonmetal atoms share electrons unequally when there is a difference in this property, measured on the Pauling scale." → **electronegativity**

"For 10 points, this property of ionic compounds — the energy released when gaseous ions form one mole of solid lattice — increases with ion charge and decreases with ion size." → **lattice energy**

#### Review Questions
1. Why is the lattice energy of Al₂O₃ much larger than that of NaCl?
