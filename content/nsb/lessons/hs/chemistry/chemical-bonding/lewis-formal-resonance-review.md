#### Formal Charge Drill
For each structure, calculate formal charge on the central atom:
- BF₃: B has 3 bonds, 0 lone pairs. FC = 3 − 0 − ½(6) = 0
- NH₄⁺: N has 4 bonds, 0 lone pairs. FC = 5 − 0 − ½(8) = +1
- CO₃²⁻ (one resonance form with one C=O and two C−O): FC on C = 4 − 0 − ½(8) = 0

#### Resonance Summary
Resonance occurs when: (1) a central atom is bonded to two or more identical atoms, (2) there are multiple bonds present, (3) lone pairs on adjacent atoms can delocalize into the bond. The resonance hybrid has fractional bond orders and equal bond lengths for equivalent positions.

#### Review Questions
1. Draw Lewis structures and calculate the formal charge on the central atom for: BH₄⁻, PF₅, ClF₃.
2. The bond order in O₃ is 1.5. Explain what this means in terms of resonance structures.
3. Why does resonance stabilize molecules or ions? Use the concept of electron delocalization.
