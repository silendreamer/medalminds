| As₂O₃ | Covalent or ionic | Diarsenic trioxide OR arsenic(III) oxide |

| XeF₄ | Covalent | Xenon tetrafluoride |

#### Review Questions
1. Name these compounds and state their type (ionic/covalent/acid): Cr₂O₃, H₃PO₄, CCl₄, (NH₄)₂S.
2. Write formulas for: cobalt(III) nitrate, dichlorine monoxide, hydroiodic acid, tin(II) fluoride.
3. Explain the difference in naming HCl gas versus HCl dissolved in water.
