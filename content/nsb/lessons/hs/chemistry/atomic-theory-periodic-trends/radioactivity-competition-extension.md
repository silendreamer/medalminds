#### Toss-Up Stems
"For 10 points, this type of radioactive decay increases the atomic number of a nucleus by one, as a neutron converts to a proton and a high-energy electron." → **beta decay**

"For 10 points, carbon-14 has this half-life in years, used to date organic material up to about 50,000 years old." → **5,730 years**

"For 10 points, this nuclear reaction powers the sun and involves combining light nuclei at extreme temperatures, releasing more energy per gram than fission." → **nuclear fusion**

#### Key Numbers
- ¹⁴C half-life: 5,730 years
- ²³⁸U half-life: 4.47 × 10⁹ years
- ²³⁵U half-life: 7.04 × 10⁸ years
