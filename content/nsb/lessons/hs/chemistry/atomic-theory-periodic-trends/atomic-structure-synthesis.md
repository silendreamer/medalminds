#### Particle Drill
For each species, find protons (p), neutrons (n), and electrons (e):
- ²³Na: p=11, n=12, e=11
- ²³Na⁺: p=11, n=12, e=10
- ³²S²⁻: p=16, n=16, e=18
- ⁵⁶Fe³⁺: p=26, n=30, e=23
- ²⁰⁸Pb: p=82, n=126, e=82

#### Isotope Mass Calculation
Boron has two isotopes: ¹⁰B (10.013 amu, 19.9%) and ¹¹B (11.009 amu, 80.1%). Atomic mass = (10.013)(0.199) + (11.009)(0.801) = 1.993 + 8.818 = 10.81 amu. This matches the periodic table value.
