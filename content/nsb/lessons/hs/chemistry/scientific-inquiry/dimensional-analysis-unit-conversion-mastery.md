#### What Dimensional Analysis Is
Dimensional analysis (factor-label method) is a systematic way to convert units using fractions where numerator and denominator are equal quantities in different units. The unit you want to eliminate goes in the denominator; the unit you want to introduce goes in the numerator. Units cancel algebraically, just like variables in algebra.

#### Single-Step Conversion Example
Convert 4.50 km to meters:
4.50 km × (1000 m / 1 km) = 4500 m = 4.50 × 10³ m

The fraction (1000 m / 1 km) is a conversion factor. Its value is exactly 1 because 1000 m = 1 km. Multiplying by 1 doesn't change the quantity, only the units.

#### Multi-Step Conversions
Convert 55 miles/hour to meters/second:
55 mi/hr × (1609 m / 1 mi) × (1 hr / 3600 s) = 55 × 1609 / 3600 m/s = **24.6 m/s**

Always cancel units as you go. If a unit doesn't cancel, you've set up a conversion factor upside-down.

#### Chemistry Application: Molar Conversions
Convert 5.0 g of NaCl to moles (molar mass of NaCl = 58.44 g/mol):
5.0 g NaCl × (1 mol NaCl / 58.44 g NaCl) = **0.086 mol NaCl**

Convert 0.25 mol H₂O to molecules:
0.25 mol × (6.022 × 10²³ molecules / 1 mol) = **1.5 × 10²³ molecules**

#### Science Bowl Calculation Pattern
Bowl problems rarely give you direct unit conversions — they embed them in chemical contexts. Practice recognizing: grams → moles (divide by molar mass), moles → atoms/molecules (multiply by Avogadro's), moles → liters at STP (multiply by 22.4 L/mol at STP for an ideal gas).

#### Review Questions
1. Convert 3.5 g/cm³ to kg/m³ using dimensional analysis. Show all conversion factors.
2. How many atoms are in 2.5 mol of carbon? Express in scientific notation.
3. A car travels 90.0 km/h. Convert to m/s, showing all unit cancellations.
