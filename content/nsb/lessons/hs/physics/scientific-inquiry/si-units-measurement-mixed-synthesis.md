#### Synthesis Problem 1: Equation Building
A pendulum swings. Someone tells you the period depends only on length L and gravity g. Use dimensional analysis to find the form of the equation. [L] = m, [g] = m/s². We need [T] = s. Try T ∝ Lᵃgᵇ: mᵃ(m/s²)ᵇ = s → m^(a+b) s^(−2b) = s⁰ s¹. So a+b=0 and −2b=1 → b=−½, a=½. Therefore T ∝ √(L/g). ✓

#### Synthesis Problem 2: Sig Fig Chain
Calculate the density of a block: mass = 125.0 g, volume = 87.3 cm³. Density = 125.0/87.3 = 1.432… g/cm³. Sig figs: 125.0 has 4, 87.3 has 3. Round to 3 sig figs: 1.43 g/cm³. Convert to kg/m³: 1.43 g/cm³ × 1000 kg/g × 10⁶ cm³/m³... wait, that's wrong. Correctly: ×(1 kg/1000 g) × (100 cm/m)³ = 1.43 × 10³ kg/m³.

#### Synthesis Problem 3: Error Propagation
If A = 3.2 ± 0.1 m and B = 1.5 ± 0.1 m, find A+B and A×B with uncertainties. A+B = 4.7 ± 0.2 m (add absolute uncertainties). A×B = 4.8 m²; relative uncertainty = 0.1/3.2 + 0.1/1.5 = 0.031 + 0.067 = 0.098 ≈ 10%; absolute uncertainty = 4.8 × 0.098 ≈ ±0.47 m² → A×B = 4.8 ± 0.5 m².

#### Review Questions
1. Use dimensional analysis to find the SI units of the spring constant k, given F = kx.
2. A current of 2.5 A flows for 45 s. Calculate charge (Q = It) with correct sig figs and units.
3. If your measurement of 10.0 m has 2% uncertainty, what is the absolute uncertainty?
