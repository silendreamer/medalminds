#### The Conversion Factor Method
Every conversion factor equals exactly 1 (e.g., 1 km/1000 m = 1). Multiplying any quantity by 1 doesn't change its value — only the units change. To convert 60 mph to m/s: 60 miles/hour × 1609 m/1 mile × 1 hour/3600 s = 26.8 m/s. Line up so the units you want to cancel are on opposite sides of the fraction.

#### Multi-Step Conversions
Convert 5.0 g/cm³ to kg/m³:
5.0 g/cm³ × (1 kg/1000 g) × (100 cm/1 m)³
= 5.0 × (1/1000) × (10⁶) kg/m³
= 5000 kg/m³.
Note: cubing the length unit means cubing the conversion factor (100 cm = 1 m, so 100³ cm³ = 1 m³).

#### Dimensional Analysis to Check Equations
If an equation's units don't balance, the equation is wrong. Check kinetic energy: KE = ½mv². Units: kg × (m/s)² = kg·m²/s² = J ✓. Check: F = mv/t²? Units: kg × m/s / s² = kg·m/s³. That's not newtons (kg·m/s²). ✗ The equation is wrong.

#### Estimating with Dimensional Analysis
You can estimate unknown quantities using dimensional analysis alone. How many seconds are in a year? 365 days × 24 hr × 60 min × 60 s ≈ 3.15 × 10⁷ s. This is a famous physics approximation worth memorizing (π × 10⁷ s).

#### Review Questions
1. Convert 90 km/h to m/s using dimensional analysis. Show all conversion factors.
2. A student writes the formula a = v²/d. Check if the units work for acceleration (m/s²).
3. How many microseconds are in one day?
