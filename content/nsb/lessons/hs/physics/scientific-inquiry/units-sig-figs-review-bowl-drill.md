#### Quick Reference: SI Prefixes
| Prefix | Symbol | Power |
|--------|--------|-------|
| nano | n | 10⁻⁹ |
| micro | μ | 10⁻⁶ |
| milli | m | 10⁻³ |
| centi | c | 10⁻² |
| kilo | k | 10³ |
| mega | M | 10⁶ |
| giga | G | 10⁹ |

Key derived units: 1 N = 1 kg·m/s²; 1 J = 1 N·m = 1 kg·m²/s²; 1 W = 1 J/s; 1 Pa = 1 N/m².

#### Sig Fig Quick Rules
Multiply/divide → fewest sig figs in inputs. Add/subtract → fewest decimal places. Leading zeros never count. Trailing zeros count only after a decimal point. Scientific notation removes ambiguity.

#### Rapid-Fire Practice
1. How many sig figs in 0.050? *(2)*
2. 3.0 × 2.10 = ? with correct sig figs. *(6.3)*
3. Convert 2.5 km to mm. *(2.5 × 10⁶ mm)*
4. SI unit of pressure = ? *(pascal, Pa = N/m²)*
5. SI unit of power = ? *(watt, W = J/s)*

#### Review Questions
1. A student writes 4.506 × 10² + 2.3 × 10¹. What is the result with correct sig figs?
2. Which has more sig figs: 1.30 × 10³ or 1300?
3. A force of 50 N acts over a distance of 3.0 m in 2.0 s. Calculate power in watts with correct sig figs.
