#### High-Yield Bowl Facts
1. Speed is scalar; velocity is vector.
2. Distance is scalar; displacement is vector.
3. Kinetic energy, work, power = scalars (even though they involve vectors in calculation).
4. Force, momentum, acceleration = vectors.
5. Dot product = scalar; cross product = vector.
6. W = **F**·**d** = Fd cos θ (work = dot product).
7. Perpendicular force → zero work.
8. Resultant of vectors at 90°: R = √(A² + B²).
9. Unit vectors î, ĵ, k̂ each have magnitude 1.
10. Vector components: Aₓ = A cos θ, Aᵧ = A sin θ.
11. To negate a vector, reverse its direction.
12. For equilibrium: sum of all force vectors = zero.
13. Velocity of object relative to ground = velocity relative to medium + velocity of medium relative to ground.
14. Work is zero when force ⊥ displacement (e.g., normal force, centripetal force).

#### Practice Toss-Ups
1. "For 10 points — this mathematical operation on two vectors yields a scalar equal to the product of their magnitudes times the cosine of the angle between them." *(Dot product / scalar product)*
2. "For 10 points — unlike speed, which is scalar, this vector quantity includes both magnitude and direction of motion." *(Velocity)*
3. "For 10 points — a 10 N force acts perpendicular to a 5 m displacement. How much work is done?" *(Zero joules)*

#### Review Questions
1. Is momentum a scalar or a vector?
2. Two vectors of equal magnitude are added. Under what condition is the resultant zero?
3. Calculate the work done by gravity on a 2 kg ball dropped 5 m. (g = 9.8 m/s²)
