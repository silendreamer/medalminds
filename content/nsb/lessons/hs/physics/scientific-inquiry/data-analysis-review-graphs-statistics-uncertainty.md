#### Key Concepts Summary
- Slope of x-t graph = velocity; slope of v-t graph = acceleration.
- Area under v-t graph = displacement; area under F-x graph = work.
- Linearization: plot y² vs. x for y∝√x; plot 1/y vs. x for y∝1/x.
- Mean = Σx/n; SD measures spread; SE = σ/√n measures precision of mean.
- Error bars = ±uncertainty; best-fit line minimizes residuals.
- Residual plot: random scatter → good model; systematic pattern → wrong model.
- Uncertainty propagation: add % uncertainties for multiplication/division.

#### Practice Problems
1. A v-t graph shows a straight line from (0, 10) to (5, 0) m/s. Find acceleration and displacement. *(a = −2 m/s²; displacement = area = ½ × 5 × 10 = 25 m)*
2. If mass uncertainty is 1% and velocity uncertainty is 3%, what is KE = ½mv² uncertainty? *(1 + 6 = 7%)*
3. A residual plot shows all residuals positive at small x and negative at large x. What does this indicate? *(Model overestimates at small x; the relationship may be nonlinear)*

#### Review Questions
1. What does the slope of an F vs. x graph represent? What does the area represent?
2. A mean is calculated as 15.3 with standard deviation 0.4 from 16 trials. What is the standard error?
3. State the rule for significant figures when adding measurements.
