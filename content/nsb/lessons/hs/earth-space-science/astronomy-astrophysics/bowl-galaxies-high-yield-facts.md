#### Key Facts
- Milky Way: barred spiral galaxy (SBbc); ~100,000 ly diameter; ~200–400 billion stars
- Sun distance from galactic center: ~26,000 ly; galactic orbital period ~225–250 million years
- Sgr A* = Milky Way's central SMBH = 4 million solar masses
- Andromeda (M31): nearest large galaxy, ~2.537 million light-years; on collision course (merge in ~4.5 billion years)
- Magellanic Clouds: LMC and SMC are irregular dwarf satellite galaxies; LMC ~160,000 ly; SN 1987A was in the LMC
- Hubble sequence: E0–E7 (ellipticals) → S0 (lenticular) → Sa–Sc and SBa–SBc (spirals and barred spirals)
- Vera Rubin: established galaxy rotation curve evidence for dark matter in the 1970s
- Bullet Cluster: best direct evidence for dark matter as physically distinct from gas
- Dark matter: ~27% of universe; ordinary matter ~5%; dark energy ~68%

#### Toss-Up Recognition Patterns
- "…rotation curve remains flat at large radii…more mass than visible…" → dark matter evidence
- "…two colliding clusters where gas slowed but lensing mass passed through…" → Bullet Cluster
- "…nearest spiral galaxy…approaching the Milky Way…" → Andromeda (M31)
- "…barred spiral…Sagittarius A*…" → Milky Way
- "…spherical collections of old, metal-poor stars orbiting the galactic halo…" → globular clusters
