#### Key Facts
- Quasars = quasi-stellar radio sources; actually AGN at high redshift; 3C 273 = first confirmed quasar (1963, z = 0.158)
- AGN powered by accretion onto supermassive black holes; can exceed 10¹³ L☉
- Seyfert galaxies: Seyfert 1 (broad + narrow lines) vs. Seyfert 2 (only narrow, obscured by torus)
- Blazar: AGN with jet pointed at Earth; most extreme variability
- M87: giant elliptical with prominent optical jet; central BH = 6.5 × 10⁹ M☉; first BH imaged (EHT 2019)
- M-sigma relation: BH mass correlates with host galaxy bulge velocity dispersion
- Quasar peak: z ≈ 2–3 (~10 billion years ago); universe was gas-rich

#### Review Questions
1. For 10 points — what type of AGN is characterized by a relativistic jet pointed nearly directly at Earth, producing extreme variability?
2. What was 3C 273, and why was its discovery significant in the 1960s?
3. Name the black hole whose mass was estimated at 6.5 billion solar masses by the Event Horizon Telescope.
