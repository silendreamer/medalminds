#### Key Facts
- White dwarf: ~0.6 M☉, ~Earth radius, supported by electron degeneracy pressure; max mass = 1.4 M☉ (Chandrasekhar limit)
- Neutron star: ~1.4–3 M☉, ~10 km radius, density ~10¹⁴ g/cm³; supported by neutron degeneracy pressure
- Pulsar discovered 1967 by Jocelyn Bell Burnell; Crab Pulsar period = 33 ms (remnant of 1054 CE supernova)
- Schwarzschild radius for 1 M☉ ≈ 3 km; r_s = 2GM/c²
- Sagittarius A* = Milky Way's central supermassive black hole; ~4 million solar masses
- M87* = 6.5 billion solar masses; first black hole imaged by EHT (2019)
- LIGO: first GW detection Sept 14, 2015 (GW150914) — two merging black holes
- GW170817 (Aug 2017): neutron star merger, first multi-messenger event; confirmed r-process nucleosynthesis of gold/platinum
- Type Ia supernova: white dwarf accreting in binary exceeds Chandrasekhar limit; used as standard candles (led to discovery of dark energy/accelerating expansion)

#### Toss-Up Recognition Patterns
- "…supported by electron degeneracy pressure…maximum mass of 1.4 solar masses…" → white dwarf / Chandrasekhar limit
- "…neutron degeneracy pressure…city-sized…nuclear density…" → neutron star
- "…periodic radio pulses…magnetic axis misaligned…" → pulsar
- "…first detected September 14, 2015…" → LIGO first gravitational wave detection
- "…Event Horizon Telescope imaged…shadow…" → M87* (2019) or Sgr A* (2022)
- "…gold and platinum formed in…" → neutron star mergers / r-process
