#### Key Facts
- Nitrogen cycle key steps: fixation (N₂ → NH₃) → nitrification (NH₃ → NO₃⁻) → assimilation → ammonification → denitrification (NO₃⁻ → N₂)
- Haber-Bosch process: industrial N₂ fixation using Fe catalyst, high T and P; responsible for feeding ~half of humanity
- Rhizobium: nitrogen-fixing bacteria in legume root nodules
- Phosphorus: no atmospheric reservoir; limiting nutrient in most freshwater systems
- Eutrophication: excess nutrients (N or P) → algal blooms → O₂ depletion → dead zones
- Carbon largest reservoir: lithosphere (~100 million GtC); smallest exchangeable reservoir: atmosphere (~860 GtC)
- Ice-albedo feedback: positive (amplifying); melting ice → lower albedo → more warming
- Silicate weathering: negative (stabilizing) feedback on long-term CO₂/climate

#### Toss-Up Patterns
- "…converts atmospheric N₂ into ammonia…Rhizobium…legume…" → nitrogen fixation
- "…no atmospheric reservoir…weathering of apatite…limiting in freshwater…" → phosphorus cycle
- "…algal bloom…oxygen depletion…dead zone…excess nutrients…" → eutrophication
- "…melting ice reduces albedo…amplifies warming…" → ice-albedo positive feedback
- "…weathering increases as temperature rises…removes CO₂…" → silicate weathering thermostat / negative feedback
