#### Review Questions (Extended Set)
1. A fission product has t½ = 30 years. How many years before its activity is reduced by a factor of 1,000 (approximately 10 half-lives)?
2. Explain why the "multiple barrier" approach is used for deep geological disposal and identify four specific barriers.
3. France generates about 10 tonnes of vitrified HLW per year from 58 reactors. Compare this to the CO₂ produced by an equivalent natural gas fleet. What does this comparison suggest about nuclear power's waste footprint?
4. The Yucca Mountain site was chosen partly because Nevada is geologically stable and arid. Explain why low groundwater and geological stability are important for a nuclear repository.
5. Advanced reactor designs (molten salt, fast reactors) can "burn" transuranic actinides as fuel, converting long-lived waste to short-lived fission products. If successful, how would this change the required isolation time for nuclear waste?
