#### Review Questions (Extended Set)
1. Distinguish between stochastic effects (cancer, hereditary effects — probability increases with dose, no threshold) and deterministic effects (radiation burns, ARS — severity increases with dose above threshold).
2. The linear no-threshold (LNT) model assumes any dose carries proportional risk. Critics argue radiation hormesis exists (low doses may be beneficial). What evidence exists for each position?
3. A whole-body dose of 4 Gy of gamma radiation (LD₅₀/₃₀ — lethal to 50% of exposed individuals within 30 days without treatment). Explain the mechanism of acute radiation syndrome at this dose level.
4. Radon-222 is estimated to cause ~21,000 lung cancer deaths per year in the US. Why is a gas that emits alpha particles dangerous to the lungs?
5. Bone marrow transplants can save lives after whole-body radiation doses of 3–10 Gy. Explain why marrow is particularly sensitive and why transplants help.
