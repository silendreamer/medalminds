#### High-Yield Facts
- D-T fusion: ²H + ³H → ⁴He + n + 17.6 MeV
- ITER location: Cadarache, France; 7 partners; Q = 10 target
- NIF ignition: December 2022; Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory; Q_fusion > 1
- JET record: 59 MJ in 5 seconds (February 2022)
- Plasma temperature for D-T fusion: ~100 million K (~10 keV)
- Lawson criterion: nτT > ~10²¹ m⁻³ s keV
- Tritium half-life: 12.3 years; bred from ⁶Li + n → ³H + ⁴He
- SPARC: Commonwealth Fusion Systems; HTS magnets at 20 tesla

#### Bowl Toss-Up Stems
- "For 10 points, this international fusion experiment under construction in Cadarache, France targets a Q factor of 10…" → **ITER**
- "For 10 points, this facility at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory achieved fusion ignition in December 2022 using 192 laser beams…" → **NIF (National Ignition Facility)**
- "For 10 points, name the fusion reaction that combines deuterium and tritium…" → **D-T fusion** (or ²H + ³H → ⁴He + n)

#### Review Questions
1. What does Q > 1 mean, and which facility first achieved it?
2. Why is tritium not naturally abundant, and how is it produced for fusion reactors?
3. Distinguish between magnetic confinement fusion and inertial confinement fusion.
