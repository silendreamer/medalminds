#### Review Questions (Extended Set)
1. Compare fission and fusion in terms of: (a) fuel availability, (b) waste products, (c) energy per kg of fuel, (d) current commercial viability.
2. The Lawson criterion requires high density, long confinement time, and high temperature. Explain the tradeoff between density and confinement time in tokamaks vs. ICF.
3. A D-T fusion reaction releases 17.6 MeV. How many reactions per second are needed to produce 1 GW of thermal power? (1 eV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J)
4. Why does a fusion reactor not face the criticality risks of a fission reactor?
5. Tritium has a half-life of 12.3 years. A fusion reactor requires 1 kg/day of tritium. Explain the tritium breeding blanket concept and why it's essential for commercial fusion.
