#### Core Facts Checklist
- Law of reflection: θ_incidence = θ_reflection (both from normal)
- Refraction: speed change → direction change; into denser medium → toward normal
- Index of refraction: n = c/v; higher n = slower light = more bending
- Total internal reflection: denser to less dense at angle > critical angle
- Convex lens: converging; real/inverted images for distant objects; magnifying for close
- Concave lens: diverging; always virtual, upright, reduced images

#### Quick Problems
1. Light hits a mirror at 25° to the normal. Angle of reflection? (25°)
2. Light goes from air (n=1.0) into diamond (n=2.42). Does it bend toward or away from normal? (Toward)
3. Fiber optic cables use what phenomenon? (Total internal reflection)
4. What type of lens corrects farsightedness? (Convex)

#### Review Questions
1. A periscope uses two mirrors at 45°. Trace the path of a light ray through it.
2. Why does a glass prism separate white light into colors?
3. Compare the images formed by a convex vs. concave lens for an object placed far away.
