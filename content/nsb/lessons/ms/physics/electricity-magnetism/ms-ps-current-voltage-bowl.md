#### Bowl-Ready Facts
- Ohm's Law: V = IR; SI units: V (volts), I (amperes/amps), R (ohms Ω)
- Power: P = IV = I²R = V²/R; unit: watt (W)
- Energy: E = Pt; bill in kilowatt-hours (kWh)
- Ammeter: series; voltmeter: parallel
- Conductors: metals (copper, silver, gold); insulators: rubber, glass, plastic
- Current: I = Q/t; unit: ampere (A) = 1 C/s

#### Practice Toss-Ups
- "For 10 points, what law states that voltage equals current times resistance?" — **Ohm's Law**
- "For 10 points, a circuit has 12 V across a 4 Ω resistor. What is the current?" — **3 amperes**
- "For 10 points, what is the unit of electrical resistance?" — **Ohm**
- "For 10 points, a 100 W bulb runs for 10 hours. How many kilowatt-hours of energy does it use?" — **1 kilowatt-hour**

#### Review Questions
1. What is the resistance of a device that draws 0.5 A from a 6 V battery?
2. How many watts is a 3 A current through a 15 Ω resistor?
3. Name the SI unit of electric current and of voltage.
