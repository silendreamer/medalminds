#### Quick Classification
Classify each as element (E) or compound (C):
- O₂ → **E** (oxygen element, diatomic)
- H₂O → **C**
- NaCl → **C**
- Fe → **E**
- CO₂ → **C**
- N₂ → **E**
- CH₄ → **C**
- Au → **E**

#### Symbol Recall Drill
Match element symbol to name:
- Fe = Iron | Au = Gold | Ag = Silver | Pb = Lead
- Hg = Mercury | K = Potassium | Na = Sodium | Cu = Copper
- W = Tungsten | Sn = Tin (stanum)

#### Diatomic Elements List
H₂, N₂, O₂, F₂, Cl₂, Br₂, I₂ — "Have No Fear Of Ice Cold Beer"

#### Review Questions
1. A substance is made entirely of carbon atoms. Is it an element or a compound?
2. Name the seven diatomic elements from memory.
3. Give the name and chemical formula for three common household chemical compounds.
