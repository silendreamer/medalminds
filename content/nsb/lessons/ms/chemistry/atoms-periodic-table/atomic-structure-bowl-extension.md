#### Toss-Up Patterns

**Pattern 1:**
*"For 10 points, what physicist conducted the gold foil experiment in 1911, leading to the discovery of the atomic nucleus?"*
Answer: **Ernest Rutherford**. Buzz on "gold foil" immediately.

**Pattern 2:**
*"What subatomic particle has a charge of negative one and is found outside the nucleus?"*
Answer: **Electron**. Buzz on "negative charge" + "outside nucleus."

**Pattern 3:**
*"For 10 points, what are atoms of the same element with different numbers of neutrons called?"*
Answer: **Isotopes**. Buzz on "same element" + "different neutrons."

**Pattern 4:**
*"Carbon-14 is used in what dating technique that helps archaeologists determine the age of organic materials?"*
Answer: **Radiocarbon dating** (accept carbon-14 dating). Buzz on "Carbon-14" + "age" + "organic."

#### Common Traps
- Don't confuse mass number and atomic number. Atomic number = protons only; mass number = protons + neutrons.
- Electrons have essentially zero mass — the mass of the atom comes from protons + neutrons.
- Thomson discovered the electron; Rutherford discovered the nucleus; Bohr proposed energy levels. Don't mix them up.

#### Review Questions
1. What is the atomic number of an element, and what subatomic particle does it count?
2. A neutral atom of an element has 15 protons and 16 neutrons. What is its mass number?
3. Which atomic model introduced the concept of electrons occupying specific energy levels?
