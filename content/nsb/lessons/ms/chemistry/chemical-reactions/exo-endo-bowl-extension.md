#### Toss-Up Patterns

**Pattern 1:**
*"For 10 points, what type of reaction releases heat energy to the surroundings, causing the surrounding temperature to increase?"*
Answer: **Exothermic reaction**. Buzz on "releases heat" + "temperature increases."

**Pattern 2:**
*"Photosynthesis requires input of energy from the Sun. Is it exothermic or endothermic?"*
Answer: **Endothermic** — it absorbs energy. Buzz on "requires energy input."

**Pattern 3:**
*"For 10 points, what term describes a substance that speeds up a chemical reaction by lowering its activation energy, without being consumed in the reaction?"*
Answer: **Catalyst**. Buzz on "speeds up" + "not consumed."

**Pattern 4:**
*"An instant cold pack becomes cold when you crack it. What type of reaction is occurring?"*
Answer: **Endothermic** (ammonium nitrate dissolving absorbs heat). Buzz on "becomes cold" + "crack the pack."

#### Fast-Recall: pH Numbers
- Stomach acid: pH 1–2
- Lemon juice: pH 2
- Vinegar: pH 3
- Pure water: pH 7
- Baking soda: pH 9
- Bleach: pH 13
- NaOH solution: pH 14

#### Review Questions
1. What does a catalyst do to the activation energy of a reaction?
2. A student mixes baking soda and vinegar in a bowl. What type of acid-base reaction occurs, and what gas is produced?
3. State one real-world product that uses an endothermic reaction and one that uses an exothermic reaction.
