#### Photosynthesis Powers Agriculture
All crops depend on photosynthesis. Wheat, rice, corn, soybeans, and potatoes are optimized for high photosynthetic productivity. Breeding programs select plants with higher photosynthetic rates. Controlled environment agriculture (greenhouses) manipulates light, CO₂, and temperature to maximize photosynthesis.

#### Biofuels from Photosynthesis
Ethanol from corn and biodiesel from soybeans capture photosynthetic energy. These biofuels are renewable but compete with food crops for land. Algae-based biofuels are under development — algae have very high photosynthetic efficiency and don't compete with food crops.

#### Photosynthesis and Atmospheric Oxygen
All atmospheric O₂ comes from photosynthesis. Without photosynthesis, Earth's atmosphere would be mostly nitrogen and CO₂, with no free oxygen. The oxygen-rich atmosphere supports aerobic life, including all animals.

#### Carbon Cycle and Climate
Photosynthesis removes CO₂ from the atmosphere and stores it in plant biomass and soil. Forests are major carbon sinks — a mature forest sequesters tons of carbon per acre per year. Deforestation reduces carbon uptake, accelerating climate change. Reforestation is a key climate solution.

#### Indoor Farming and Controlled Environments
Vertical farms use artificial lights tuned to red and blue wavelengths for maximum efficiency. Stacking plants in trays increases production per square foot. Hydroponics (growing in nutrient solution rather than soil) eliminates soil diseases and reduces water use. These methods will feed growing urban populations.
