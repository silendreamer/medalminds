#### 20 Must-Know DNA Facts

**Structure:**
1. DNA = deoxyribonucleic acid; found in nucleus (eukaryotes)
2. Shape = double helix; described by Watson & Crick, 1953
3. Building block = nucleotide (sugar + phosphate + base)
4. Four bases: A, T, G, C — A pairs with T; G pairs with C
5. Human genome: ~3 billion base pairs; 46 chromosomes

**Replication:**
6. Replication is semiconservative (1 old + 1 new strand)
7. Helicase unwinds; DNA polymerase builds new strands
8. Direction: 5' → 3'; lagging strand uses Okazaki fragments
9. DNA ligase joins Okazaki fragments
10. Occurs during S phase of cell cycle

**Central Dogma:**
11. Flow: DNA → mRNA → Protein
12. Transcription: nucleus; RNA polymerase makes mRNA
13. RNA uses U (uracil) instead of T (thymine)
14. Translation: ribosome; mRNA codons → amino acids
15. tRNA carries amino acids; anticodon matches mRNA codon
16. Three RNA types: mRNA, tRNA, rRNA

**Applications & Variation:**
17. Gene = segment of DNA coding for a protein or RNA
18. Allele = alternate version of a gene
19. SNP = single nucleotide polymorphism (most common variation)
20. DNA fingerprinting uses STRs at 13–20 specific loci
