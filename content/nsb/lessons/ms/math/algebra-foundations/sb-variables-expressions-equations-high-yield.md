#### Top 8 High-Yield Facts
1. "A number 5 less than x" → x − 5 (NOT 5 − x)
2. "Five less than twice a number" → 2x − 5 (subtract 5 from the product)
3. Combining like terms: 3x + 5x = 8x; 3x + 5y cannot be simplified
4. No-solution signals: after collecting terms, you get a false constant equation
5. Identity signals: after collecting terms, you get a true constant equation
6. Angles of triangle sum to 180°; of quadrilateral to 360° (useful for equation setups)
7. "Two consecutive even integers" → n and n+2 (both must be even, so step size 2)
8. Checking a solution by substitution is fast and catches most errors

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "For 10 points — what is the value of 3x − 4 when x = −2?" → **−10**
2. "For 10 points — solve for x: 5x + 8 = 2x − 7." → **x = −5**
3. "For 10 points — the sum of two consecutive integers is 31. What are they?" → **15 and 16**
4. "For 10 points — simplify: 4x − 3 + 2x + 7." → **6x + 4**
5. "For 10 points — solve: 3(2x − 1) = 4x + 7." → 6x − 3 = 4x + 7 → 2x = 10 → **x = 5**
6. "For 10 points — what is the solution set of 4x + 2 = 4x − 5?" → **no solution**

#### Review Questions
1. Write an algebraic expression for "the product of 6 and the sum of x and 3."
2. Solve for n: 2(n − 4) = 5(n + 1) − 21.
3. What must be true of an equation for it to have infinitely many solutions?
