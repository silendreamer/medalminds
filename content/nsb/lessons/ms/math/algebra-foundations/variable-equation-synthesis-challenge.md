#### Geometry Meets Algebra
"The angles of a triangle are x, 2x, and x + 30. Find each angle." Sum of angles = 180: x + 2x + (x + 30) = 180 → 4x + 30 = 180 → 4x = 150 → x = 37.5°. Angles: 37.5°, 75°, 67.5°. Check: 37.5 + 75 + 67.5 = 180 ✓.

#### Distance Problems
"A train leaves at 60 mph. A car leaves 1 hour later at 80 mph. When does the car catch the train?" Let t = time (hours) for the car. The train travels t+1 hours. Same distance: 60(t+1) = 80t → 60t + 60 = 80t → 60 = 20t → t = 3 hours. Car catches after 3 hours (train: 4 hours, both travel 240 miles). Check: 60 × 4 = 240; 80 × 3 = 240 ✓.

#### Perimeter and Area Equations
"A rectangle has perimeter 54 m. The length is 3 more than twice the width. Find both dimensions." Let w = width. l = 2w + 3. Perimeter: 2l + 2w = 54 → 2(2w+3) + 2w = 54 → 4w + 6 + 2w = 54 → 6w = 48 → w = 8 m, l = 19 m.

#### Mixture Equation
"How many liters of 20% acid solution must be added to 10 liters of 50% acid to get a 30% solution?" Let x = liters of 20% added. Acid equation: 0.20x + 0.50(10) = 0.30(x + 10) → 0.20x + 5 = 0.30x + 3 → 2 = 0.10x → x = 20 liters.

#### Building the Model
The hardest part of word problems is translation, not arithmetic. Habits that help: (1) define your variable clearly before writing any equation; (2) write the equation in words first, then in symbols; (3) circle the unknown and box the given amounts.

#### Review Questions
1. Three friends split a restaurant bill. Friend A pays $5 more than Friend B, and Friend C pays twice as much as Friend B. The total is $65. How much does each pay?
2. A rectangle's length is 4 times its width. Its perimeter is 80 cm. Find its area.
3. The sum of two numbers is 48 and their difference is 14. Find both numbers.
