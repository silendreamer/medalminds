#### Top 7 High-Yield Facts
1. Slope between (x₁,y₁) and (x₂,y₂): m = (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁)
2. Perpendicular slope: flip and negate. If m = 3, perpendicular slope = −1/3
3. Flip inequality when multiplying/dividing by negative
4. x-intercept: set y = 0; y-intercept: set x = 0
5. For parallel/perpendicular, only the slope matters, not the intercept
6. Standard form Ax + By = C can be rewritten as y = −(A/B)x + C/B for slope
7. Linear equations appear in bowl disguised as: "the line through...", "at what point do the lines intersect?", or "the slope of..."

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "For 10 points — what is the slope of the line passing through (2, 5) and (6, 13)?" → **2**
2. "For 10 points — what is the y-intercept of 4x − 2y = 10?" → set x=0: −2y=10, y=**−5**
3. "For 10 points — what is the slope of a line perpendicular to y = 3x + 1?" → **−1/3**
4. "For 10 points — solve for x: −5x + 10 > 25." → −5x > 15, x < **−3**
5. "For 10 points — at what point do y = 2x − 1 and y = x + 3 intersect?" → 2x−1=x+3, x=4, y=7 → **(4, 7)**

#### Review Questions
1. What is the slope of a horizontal line? A vertical line?
2. Two lines have slopes 3/4 and −4/3. Are they parallel, perpendicular, or neither?
3. Solve: 2 ≤ 3x − 1 ≤ 14. Express the answer as a compound inequality.
