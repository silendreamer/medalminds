#### What Slope Means
Slope = rise / run = (change in y) / (change in x) = Δy/Δx. A slope of 3 means "for every 1 unit right, go 3 units up." A slope of −1/2 means "for every 2 units right, go 1 unit down." Positive slope: line goes up left to right. Negative slope: line goes down left to right. Zero slope: horizontal line. Undefined slope: vertical line.

#### Computing Slope from Two Points
Given (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂): m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁). Order matters consistently — subtract in the same order for both. From (2, 3) to (5, 9): m = (9−3)/(5−2) = 6/3 = 2. From (4, 7) to (1, 4): m = (4−7)/(1−4) = (−3)/(−3) = 1.

#### Slope-Intercept Form: y = mx + b
m = slope; b = y-intercept (where the line crosses the y-axis, the point (0, b)). Given y = 3x − 5: slope = 3, y-intercept = −5, crosses y-axis at (0, −5). To graph: plot (0, −5), then from there go up 3 and right 1 for another point.

#### Writing the Equation from a Point and Slope
Given slope = 2 and point (3, 7): substitute into y = mx + b: 7 = 2(3) + b → 7 = 6 + b → b = 1. Equation: y = 2x + 1. Alternatively, use point-slope form: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁) → y − 7 = 2(x − 3) → y = 2x + 1.

#### Horizontal and Vertical Lines
Horizontal: y = k (constant) — slope = 0. Vertical: x = k (constant) — slope undefined. These don't fit slope-intercept form. Science Bowl sometimes asks for the equation of a horizontal or vertical line given a point.

#### Review Questions
1. Find the slope through (1, 4) and (5, 12).
2. What is the slope and y-intercept of y = −3x + 7?
3. Write the equation of the line with slope 4 passing through (−1, 2).
