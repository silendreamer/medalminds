#### 20 Must-Know Facts
1. 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%
2. 2/5 = 0.4 = 40%
3. 5/8 = 0.625 = 62.5%
4. 1/6 = 0.1666… ≈ 16.7%
5. To convert fraction to decimal: divide numerator ÷ denominator
6. To convert decimal to percent: multiply by 100
7. LCM of 4 and 6 = 12 (LCD for fractions)
8. GCF of 18 and 24 = 6 (for simplifying)
9. 2/3 × 3/4 = 1/2 (cross-cancel 3s)
10. 5/6 ÷ 5/12 = 5/6 × 12/5 = 2
11. Percent change = (new − old)/old × 100%
12. 10% of 250 = 25; 15% of 250 = 37.5
13. 37% of 50 = 50% of 37 = 18.5 (switch trick)
14. Mixed number 3 2/5 = improper fraction 17/5
15. 0.006 = 0.6% (NOT 6%)
16. 150% of 60 = 1.5 × 60 = 90
17. "What percent is 15 of 60?" → 15/60 = 1/4 = 25%
18. After 25% increase then 20% decrease: net = 1.25 × 0.80 = 1.0 exactly (they cancel)
19. 7/9 as a decimal = 0.7̄7̄ (repeating 7s)
20. Simplify 45/75: GCF = 15, so 45/75 = 3/5

2. Simplify: (3/5 ÷ 9/10) × 2/3.
