#### Top 10 High-Yield Facts
1. 2¹⁰ = 1024 ≈ 10³ (useful approximation in computing and Science Bowl)
2. Perfect squares to memorize through 20²: 1,4,9,16,25,36,49,64,81,100,121,144,169,196,225,256,289,324,361,400
3. (a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b² — NEVER a² + b²
4. a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ; negative exponent = reciprocal with positive exponent
5. √a × √b = √(ab); (√a)² = a
6. Scientific notation coefficient must satisfy 1 ≤ a < 10
7. When multiplying same base: add exponents; dividing: subtract
8. 0.1 in scientific notation = 1 × 10⁻¹
9. Avogadro's number: 6.022 × 10²³
10. Speed of light: 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s

#### Toss-Up Practice Stems
1. "For 10 points — what is 2 to the tenth power?" → **1,024**
2. "For 10 points — simplify the square root of 300." → **10√3**
3. "For 10 points — express 0.000052 in scientific notation." → **5.2 × 10⁻⁵**
4. "For 10 points — what is (3 × 10⁵) times (4 × 10⁻²) in scientific notation?" → **1.2 × 10⁴**
5. "For 10 points — what integer is the cube root of 343?" → **7**

#### Review Questions
1. Compute 3⁵ without a calculator.
2. Describe the error: "I know 3⁻² = −9."
3. Write the number one billion in scientific notation.
