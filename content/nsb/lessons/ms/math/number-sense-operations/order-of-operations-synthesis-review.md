#### 15 Must-Know Facts
1. PEMDAS order: Parentheses, Exponents, Mult/Div (L→R), Add/Sub (L→R)
2. M and D have equal priority — go left to right
3. A and S have equal priority — go left to right
4. −x² means −(x²), NOT (−x)²
5. (−x)² is always non-negative; −x² may be negative
6. Fraction bar = grouping symbol around numerator and denominator
7. 3(5) means 3 × 5 = 15; implied multiplication happens after parentheses
8. Nested brackets: innermost first
9. Exponent applies only to its immediate base
10. Subtracting a parenthetical: 10 − (3 + 4) = 10 − 3 − 4
11. a − b + c ≠ a − (b + c); evaluate left to right
12. Evaluate expressions before comparing, not after
13. Substitute variables before applying order of operations
14. "The square of the sum" = (a+b)²; "the sum of squares" = a² + b²
15. Calculators follow PEMDAS — check your parentheses when using one

#### Speed Drill — Under 15 Seconds Each
1. 2 + 3 × 4² = ?
2. (2 + 3) × 4² = ?
3. −4² = ?
4. (−4)² = ?
5. 36 ÷ 6 × 2 = ?
6. 36 ÷ (6 × 2) = ?

#### Review Questions
1. What is the difference between 5 − 3 + 2 and 5 − (3 + 2)? Show full work.
2. Evaluate 3 + 4² ÷ (2 × 2) − 1.
3. A student writes: "8 ÷ 2(2+2) = 8 ÷ 2 × 4 = 4 × 4 = 16." Another writes "= 8 ÷ 8 = 1." Who is right, and why?
