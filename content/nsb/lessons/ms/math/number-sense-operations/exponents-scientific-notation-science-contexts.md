#### SI Prefixes and Powers of 10
| Prefix | Symbol | Power |
|---|---|---|
| giga | G | 10⁹ |
| mega | M | 10⁶ |
| kilo | k | 10³ |
| milli | m | 10⁻³ |
| micro | μ | 10⁻⁶ |
| nano | n | 10⁻⁹ |
A gigabyte = 10⁹ bytes. A nanometer = 10⁻⁹ meters. Conversions: 5 km = 5 × 10³ m; 250 ms = 250 × 10⁻³ s = 2.5 × 10⁻¹ s.

#### Comparing Astronomical and Microscopic Scales
Earth-Sun distance: ~1.5 × 10¹¹ m. Diameter of a bacterium: ~1 × 10⁻⁶ m. How many times larger is Earth-Sun distance than a bacterium? (1.5 × 10¹¹) ÷ (1 × 10⁻⁶) = 1.5 × 10¹⁷. Divide coefficients (1.5 ÷ 1 = 1.5), subtract exponents (11 − (−6) = 17).

#### Compound Growth with Exponents
A bacterial colony doubles every hour. Starting with 100 bacteria, after 6 hours: 100 × 2⁶ = 100 × 64 = 6,400. After 10 hours: 100 × 2¹⁰ = 100 × 1024 = 102,400 ≈ 1.02 × 10⁵. Exponential growth is why exponents appear in biology, chemistry, and physics.

#### Order-of-Magnitude Estimation
"About how many times has your heart beaten if you are 12 years old?" Heart rate ≈ 70 beats/min × 60 min/hr × 24 hr/day × 365 days/yr × 12 yr ≈ 70 × 525,600 × 12 ≈ 4.4 × 10⁸ beats. Order-of-magnitude reasoning uses powers of 10 to get within a factor of 10 of the right answer — extremely useful for Science Bowl estimation questions.

#### Science Bowl Power-of-10 Facts to Know
- Speed of light: 3 × 10⁸ m/s
- Avogadro's number: 6.022 × 10²³ (per mole)
- 1 light-year ≈ 9.46 × 10¹⁵ m
- Diameter of a human hair ≈ 70 μm = 7 × 10⁻⁵ m
- Electron mass: 9.1 × 10⁻³¹ kg

2. If a radio wave has wavelength 3 × 10⁻² m, express this in millimeters.
3. Estimate the number of seconds in one year in scientific notation.
