#### Combined Problems
1. Simplify (2³ × 3²) / (6²): 8 × 9 / 36 = 72/36 = **2**. Alternatively: 2³ × 3² / (2 × 3)² = 2³ × 3² / (2² × 3²) = 2³/2² × 3²/3² = 2¹ × 1 = **2**.

2. Find x: 4ˣ = 32. Rewrite with same base: 2²ˣ = 2⁵ → 2x = 5 → x = 5/2. Science Bowl often uses this "same base" technique.

3. Simplify: √(2⁶ × 3⁴) = 2³ × 3² = 8 × 9 = **72**.

#### Scientific Notation Multi-Step
"The Sun is 1.5 × 10¹¹ m from Earth. Light travels at 3 × 10⁸ m/s. How long does sunlight take to reach Earth?" Time = distance/speed = (1.5 × 10¹¹) / (3 × 10⁸) = 0.5 × 10³ = 5 × 10² seconds = **500 seconds** ≈ 8.3 minutes.

#### Square Roots in Geometry
Area of a square = s². If area = 225 m², then s = √225 = 15 m, perimeter = 60 m. Volume of cube = s³. If volume = 512 cm³, s = ∛512 = 8 cm, surface area = 6s² = 6 × 64 = 384 cm².

#### The Most Common Error: Distribution
(a + b)² ≠ a² + b². Correct: (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b². This error causes wrong answers on almost every algebra test and many Science Bowl questions that involve squared sums.

#### Ordering Powers of Decimals
Compare 0.2², 0.2³, 0.2⁴: 0.04, 0.008, 0.0016. When the base is between 0 and 1, higher powers produce SMALLER results. This is the opposite of what happens with bases > 1 and catches many students off guard.

#### Review Questions
1. Simplify: (4³ × 4⁻¹) / 4².
2. Compute: (1.2 × 10³)² and express in scientific notation.
3. If a cube has surface area 96 cm², what is its volume?
