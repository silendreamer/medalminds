#### Top 15 High-Yield Facts
1. 1/7 = 0.142857142857… (repeating 6-digit block: 142857)
2. 0.̄3 = 1/3; 0.̄6 = 2/3; 0.̄9 = 1 (exact)
3. Any fraction n/9 repeats the digit n: 4/9 = 0.444…
4. Any fraction n/99 repeats the two-digit block nn: 23/99 = 0.232323…
5. LCM(a,b) × GCF(a,b) = a × b always
6. To find GCF quickly: Euclidean algorithm (divide, take remainder, repeat)
7. Percent increase then same percent decrease always gives a net loss: (1+r)(1−r) = 1−r²
8. n% of m = m% of n (the switch trick)
9. Dividing by a fraction < 1 always increases the value
10. Multiplying a fraction by its reciprocal always gives 1
11. 0.1̄ = 1/9; 0.2̄ = 2/9; 0.̄1 = 1/9 as well
12. Simplifying: divide both terms by GCF — never guess and check
13. Mixed number multiplication: always convert to improper fractions first
14. Percent of percent: 20% of 30% = 0.20 × 0.30 = 0.06 = 6%
15. "What percent MORE than" ≠ "what percent OF": if B = 1.4A, then B is 40% more than A, but 140% of A

#### Toss-Up Recognition Patterns
- "Express as a fraction in lowest terms…" → simplify by GCF, check for benchmark
- "What decimal is equivalent to…" → divide or recall benchmark table
- "By what percent did X increase/decrease?" → (new−old)/old × 100%
- "What is X% of Y?" → convert percent, multiply

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "For 10 points — what fraction in lowest terms equals 0.375?" → **3/8**
2. "For 10 points — a price increases from $60 to $75. By what percent did it increase?" → **25%**
3. "For 10 points — what is 2/3 of 3/4?" → **1/2**
4. "For 10 points — express 5/6 as a repeating decimal." → **0.8̄3̄**
5. "For 10 points — 18 is 45% of what number?" → **40**

#### Review Questions
1. Without paper: what is 3/7 as a decimal (first 4 digits)?
2. A value decreases 10% then increases 10%. Is it higher, lower, or the same as the start?
3. Write 0.̄4̄5 as a fraction in lowest terms.
