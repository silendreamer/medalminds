#### 20 Must-Know Facts
1. aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ
2. aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ
3. (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ
4. a⁰ = 1 (a ≠ 0)
5. a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ
6. (ab)ⁿ = aⁿbⁿ
7. √(ab) = √a × √b
8. √a² = |a| (absolute value, always non-negative)
9. ∛(−125) = −5 (cube roots of negatives are real)
10. Perfect squares to 15²: 1,4,9,16,25,36,49,64,81,100,121,144,169,196,225
11. 2¹⁰ = 1024
12. Scientific notation: 1 ≤ coefficient < 10
13. Large number → positive exponent; small number → negative exponent
14. Multiply in sci. notation: multiply coefficients, add exponents
15. Divide in sci. notation: divide coefficients, subtract exponents
16. Simplify √72 = 6√2
17. 3⁴ = 81; 4³ = 64; 5³ = 125
18. (2/3)² = 4/9
19. Negative exponent ≠ negative result: 5⁻² = 1/25 (positive)
20. 10⁶ = one million; 10⁹ = one billion
