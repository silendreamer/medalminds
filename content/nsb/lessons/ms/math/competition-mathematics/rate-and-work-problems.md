#### Distance = Rate × Time
Problem: "A train travels 300 miles in 5 hours. What's its speed?" Speed = 300/5 = 60 mph. Problem: "If a car travels 60 mph for 3 hours, how far?" Distance = 60 × 3 = 180 miles. Rearranging: time = distance/rate.

#### Work Problems
Problem: "Sarah can paint a house in 6 days. Tom can paint it in 9 days. Working together, how long?" Sarah's rate: 1/6 house per day. Tom's rate: 1/9 house per day. Combined: 1/6 + 1/9 = 3/18 + 2/18 = 5/18 house per day. Time to finish: 1 ÷ (5/18) = 18/5 = 3.6 days.

#### Relative Speed (Approaching/Receding)
Problem: "Two cars start 400 miles apart, driving toward each other at 60 mph and 40 mph. When do they meet?" Combined approach speed: 60 + 40 = 100 mph. Time to meet: 400/100 = 4 hours. They meet after 4 hours, having traveled 240 and 160 miles respectively (totaling 400).

#### Relative Speed (Parallel Motion)
Problem: "A car on a highway passes a car on a local road. The highway car travels 60 mph, the local car 30 mph. If the highway car is initially 10 miles behind, when does it catch up?" Relative speed: 60 - 30 = 30 mph. Time to catch up: 10/30 = 1/3 hour = 20 minutes.

#### Filling/Draining
Problem: "A pool fills at 20 gallons per minute but drains at 5 gallons per minute. How long to fill a 300-gallon pool?" Net fill rate: 20 - 5 = 15 gallons per minute. Time: 300/15 = 20 minutes. If the drain rate exceeded the fill rate, the pool wouldn't fill.

#### Review Questions
1. A cyclist travels 50 miles in 2.5 hours. What's the speed?
2. If Sarah paints in 6 days and Tom in 9 days, how long together?
3. Two cars approach at 50 mph and 70 mph, 240 miles apart. When do they meet?
