### Introduction to Interval Notation

Interval notation is a mathematical way to represent a range of numbers, particularly useful for expressing the solutions to inequalities. It allows us to succinctly describe sets of numbers that fall within certain bounds. Understanding interval notation is essential for solving problems in algebra and calculus, as it provides a clear visual representation of the solution sets.

### Open and Closed Intervals

In interval notation, we use parentheses and brackets to indicate whether endpoints are included in the interval. An open interval, denoted as \( (a, b) \), means that the endpoints \( a \) and \( b \) are not included in the interval. Conversely, a closed interval, represented as \( [a, b] \), includes both endpoints, meaning \( a \) and \( b \) are part of the set. This distinction is crucial when solving inequalities, as it affects the solution set.

### Half-Open Intervals

Half-open intervals, also known as half-closed intervals, combine elements of both open and closed intervals. For example, the interval \( [a, b) \) includes the left endpoint \( a \) but excludes the right endpoint \( b \). Similarly, the interval \( (a, b] \) includes \( b \) but excludes \( a \). Understanding half-open intervals is important for accurately representing solutions to inequalities that do not include one of the endpoints.

### Combining Intervals

Sometimes, the solution to an inequality may involve multiple intervals. For instance, if we have the inequality \( x < -2 \) or \( x \geq 3 \), we can express this in interval notation as \( (-\infty, -2) \cup [3, \infty) \). The symbol \( \cup \) represents the union of two intervals, indicating that any number in either interval is part of the solution set. Mastering how to combine intervals is essential for effectively communicating complex solutions.

### Graphing Intervals

Graphing intervals on a number line can help visualize the solutions represented in interval notation. Open intervals are depicted with hollow circles at the endpoints, while closed intervals are shown with solid circles. When graphing combined intervals, it is important to clearly indicate each section of the number line that corresponds to the solution set. This visual representation aids in understanding and reinforces the concept of interval notation.

### Applications of Interval Notation

Interval notation is widely used in various fields of mathematics, including calculus and statistics. It is particularly useful for defining domains and ranges of functions, as well as for solving inequalities in real-world applications. By mastering interval notation, students can enhance their problem-solving skills and prepare for more advanced mathematical concepts encountered in higher education.

### Review Questions

1. What does an open interval \( (a, b) \) signify about the endpoints \( a \) and \( b \)?
2. How would you express the solution to the inequality \( x \leq 4 \) in interval notation?
3. What is the interval notation for the combined solution of \( x < -1 \) and \( x \geq 2 \)?
