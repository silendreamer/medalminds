#### Conversion Factors

A **conversion factor** is a fraction equal to 1 that relates two units, such as (1 m / 100 cm) or (1,000 mL / 1 L). Because it equals 1, multiplying by it changes the units without changing the actual amount. You pick the version of the fraction that puts the unit you want to **keep** on top and the unit you want to **cancel** on the bottom.

#### Unit Cancellation (Dimensional Analysis)

**Dimensional analysis** means tracking units like algebra so they cancel. Convert 5.0 kilometers to meters:

5.0 km × (1,000 m / 1 km) = 5,000 m

The "km" on top cancels the "km" on the bottom, leaving meters. Always write the units — if they don't cancel to what you want, you used the factor upside down.

#### Decimal Shifting for Speed

Within the metric system you usually don't need to multiply at all — just move the decimal point by the number of powers of ten between the prefixes:
- **Bigger unit → smaller unit:** the number gets bigger (move the decimal right). 3.2 m → 320 cm.
- **Smaller unit → bigger unit:** the number gets smaller (move the decimal left). 4,500 mg → 4.5 g.

#### Squared and Cubed Units

When a unit is **squared or cubed**, the conversion factor is squared or cubed too. From centimeters to meters, 1 m = 100 cm, so:
- **Area:** 1 m² = (100 cm)² = **10,000 cm²**.
- **Volume:** 1 m³ = (100 cm)³ = **1,000,000 cm³**.

A frequent buzzer trap is converting cm² to m² by dividing by 100 instead of by 100² = 10,000. Remember to apply the power to the conversion factor.

#### Buzzer Strategy

For the 5-second clock: identify the two prefixes, find the exponent gap, and shift the decimal. Reserve full conversion-factor fractions for trickier squared/cubed or cross-unit problems where writing the units keeps you from flipping the factor.

#### Review Questions
1. Convert 250 centimeters to meters using a conversion factor.
2. How many square centimeters are in 1 square meter?
3. A student converts 2,000 milligrams to grams and gets 2,000,000 g. What mistake did they make, and what is the correct answer?
