#### Formula Recall Drill
Without looking: Rectangle area = ___. Triangle area = ___. Circle area = ___. Rectangular prism volume = ___. Cylinder volume = ___. Cube surface area = ___. Trapezoid area = ___. Check: l×w, ½bh, πr², lwh, πr²h, 6s², ½(b₁+b₂)h. If you hesitated on any, that's your study focus.

#### Mixed Problems
1. A circle has circumference 20π cm. Find its area. *Hint:* C = 2πr → r = 10 → A = π×10² = **100π cm²**.
2. A triangular prism has a triangular base with base 6 cm and height 4 cm, and the prism is 10 cm long. Find its volume. *V = ½×6×4 × 10 = 12×10 = **120 cm³***.
3. A square has the same area as a circle of radius 4. Find the side of the square. *Circle area = 16π. Side = √(16π) = 4√π ≈ 7.09 cm*.
4. A room (5×4×3 m) has two windows (1×1.5 m each) and one door (1×2 m) in the walls. Find the paintable wall area. *Wall area = 2(5×3)+2(4×3) = 30+24 = 54 m². Subtract openings: 2×1.5 = 3 m², 1×2 = 2 m². Paintable = 54−3−2 = **49 m²***.

#### Identify the Error
A student finds the volume of a cylinder (r=3, h=5) as V = π×3×5 = 15π. What's the error? The student forgot to **square the radius**. Correct: V = π×3²×5 = 45π ≈ 141.4 cm³. Always apply the full formula: V = πr²h, not πrh.

#### Timed Challenge
Set a timer for 2 minutes. Solve: A cube has surface area 150 cm². (a) Find the side length. (b) Find the volume. *SA = 6s² = 150 → s² = 25 → s = 5 cm. V = 5³ = **125 cm³**.*

#### Review Questions
1. A trapezoid has parallel sides 8 and 14 and height 6. A semicircle of diameter 8 is removed from the bottom base. Find the remaining area. (Use π ≈ 3.14)
2. A rectangular swimming pool is 15 m × 8 m. The shallow end is 1 m deep and the deep end is 3 m deep, with a uniform slope. Find the volume of water it holds. (Hint: it's a trapezoidal prism.)
3. What happens to the volume of a cube if you double all its side lengths?
