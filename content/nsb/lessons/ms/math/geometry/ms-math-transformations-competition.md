#### Bowl-Style Toss-Up Practice
*"For 10 points — point (3, 5) is rotated 90° counterclockwise about the origin. What are the coordinates of the image?"* Apply (x,y)→(−y,x): **(−5, 3)**. *"For 10 points — a figure with 6-fold rotational symmetry is rotated 60°. How many times does it look identical in a full 360° rotation?"* Answer: **6 times**. *"For 10 points — a point is reflected over the y-axis and then the x-axis. This composition is equivalent to what single transformation?"* Answer: **180° rotation about the origin**.

#### Symmetry Speed Facts
Equilateral triangle: 3 lines of symmetry, rotational order 3. Square: 4 lines, order 4. Rectangle: 2 lines, order 2. Regular hexagon: 6 lines, order 6. Parallelogram: 0 lines, order 2. Circle: infinite lines, infinite order. Isosceles triangle: 1 line, order 1. Scalene triangle: 0 lines, order 1.

#### Common Trap: Clockwise vs. Counterclockwise
When a problem says "rotated 90°" without specifying direction, assume counterclockwise (the math convention) unless told otherwise. Clockwise 90° is the same as counterclockwise 270°. Rule for 90° CW: (x,y)→(y,−x). Rule for 90° CCW: (x,y)→(−y,x). Always note the direction before applying the rule.

#### Review Questions

2. For 10 points: how many lines of symmetry does a regular octagon have?
3. For 10 points: a point at (3, 4) is dilated by a scale factor of 3 centered at the origin. What is the distance of the image from the origin?
