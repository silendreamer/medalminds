#### Must-Know Soil Facts
1. Soil = weathered rock particles + organic matter + water + air + living organisms
2. The A horizon (topsoil) is the most fertile and darkest layer
3. Humus = decomposed organic matter; gives topsoil its dark color and fertility
4. Soil formation factors: Climate, Living organisms, Organic matter, Relief, Parent material, Time (CLORPT)
5. Climate is the most important factor in soil formation
6. Sandy soil drains fast, clay holds water; loam is best for agriculture
7. The Dust Bowl (1930s): drought + deep plowing destroyed Great Plains topsoil
8. Soil stores more carbon than atmosphere + all land plants combined
9. 1 cm of topsoil takes 100–1,000 years to form
10. No-till farming prevents erosion by leaving plant roots in ground

#### Bowl Clue Patterns
- "...organic layer of decomposing material at soil surface..." → **O horizon**
- "...most fertile, darkest layer of soil..." → **A horizon / topsoil**
- "...material from which soil forms..." → **Parent material**
- "...decomposed organic matter that gives soil fertility..." → **Humus**
- "...1930s Great Plains ecological disaster caused by drought and overplowing..." → **Dust Bowl**

#### Review Questions
1. A soil sample is dark-colored, rich in organic matter, and supports the most plant roots. Which horizon is it from?
2. Why does it matter that soil stores more carbon than the atmosphere and land plants combined?
3. What is the difference between weathering and erosion in terms of soil formation?
