#### Top Mineral Toss-Up Clue→Answer Pairings
| Clue | Answer |
|---|---|
| "...hardest natural substance, Mohs 10..." | Diamond |
| "...softest mineral, Mohs 1, used in baby powder..." | Talc |
| "...both pure carbon, one hard one soft..." | Diamond and graphite (polymorphs) |
| "...NaCl, cubic crystals, table salt..." | Halite |
| "...SiO₂, 7 on Mohs scale, most abundant single mineral in crust..." | Quartz |
| "...FeS₂, called fool's gold..." | Pyrite |
| "...red variety of corundum..." | Ruby |
| "...blue variety of corundum..." | Sapphire |
| "...most abundant mineral GROUP in crust..." | Feldspars |

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "For 10 points, what mineral has the chemical formula SiO₂ and a hardness of 7 on the Mohs scale?" → **Quartz**
2. "For 10 points, diamond and graphite are both composed entirely of what element?" → **Carbon**
3. "For 10 points, what property of a mineral — the color of its powder when scratched on an unglazed tile — is more reliable for identification than its surface color?" → **Streak**
4. "For 10 points, what mineral, iron sulfide with formula FeS₂, is nicknamed fool's gold?" → **Pyrite**

#### Review Questions
1. What two minerals are polymorphs of carbon, and how do they differ in properties?
2. If a mineral scratches feldspar but not quartz, what is its approximate Mohs hardness?
3. A bowl question says "this is the most abundant mineral GROUP in Earth's crust." What is the answer?
