#### The 5 Mineral Criteria — Quick Recall
Naturally occurring · Inorganic · Solid · Crystalline structure · Definite chemical composition. Use the mnemonic **NISC-D**: **N**aturally occurring, **I**norganic, **S**olid, **C**rystalline, **D**efinite composition.

#### Top Mineral Facts for Bowl
- Most abundant mineral group in Earth's crust: **feldspars**
- Hardest natural substance: **diamond** (10)
- Softest mineral: **talc** (1)
- Diamond and graphite: both pure **carbon** (polymorphs)
- Pyrite = "**fool's gold**" (FeS₂) — metallic luster, cubic crystals, but brittle and lighter than gold
- Quartz = **SiO₂**; halite = **NaCl**; calcite = **CaCO₃**
- **Streak** is more reliable than **color** for identification
- **Cleavage** = breaks along flat planes; **fracture** = breaks unevenly
