#### Common Bowl Traps
- **Trap:** "Wegener proposed plate tectonics." → He proposed **continental drift** (the observation). **Plate tectonics** (the complete mechanism theory) was developed by many scientists in the 1960s.
- **Trap:** Saying transform boundaries have volcanoes — they don't. Only divergent and convergent (subduction) boundaries do.
- **Trap:** Confusing mid-ocean ridges with ocean trenches. Ridges = new crust created. Trenches = old crust destroyed.

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "For 10 points, what type of plate boundary is the San Andreas Fault, where the Pacific and North American Plates slide past each other?" → **Transform boundary**
2. "For 10 points, what 1912 hypothesis, proposed by Alfred Wegener, states that all continents were once joined as a single landmass?" → **Continental drift**
3. "For 10 points, what chain of volcanoes around the Pacific Ocean results from subducting oceanic plates?" → **Ring of Fire**
4. "For 10 points, where does seafloor spreading occur, producing new oceanic crust as magma wells up?" → **Mid-ocean ridges**
