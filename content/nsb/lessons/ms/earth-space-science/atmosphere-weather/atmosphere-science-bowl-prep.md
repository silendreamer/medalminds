#### Top Facts to Know Cold
- Layer order from surface: **troposphere → stratosphere → mesosphere → thermosphere → exosphere**
- Layer where weather occurs: **troposphere**
- Layer where ozone is found: **stratosphere**
- Coldest layer: **mesosphere**
- Hottest layer (by temperature): **thermosphere**
- Composition: ~78% N₂, ~21% O₂
- Ozone molecule formula: **O₃**
- Protocol protecting ozone: **Montreal Protocol (1987)**

#### Toss-Up Stems to Recognize
- "This layer of the atmosphere contains the ozone layer..." → **stratosphere**
- "Name the coldest layer of Earth's atmosphere..." → **mesosphere**
- "This international agreement phased out CFCs to protect the ozone layer..." → **Montreal Protocol**
- "For 10 points — weather occurs in which layer of Earth's atmosphere?" → **troposphere**
- "This phenomenon, characterized by warming of eastern Pacific Ocean water, disrupts global weather patterns..." → **El Niño**

#### Common Traps
- Don't confuse thermosphere (hottest by temperature) with mesosphere (coldest). The thermosphere is hot because molecules absorb energy, but there are so few molecules that it would feel cold to you — a nuance that rarely appears in bowl questions, but the temperature classification (hottest = thermosphere) is always tested as stated.
- Ozone is O₃, not O₂ (oxygen). The question will often test whether you know the formula.

#### Review Questions
1. In order from the surface, name Earth's five atmospheric layers.
2. What does the Montreal Protocol protect?
3. Which atmospheric layer has the lowest temperatures?
