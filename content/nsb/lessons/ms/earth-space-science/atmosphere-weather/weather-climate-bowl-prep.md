#### Key Facts
- Cold front symbol: blue triangles; warm front symbol: red semicircles
- Low pressure = counterclockwise (NH) = stormy; high pressure = clockwise (NH) = clear
- El Niño = warm eastern Pacific; La Niña = cool eastern Pacific
- Hurricane categories: Saffir-Simpson Scale, 1–5
- Tornado ratings: Enhanced Fujita Scale (EF0–EF5)
- Most abundant GHG: water vapor; most discussed anthropogenic GHG: CO₂
- Pre-industrial CO₂: ~280 ppm; current CO₂: ~425 ppm
- Natural greenhouse effect keeps Earth ~33°C warmer than it would otherwise be

#### Toss-Up Stems
- "This scale, ranging from Category 1 to 5, rates hurricanes by wind speed..." → **Saffir-Simpson Scale**
- "Name the global ocean circulation system driven by differences in temperature and salinity..." → **thermohaline circulation** (or global conveyor belt)
- "This periodic warming of eastern Pacific surface waters disrupts global weather patterns..." → **El Niño**
- "For 10 points — in which direction do winds rotate around a low-pressure system in the Northern Hemisphere?" → **counterclockwise**
- "What instrument measures atmospheric pressure?" → **barometer**

#### Review Questions
1. What scale rates tornado intensity?
2. Name the large-scale ocean circulation system that distributes heat globally.
3. In which direction do surface winds rotate around a high-pressure system in the Northern Hemisphere?
