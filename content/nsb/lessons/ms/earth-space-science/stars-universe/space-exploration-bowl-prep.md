#### Key Dates and Firsts
- First satellite: **Sputnik 1** (USSR, October 4, 1957)
- First human in space: **Yuri Gagarin** (USSR, April 12, 1961)
- First Moon walk: **Neil Armstrong** (Apollo 11, July 20, 1969)
- Voyager 1 enters interstellar space: **2012**
- Hubble Space Telescope launched: **1990**
- JWST launched: **December 25, 2021**; first images: July 2022
- ISS continuously inhabited since: **November 2000**
- Current Mars rover: **Perseverance** (landed February 18, 2021)

#### Toss-Up Stems
- "This NASA spacecraft, launched in 1977, became the most distant human-made object when it entered interstellar space in 2012..." → **Voyager 1**
- "Name the first human to walk on the Moon..." → **Neil Armstrong**
- "This space telescope, launched in 2021, observes in the infrared and has captured images of some of the earliest galaxies..." → **James Webb Space Telescope (JWST)**
- "For 10 points — what was the name of the first artificial Earth satellite, launched by the Soviet Union in 1957?" → **Sputnik 1**
- "This NASA rover, which landed on Mars in 2021, is searching for signs of ancient microbial life..." → **Perseverance**

#### Universe Cosmology Quick Facts
- Age of universe: **~13.8 billion years**
- Ordinary matter: **~5%** of universe
- Dark matter: **~27%**
- Dark energy: **~68%**
- CMB temperature: **2.725 K**
- CMB discovered by: **Penzias and Wilson (1965)**
- Hubble's Law: galaxy recession velocity proportional to distance

#### Review Questions
1. In what year did Voyager 1 enter interstellar space?
2. What fraction of the universe is made of dark energy?
3. Name the current active Mars rover as of 2024.
