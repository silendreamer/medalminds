#### Small Modular Reactors (SMRs)
SMRs produce 50-500 MW (vs. 1,000 MW for large reactors), making them modular and deployable in diverse locations. Advantages: factory construction (better quality control), scalability, remote site applications (powering Arctic communities, mining operations), industrial heat applications (desalination, steelmaking). Disadvantages: higher capital cost per MW (economics of scale favor large reactors), limited operating experience (designs are new). Several companies (NuScale, TerraPower) are developing SMRs; first deployments expected mid-2020s.

#### Advanced Reactor Designs
Designs beyond traditional light-water reactors include: fast breeder reactors (breed plutonium from uranium-238, extending fuel), molten salt reactors (use molten fluoride or chloride salt as coolant/moderator, offering higher temperatures and passive safety), and advanced thermal reactors (modifications of light-water designs for better efficiency). Some are safer (passive cooling, can't melt down), more efficient (higher burnup), or produce less waste. None are deployed at scale yet.

#### Fusion Energy: Hope and Reality
Fusion combines light nuclei (deuterium, tritium) into heavier nuclei, releasing huge energy. Fuel is abundant (deuterium in ocean water). Waste is minimal (tritium is captured, helium is inert). Fusion is the power source of stars and the "holy grail" of clean energy. But achieving fusion on Earth is extremely difficult — requires temperatures >100 million Kelvin and confining plasma long enough for net energy gain.

#### ITER and the Path to Fusion
The International Thermonuclear Experimental Reactor (ITER) is being built in France as a collaborative project to demonstrate net energy gain in fusion. ITER's tokamak (doughnut-shaped plasma chamber) will contain 50 million Kelvin plasma. Completion is expected mid-2020s. If successful, ITER proves fusion's feasibility. Commercial fusion power plants would follow decades later (2050+). Fusion is often said to be "30 years away" because progress is real but slow.

#### Recent Breakthroughs and Private Investment
In 2022, the U.S. National Ignition Facility achieved fusion energy gain (output > input) in a laser-fusion approach. This was historic — proof of concept that fusion energy gain is physically possible. Private companies (Commonwealth Fusion, TAE Technologies, Helion) are receiving billions in funding for fusion R&D. Commercial fusion demonstrations are targeted for 2030-2035. However, moving from demonstration to economically viable power plants is a massive next step.

#### Why Fusion Matters for Climate
Fusion could provide baseload power (always on, unlike solar/wind) with zero emissions. A single ton of deuterium-tritium fuel releases energy equivalent to 1,000 tons of coal. Fuel scarcity is not an issue (oceans of deuterium, tritium breeding from lithium). If fusion succeeds, it could power civilization indefinitely without climate or waste concerns. This is why fusion funding is increasing despite decades of delays.
