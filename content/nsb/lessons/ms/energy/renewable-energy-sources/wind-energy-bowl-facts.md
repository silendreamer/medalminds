#### Can't-Miss Facts
- Wind power ∝ wind speed³ (cube of wind speed)
- U.S. wind leader: Texas (>40 GW installed)
- Iowa: >60% of electricity from wind
- Onshore wind capacity factor: ~35%; offshore: ~40–50%
- First U.S. commercial offshore wind farm: Block Island, Rhode Island (2016, 30 MW)
- Wind turbine components: rotor blades, nacelle (gearbox + generator), tower, yaw system
- DOE labs for wind: NREL (Golden, CO), Sandia National Laboratories (Albuquerque, NM)
- Offshore wind turbines: 10–20 MW; largest rotor diameters ~220 m
- Blade aerodynamics: lift (like an airplane wing), not drag (like a sail)
- Wind energy: no fuel cost, no combustion, no CO₂ during operation

#### Bowl Toss-Up Stems
*"For 10 points, wind power is proportional to what power of wind speed?"* → The cube (third power) of wind speed
*"For 10 points, what state leads the United States in installed wind power capacity?"* → Texas
*"For 10 points, the nacelle of a wind turbine contains what two major mechanical components?"* → Gearbox and generator (accept either; both is best)

#### Review Questions
1. If wind speed doubles, by what factor does available wind power increase?
2. Name two DOE national laboratories that conduct wind energy research.
3. What was the first commercial offshore wind farm in the United States, and where was it located?
