#### Bowl Toss-Up Patterns
Science Bowl toss-ups on this topic often open with a description and ask you to name the type of decay or particle: *"For 10 points, what type of radioactive decay produces a helium-4 nucleus?"* (Answer: alpha decay.) *"For 10 points, what unit measures radioactive decay rate as one disintegration per second?"* (Answer: becquerel.) *"For 10 points, what element has the atomic number 92 and is the primary fuel in most nuclear reactors?"* (Answer: uranium.)

#### Can't-Miss Facts
- Alpha particle = helium-4 nucleus (2p + 2n)
- Beta particle = electron (from neutron → proton conversion)
- Gamma rays = electromagnetic radiation, highest energy, most penetrating
- Carbon-14 half-life ≈ 5,730 years (used in radiocarbon dating)
- Uranium-235 half-life ≈ 704 million years; U-238 ≈ 4.5 billion years
- 1 becquerel = 1 decay/second; 1 curie = 3.7 × 10¹⁰ becquerels
- DOE's Oak Ridge National Laboratory pioneered nuclear science and still leads nuclear research

#### Review Questions
1. Name the three types of radiation and rank them from least to most penetrating.
2. What does the atomic number of an element tell you?
3. What is the half-life of carbon-14, and what is it used to date?
