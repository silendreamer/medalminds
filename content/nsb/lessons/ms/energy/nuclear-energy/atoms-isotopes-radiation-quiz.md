#### Question Set
1. Carbon-12 and carbon-14 are both carbon atoms. What makes them different?
2. A radioactive sample starts with 80 grams. After two half-lives, how many grams remain?
3. Which type of radiation requires the thickest shielding?
4. What is the unit for measuring radioactive decay rate (one disintegration per second)?
5. Name two practical uses of radioactive isotopes outside of power generation.
6. An element undergoes alpha decay. By how much does its atomic number change?
7. Why is uranium-235 used as reactor fuel instead of uranium-238?
8. Approximately how much more energy does uranium produce per kilogram compared to coal?

#### Answers
1. Number of neutrons (C-12 has 6; C-14 has 8)
2. 20 grams (halved twice: 80 → 40 → 20)
3. Gamma radiation (requires lead or thick concrete)
4. Becquerel
5. Any two of: medical imaging (technetium-99m), cancer therapy, smoke detectors (americium-241), food irradiation (cobalt-60), radiocarbon dating
6. Decreases by 2
7. U-235 undergoes fission more readily (it's fissile); U-238 is not easily fissioned by slow neutrons
8. About 1 million times more energy per kilogram
