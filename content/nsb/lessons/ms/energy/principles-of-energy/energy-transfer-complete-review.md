#### 15 Must-Know Transfer Facts
1. Conduction: contact, works best in solids and metals
2. Convection: fluid movement, creates currents
3. Radiation: electromagnetic waves, no medium needed
4. Vacuum blocks conduction and convection; only radiation crosses
5. Shiny/light surfaces reflect radiation; dark/matte surfaces absorb and emit it
6. Thermal equilibrium: temperatures equalize when heat flow stops
7. Heat always flows hot → cold spontaneously
8. All objects above 0 K emit radiation
9. Efficiency = useful output / total input × 100%
10. Waste energy → almost always becomes thermal energy
11. Energy quality degrades with each transformation
12. Coal → electricity: ~35% efficient (roughly)
13. LED bulb: ~90% efficient; incandescent: ~5% efficient
14. Sun's energy reaches Earth by radiation only
15. Convection drives weather, ocean currents, and plate tectonics

#### Mode Identification Drill
Identify the primary transfer mode:
- Coffee mug warming your hands (conduction)
- Hot air balloon rising (convection)
- Feeling warmth from a fireplace across the room (radiation)
- Ocean current moving heat from equator to poles (convection)
- Metal spoon heating up in soup (conduction)
- UV light tanning your skin (radiation)

#### Efficiency Quick Problems
1. Motor: input 800 J, useful output 560 J. Efficiency? → 70%
2. Solar panel: 1000 W sunlight, 160 W electrical output. Efficiency? → 16%
3. Engine: 25% efficient, uses 400 J of fuel energy. Useful work? → 100 J

#### Review Questions
1. A metal rod has one end in a flame. Which transfer mode carries heat to the other end?
2. Why does convection NOT occur in solids?
3. A device is 80% efficient and produces 400 J of useful output. How much energy did it consume?
