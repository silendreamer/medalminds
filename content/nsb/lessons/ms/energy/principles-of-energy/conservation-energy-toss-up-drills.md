#### Key Recognition Signals
"Dropped from rest" + "find speed at bottom" → set PE = KE, solve for v.
"Launched upward" + "find max height" → set KE = PE, solve for h.
"Roller coaster" + "find speed at bottom" → PE_top = KE_bottom.
"With friction" + "find energy lost" → subtract final mechanical energy from initial.
"Pendulum height" → same height on both sides (frictionless).

#### 6 Practice Toss-Up Stems

1. "A 1 kg ball is dropped from 20 m. For 10 points — what is its speed just before hitting the ground? Use g = 10 m/s²." → **20 m/s** (KE = 200 J = ½(1)v² → v = 20)

2. "For 10 points — name the law stating that the total energy of an isolated system remains constant." → **Law of Conservation of Energy**

3. "A 500 J spring launches a 2 kg block horizontally. For 10 points — what is the block's speed?" → **≈22.4 m/s** (500 = ½(2)v² → v² = 500 → v ≈ 22.4)

4. "For 10 points — in a frictionless pendulum, at what point in the swing is potential energy at its maximum value?" → **At the highest point of the swing**

5. "For 10 points — a roller coaster starts from rest at 40 m height. What is its speed at the bottom? Use g = 10 m/s²." → **≈28.3 m/s** (PE = 400mJ = KE → v = √800 ≈ 28.3)

6. "For 10 points — if a 1000 J system loses 300 J to friction, how much mechanical energy remains?" → **700 J**

#### Speed-Solving Tips
For "find speed" problems: skip solving for KE first. Go directly: mgh = ½mv² → gh = ½v² → v = √(2gh). With g = 10: v = √(20h). Memorize: h = 5 m → v ≈ 10 m/s; h = 20 m → v ≈ 20 m/s; h = 45 m → v = 30 m/s.

#### Review Questions
1. A 2 kg ball falls from 80 m. What is its speed at the bottom? (g = 10)
2. What clue phrase in a toss-up signals you should set PE equal to KE?
3. A pendulum is released from 1.8 m height. What is its speed at the bottom? (g = 10)
