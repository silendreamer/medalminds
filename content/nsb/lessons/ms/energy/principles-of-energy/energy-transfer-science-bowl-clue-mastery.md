#### Clue Words by Transfer Mode

**Conduction clues:** "direct contact," "solid," "metal rod," "thermal conductivity," "copper," "insulator," "touching"
**Convection clues:** "fluid," "current," "rising," "sinking," "circulation," "ocean current," "weather," "boiling," "mantle"
**Radiation clues:** "vacuum," "electromagnetic," "infrared," "no medium," "across space," "sunlight," "greenhouse"

#### When to Interrupt a Toss-Up
In Science Bowl, read-alouds give clues in decreasing order of difficulty (hardest first, easiest last). For heat transfer, the hard clue might be "the process by which heat moves through the convection zone of the Sun" — interrupt early with "convection." The easy clue (given if no one buzzed) might be "name the three modes of heat transfer" — that's your guaranteed buzz opportunity.

#### 5 Practice Toss-Up Stems

1. "This mode of heat transfer occurs in fluids as warmer, less-dense material rises and cooler material sinks, and it is responsible for the movement of Earth's tectonic plates." → **Convection**

2. "For 10 points — this mode of heat transfer requires no medium and is how the Sun's energy reaches Earth." → **Radiation**

3. "For 10 points — this property of a material describes how easily heat flows through it by direct contact, and silver has one of the highest values of any element." → **Thermal conductivity**

4. "For 10 points — a device is 40% efficient and consumes 500 joules. How many joules become waste heat?" → **300 joules**

5. "For 10 points — name the mode of heat transfer that occurs when you stir soup and hot liquid rises to the surface." → **Convection**

#### Review Questions
1. A bowl question describes "heat moving through a copper rod when one end is heated." What do you buzz and say?
2. What clue word in a bowl question should make you think "radiation"?
3. In a toss-up about efficiency, you're told a machine outputs 300 J from 500 J input. What do you answer?
