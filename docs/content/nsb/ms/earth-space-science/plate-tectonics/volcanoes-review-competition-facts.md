#### Volcano Type Comparison
| Feature | Shield | Composite (Strato) | Cinder Cone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silica content | Low | High | Intermediate |
| Viscosity | Low | High | Intermediate |
| Eruption style | Gentle, effusive | Explosive | Explosive (once) |
| Shape | Broad, gentle slope | Steep, symmetrical cone | Small, steep |
| Example | Mauna Loa, Kilauea | Mt. Fuji, Mt. St. Helens | Paricutin |

#### Top Volcanic Facts for Bowl
- Most deadly volcanic hazard: **pyroclastic flows** (not lava)
- Largest eruption in recorded history: **Tambora 1815** (VEI 7)
- 79 AD: **Pompeii** destroyed by pyroclastic flow from **Mt. Vesuvius**
- 1980: **Mt. St. Helens** erupts laterally, kills 57 people
- Largest volcano by volume: **Mauna Loa** (Hawaii)
- New volcano born in 1943: **Paricutin** (Mexico)
- Volcanoes on Ring of Fire: ~75% of all Earth's volcanoes
- Supervolcano in USA: **Yellowstone**
- Lahars = volcanic **mudflows**

#### Review Questions

2. Match: (a) Paricutin (b) Mauna Loa (c) Mt. Fuji — with volcano types: shield, composite, cinder cone.
3. What role do SO₂ emissions play in volcanic climate impact?
