#### Connecting Stars to Galaxies to Universe
- Stars form in nebulae → fuse elements → die as white dwarfs or supernovae → enrich interstellar medium → seeds next generation of stars and planets
- Galaxies are gravitationally bound collections of billions of stars → classified as spiral, elliptical, or irregular → gather in clusters and superclusters → form the cosmic web
- The universe began ~13.8 billion years ago (Big Bang) → expanded and cooled → formed atoms (~380,000 years), then stars and galaxies → continues expanding, and that expansion is accelerating (dark energy)

#### Key Numbers
- Universe age: **13.8 billion years**
- Milky Way diameter: **~100,000 light-years**
- Distance to Andromeda: **~2.5 million light-years**
- Our distance from galactic center: **~26,000 light-years**
- CMB temperature: **2.725 K**
- Light-year in km: **~9.46 trillion km**
- Nearest star: **Proxima Centauri, 4.24 ly**

3. The Andromeda Galaxy is moving toward the Milky Way at ~110 km/s. Will they collide? In approximately how many years?
