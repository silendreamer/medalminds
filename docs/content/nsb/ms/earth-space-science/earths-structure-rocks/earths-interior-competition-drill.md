#### Top Clue→Answer Pairings
| Clue phrase | Answer |
|---|---|

| "...boundary between crust and mantle..." | Mohorovičić discontinuity (Moho) |
| "...cannot travel through Earth's outer core..." | S-waves |

#### Common Bowl Traps
- **Trap:** "The mantle is liquid." → **Reality:** The mantle is solid (though it flows plastically over millions of years). Only the outer core is liquid.
- **Trap:** Confusing asthenosphere (weak, plastic) with lithosphere (rigid). The lithosphere rides on the asthenosphere.
- **Trap:** Saying the inner core is the hottest AND the reason it's liquid — it's actually solid because pressure outweighs temperature.

#### 5 Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "For 10 points, name the boundary that separates Earth's crust from its mantle." → **Mohorovičić discontinuity**
2. "For 10 points, what zone of the upper mantle is partially molten and allows tectonic plates to move?" → **Asthenosphere**
3. "For 10 points, which type of seismic wave cannot pass through Earth's outer core, proving it is liquid?" → **S-waves**
4. "For 10 points, what Danish seismologist discovered Earth's solid inner core in 1936?" → **Inge Lehmann**
5. "For 10 points, what process — driven by convection in Earth's liquid outer core — generates Earth's protective magnetic field?" → **Geodynamo**

#### Review Questions

2. What is the key word that tells you a question is about the asthenosphere rather than the lithosphere?
3. Why is "S-waves" a better answer than "seismic waves" when asked what cannot travel through the outer core?
