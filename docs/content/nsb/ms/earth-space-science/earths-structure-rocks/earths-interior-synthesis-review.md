#### 20 Must-Know Facts About Earth's Interior

1. Earth has four main layers: crust, mantle, outer core, inner core.
2. The crust is thinnest (5–10 km) under oceans (basalt) and thickest (30–70 km) under continents (granite).
3. The Mohorovičić discontinuity (Moho) separates the crust from the mantle.
4. The mantle makes up ~84% of Earth's total volume.
5. The asthenosphere is the partially molten upper mantle zone where tectonic plates move.
6. The lithosphere = crust + rigid upper mantle.
7. The outer core is **liquid** iron-nickel; the inner core is **solid** iron-nickel.
8. P-waves travel through all layers; S-waves cannot travel through the liquid outer core.
9. The P-wave shadow zone (103°–142°) proves the outer core is liquid.
10. Inge Lehmann (1936) discovered the inner core from anomalous P-wave arrivals in the shadow zone.
11. Earth's magnetic field is generated by convection in the liquid outer core (the geodynamo).
12. Earth's internal heat comes from primordial heat (formation) and radioactive decay.
13. Temperature increases ~25–30°C per km near the surface (geothermal gradient).
14. Inner core is ~6,000°C but solid due to extreme pressure (360 GPa).
15. Mantle convection drives tectonic plate movement.
16. Mantle plumes create volcanic hot spots like the Hawaiian Islands.
17. Magnetic reversals are recorded in oceanic crust on both sides of mid-ocean ridges.
18. No earthquakes occur below ~700 km depth.
19. Mars lost its magnetic field when its core solidified ~4 billion years ago.
20. Earth's differentiation (density sorting when molten) created its layered structure.
