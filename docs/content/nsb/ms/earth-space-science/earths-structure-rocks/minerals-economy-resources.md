#### Ore Minerals and Mining
An **ore** is a rock or mineral deposit that contains enough of a valuable substance to be economically mined. **Iron ore** (hematite, Fe₂O₃; magnetite, Fe₃O₄) is smelted to make steel. **Copper ore** (chalcopyrite) is used in electrical wiring. **Aluminum** comes from bauxite ore. **Gold** (Au) and **silver** (Ag) occur as native elements. The U.S. imports many critical minerals — including **rare earth elements** (neodymium for magnets in wind turbines and EV motors, lithium for batteries) — raising national security concerns about supply chains.

#### Silicon: The Mineral That Powers the Digital Age
**Silicon** is the second-most abundant element in Earth's crust (after oxygen). Purified silicon, derived from quartz (SiO₂), is the semiconductor that makes computer chips, solar cells, and fiber optic cables possible. The San Francisco Bay Area's "Silicon Valley" is named after this mineral. The purity required for computer chips (99.9999999% pure silicon — "nine nines") is one of the most demanding manufacturing processes in history.

#### Minerals You Use Every Day
- **Table salt** = halite (NaCl)
- **Toothpaste abrasive** = calcium carbonate (calcite) or silica (quartz)
- **Talcum powder** = talc (the softest mineral)
- **Plaster and drywall** = gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O)
- **Pencil "lead"** = graphite (pure carbon) — not actually lead
- **Jewelry** = quartz (amethyst, citrine), corundum (ruby, sapphire), beryl (emerald, aquamarine), diamond (carbon)
- **Fertilizer** = phosphate minerals (apatite)

#### Mineral Sustainability and Environmental Impact
Mining extracts minerals that took millions of years to form — they are **non-renewable** on human timescales. Open-pit mining (used for copper, gold) can remove entire mountaintops and create acid mine drainage when sulfide minerals react with water and oxygen, producing sulfuric acid that poisons streams. Recycling metals (especially aluminum, which requires only 5% of the energy to recycle vs. mine) reduces environmental impact. The growing demand for lithium and cobalt for electric vehicle batteries is driving new mining in South America and Africa.

#### Gemstones: Minerals Valued for Beauty
**Gemstones** are minerals (or rarely, rocks) valued for beauty, rarity, and durability. The "four C's" of gem grading: **cut** (how it's shaped to maximize light reflection), **color**, **clarity** (freedom from inclusions), and **carat** (weight: 1 carat = 0.2 grams). Diamond is the hardest natural substance (Mohs 10) and the most valuable gemstone. Rubies and sapphires are both corundum (Al₂O₃) — rubies are red due to chromium impurities, sapphires are blue due to iron and titanium.
