#### Top Clue→Answer Pairings
| Clue | Answer |
|---|---|
| "...coarse-grained, formed from slowly cooled magma underground..." | Granite (intrusive igneous) |
| "...fine-grained, formed from rapidly cooled lava..." | Basalt (extrusive igneous) |
| "...glassy, formed when lava cools almost instantly..." | Obsidian |
| "...limestone transformed by heat and pressure..." | Marble |
| "...shale transformed by metamorphism..." | Slate (then phyllite, schist, gneiss) |
| "...sandstone transformed by metamorphism..." | Quartzite |
| "...sedimentary rock most likely to contain fossils..." | Limestone or shale |
| "...Law stating older layers are at the bottom..." | Law of Superposition |

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "For 10 points, what type of rock forms when magma cools slowly underground, producing large crystals?" → **Intrusive (plutonic) igneous rock / granite**
2. "For 10 points, what metamorphic rock forms when limestone is subjected to heat and pressure?" → **Marble**
3. "For 10 points, which type of rock most commonly contains fossils?" → **Sedimentary**
4. "For 10 points, what is the process by which sediment is compacted and cemented into sedimentary rock?" → **Lithification**

#### Review Questions
1. A bowl question describes "fine-grained, dark-colored volcanic rock that makes up most of the oceanic crust." What is the answer?
2. What single clue word tells you a rock is metamorphic: "crystals," "layers," or "foliation"?
3. How does crystal size help you distinguish between intrusive and extrusive igneous rocks?
