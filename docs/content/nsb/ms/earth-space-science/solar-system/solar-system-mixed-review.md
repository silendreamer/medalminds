#### Planet Classification
- 8 planets: **Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune**
- Terrestrial (rocky, inner): Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars
- Gas giants: Jupiter, Saturn
- Ice giants: Uranus, Neptune
- Dwarf planets (examples): Pluto, Eris, Ceres, Haumea, Makemake

#### Small Body Quick Reference
- **Asteroid**: rocky, orbits Sun; most in asteroid belt (Mars-Jupiter)
- **Comet**: icy nucleus; develops tail near Sun; short-period from Kuiper Belt, long-period from Oort Cloud
- **Meteor**: streak of light; meteorite if it reaches ground
- **Meteor shower**: Earth passes through comet debris trail (annual, predictable)

#### Earth Motions Summary
- Day/night: **rotation** (24 hours)
- Year: **revolution** (365.25 days)
- Seasons: **axial tilt** (23.5°)
- Longest day NH: **summer solstice (~June 21)**
- Equal day/night: **equinoxes (~March 20, Sept 22)**
