#### Planet Quick Reference
| Planet | Notable Fact |
|---|---|
| Mercury | Smallest; extreme temperature swings |
| Venus | Hottest; retrograde rotation; no moons |
| Earth | Only life; liquid water |
| Mars | Red; Olympus Mons (tallest volcano); 2 moons |
| Jupiter | Largest; Great Red Spot; 95 moons; Galilean moons |
| Saturn | Rings; least dense; Titan & Enceladus |
| Uranus | Tilted 98°; blue-green (methane) |
| Neptune | Strongest winds; Triton (retrograde orbit) |

#### Key Bowl Facts
- Hottest planet: **Venus** (not Mercury — greenhouse effect)
- Largest moon in solar system: **Ganymede** (Jupiter's moon)
- Moon with most volcanoes: **Io** (Jupiter's moon)
- Moon with subsurface ocean & geysers: **Enceladus** (Saturn's moon)
- Pluto reclassified: **2006** by IAU
- New Horizons flew by Pluto: **2015**

#### Toss-Up Stems
- "This moon of Jupiter is the most volcanically active body in the solar system..." → **Io**
- "Name the largest moon in the solar system..." → **Ganymede**
- "This planet has the most powerful winds in the solar system..." → **Neptune**
- "For 10 points — why was Pluto reclassified as a dwarf planet?" → it hasn't cleared its orbital neighborhood (of the Kuiper Belt)
- "This feature on Jupiter is a storm larger than Earth that has persisted for centuries..." → **Great Red Spot**

#### Review Questions
1. Which planet has the hottest surface temperature, and why?
2. Name the four Galilean moons of Jupiter.
3. In what year was Pluto reclassified as a dwarf planet?
