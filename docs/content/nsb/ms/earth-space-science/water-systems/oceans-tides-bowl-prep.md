#### Key Facts
- Average ocean salinity: **35 ppt**
- Ocean covers: **71%** of Earth's surface
- Most abundant ocean salt: **sodium chloride (NaCl)**
- Moon + Sun aligned → **spring tide** (bigger range)
- Moon + Sun at 90° → **neap tide** (smaller range)
- World's largest tidal range: **Bay of Fundy**, Canada (~16 m)
- Deepest point: **Challenger Deep**, Mariana Trench (~11,000 m)
- Hydrothermal vents discovered: **1977**
- Photic zone depth: **~200 m**

#### Toss-Up Stems
- "This location in Canada has the world's largest tidal range..." → **Bay of Fundy**
- "Name the zone of the ocean where photosynthesis can occur..." → **photic zone**
- "Tides that occur during new and full moon phases are called..." → **spring tides**
- "For 10 points — what is the average salinity of ocean water in parts per thousand?" → **35 ppt**
- "Discovered in 1977, these seafloor ecosystems are powered by chemosynthesis..." → **hydrothermal vents**

#### Review Questions
1. What type of tide has the greatest tidal range?
2. Name the deepest known point in the ocean and its approximate depth.
3. What process powers hydrothermal vent ecosystems instead of photosynthesis?
