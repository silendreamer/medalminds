#### Quick Recall
- Water cycle processes in order for a raindrop: **precipitation → runoff/infiltration → groundwater/river → evaporation → condensation → precipitation**
- Ocean salinity average: **35 ppt**
- Two types of tides: **spring** (Moon-Sun aligned) and **neap** (Moon-Sun at 90°)
- Glacier accumulation zone: gains mass; ablation zone: loses mass
- Eutrophication: nutrient pollution → algal bloom → oxygen depletion → dead zone

#### Cross-Concept Connections
- Glaciers are the largest fresh water reservoir → their melting raises sea level → threatens coastal cities
- Groundwater recharge requires infiltration → urbanization reduces infiltration → groundwater depletion
- River sediment loads → delta formation → where rivers meet oceans
- Ocean thermocline → limits mixing → why surface and deep waters differ dramatically in properties
- Upwelling → brings cold, nutrient-rich deep water to surface → highly productive fisheries

#### Practice Questions
1. A farmer applies excess fertilizer near a lake. Trace the chain of events from fertilizer application to a hypoxic dead zone in the lake.
2. Why are glaciers important as a fresh water resource even though they're frozen?
3. Explain why a city built on impervious pavement experiences more flooding than the same area with natural soil cover.
