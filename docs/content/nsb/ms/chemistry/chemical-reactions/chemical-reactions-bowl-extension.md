#### Toss-Up Patterns

**Pattern 1:**
*"For 10 points, in a chemical reaction, what law states that the total mass of reactants must equal the total mass of products?"*
Answer: **Law of Conservation of Mass**. Buzz on "mass" + "reactants equals products." Also accept: Lavoisier's Law.

**Pattern 2:**
*"For 10 points, what type of chemical reaction involves a fuel and oxygen as reactants, with carbon dioxide and water as products?"*
Answer: **Combustion reaction**. Buzz on "fuel" + "oxygen" + "CO₂ and water."

**Pattern 3:**
*"In the reaction 2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂, what type of reaction is occurring?"*
Answer: **Decomposition**. Buzz on "one reactant, multiple products."

**Pattern 4:**
*"For 10 points, what is the term for a chemical equation in which each side has the same number of atoms of each element?"*
Answer: **Balanced equation**. Buzz on "same number of atoms on each side."

#### Common Traps
- Subscripts vs. coefficients: subscripts are fixed (changing them changes the substance); coefficients can be adjusted.
- Combustion of hydrocarbons ALWAYS produces CO₂ and H₂O (when oxygen is in excess).
- Conservation of mass applies to ALL reactions — even nuclear? No — nuclear reactions convert mass to energy; conservation of mass-energy applies.

2. Which scientist is credited with establishing the Law of Conservation of Mass?
3. What are the products when methane (CH₄) undergoes complete combustion?
