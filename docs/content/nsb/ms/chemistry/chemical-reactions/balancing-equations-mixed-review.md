#### Practice Set
Balance and classify each:

1. __ Mg + __ O₂ → __ MgO
   → 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO | **Synthesis**

2. __ H₂O → __ H₂ + __ O₂
   → 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ | **Decomposition**

3. __ Al + __ HCl → __ AlCl₃ + __ H₂
   → 2Al + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂ | **Single replacement**

4. __ NaOH + __ HCl → __ NaCl + __ H₂O
   → NaOH + HCl → NaCl + H₂O | **Double replacement** (acid-base)

5. __ C₂H₆ + __ O₂ → __ CO₂ + __ H₂O
   → 2C₂H₆ + 7O₂ → 4CO₂ + 6H₂O | **Combustion**

#### Law of Conservation of Mass Check
Sum of masses of reactants = sum of masses of products. This is why equations must be balanced — balanced equations are a mathematical expression of conservation of mass.

#### Review Questions
1. Balance: __ P + __ O₂ → __ P₂O₅, and identify the reaction type.
2. What law of science requires chemical equations to be balanced?
3. In the reaction 2KClO₃ → 2KCl + 3O₂, what type of reaction is this, and what is the total number of oxygen atoms on each side?
