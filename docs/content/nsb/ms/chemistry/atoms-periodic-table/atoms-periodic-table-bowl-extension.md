#### Toss-Up Patterns

**Pattern 1:**
*"For 10 points, which group on the periodic table contains the most reactive nonmetals, including fluorine and chlorine?"*
Answer: **Group 17** (halogens). Buzz on "most reactive nonmetals" or "includes fluorine and chlorine."

**Pattern 2:**
*"What element has the highest electronegativity of all elements on the periodic table?"*
Answer: **Fluorine (F)**. Quick recall — always fluorine.

**Pattern 3:**
*"For 10 points, what type of chemical bond involves the transfer of electrons from a metal to a nonmetal?"*
Answer: **Ionic bond**. Buzz on "transfer of electrons" + "metal to nonmetal."

**Pattern 4:**
*"What term describes the electrons in the outermost energy level of an atom, which determine the atom's chemical behavior?"*
Answer: **Valence electrons**. Buzz on "outermost energy level" + "chemical behavior."

#### Fast-Recall Facts
- Most reactive metal: Francium (Fr) — Group 1, bottom
- Most reactive nonmetal: Fluorine (F) — Group 17, top
- Most electronegative element: Fluorine
- Noble gases: He, Ne, Ar, Kr, Xe, Rn (Group 18)
- Alkali metals: Li, Na, K, Rb, Cs, Fr (Group 1)
- Halogens: F, Cl, Br, I, At (Group 17)
- Diatomic elements: H₂, N₂, O₂, F₂, Cl₂, Br₂, I₂

#### Review Questions
1. Which two elements are the most reactive on the periodic table (one metal, one nonmetal)?
2. Describe the trend in atomic radius moving from left to right across period 3.
3. Name the group of elements that includes helium, neon, and argon. Why do these elements rarely form compounds?
