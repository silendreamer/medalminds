#### Metallic Alloys
An **alloy** is a solid homogeneous mixture of two or more metals (or metal + non-metal). Alloys often have superior properties to pure metals:
- **Steel**: iron + carbon → stronger than pure iron; used in construction
- **Stainless steel**: iron + chromium + nickel → rust resistant; used in cutlery, medical tools
- **Bronze**: copper + tin → harder than copper; used historically for weapons and coins
- **Brass**: copper + zinc → gold-colored, used in instruments and plumbing fittings
- **24K gold** = pure gold; **18K gold** = 75% gold alloy (more durable)

#### Composition of Air
Air is a homogeneous gaseous mixture (not a compound). Approximate composition:
- Nitrogen (N₂): ~78%
- Oxygen (O₂): ~21%
- Argon (Ar): ~0.93%
- Carbon dioxide (CO₂): ~0.04%
- Other gases: traces

Common trap: students think air is mostly oxygen — it's mostly **nitrogen**. The oxygen is only about 21%.

#### Biological Solutions
Blood is a heterogeneous mixture (plasma + cells). Ocean water is a solution (water + dissolved salts, gases). Cytoplasm inside cells is a solution of water, salts, and organic molecules. Stomach acid is a dilute solution of hydrochloric acid (HCl).
