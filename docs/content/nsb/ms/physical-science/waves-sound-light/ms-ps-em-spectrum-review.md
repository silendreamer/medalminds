#### EM Spectrum Quick-Reference
| Type | Wavelength | Energy | Key Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Radio | Longest | Lowest | Communication |
| Microwave | Long | Low | Cooking, radar |
| Infrared | Medium-long | Medium-low | Heat, remotes |
| Visible | Medium | Medium | Vision |
| UV | Short | Medium-high | Sterilization |
| X-ray | Very short | High | Medical imaging |
| Gamma | Shortest | Highest | Cancer treatment |

#### Mixed Problems
1. Which has higher frequency: UV or infrared? (UV — shorter wavelength = higher frequency)
2. Cell phones use microwaves. Which region of the spectrum? (Microwave)
3. Which EM wave is most dangerous to biological tissue? (Gamma rays — highest energy)
4. A hospital uses EM radiation to sterilize equipment. Which type? (UV or gamma — both used; gamma for sealed equipment)

#### Review Questions
1. Arrange in order of increasing wavelength: X-rays, radio waves, visible light, infrared.
2. All EM waves travel at the same speed in a vacuum. What varies between them?
3. Why can you hear music from a radio in a building but not see the radio station through walls?
