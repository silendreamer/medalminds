#### Bowl-Ready Facts
- Order: Radio → Micro → IR → Visible → UV → X-ray → Gamma (increasing energy)
- All EM waves: speed c = 3×10⁸ m/s in vacuum; transverse; no medium needed
- Visible light: red (longest λ) to violet (shortest λ); ROY G BIV
- Highest energy/frequency/shortest λ: gamma rays; lowest: radio waves
- v = fλ applies to all EM waves; c = fλ in vacuum

#### Practice Toss-Ups
- "For 10 points, which region of the electromagnetic spectrum has the highest energy?" — **Gamma rays**
- "For 10 points, what type of electromagnetic radiation is emitted by TV remotes?" — **Infrared**
- "For 10 points, arrange these in order of increasing wavelength: X-rays, radio waves, UV." — **X-rays, UV, radio waves**
- "For 10 points, which electromagnetic wave region includes wavelengths between 380 and 700 nanometers?" — **Visible light**

#### Review Questions
1. What do all electromagnetic waves have in common?
2. Which has more energy per photon: a 500 nm green light wave or a 700 nm red light wave?
3. Name one use each of X-rays and ultraviolet light in medicine.
