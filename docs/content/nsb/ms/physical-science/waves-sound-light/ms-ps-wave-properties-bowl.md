#### Bowl-Ready Facts
- Mechanical waves need a medium; EM waves do not
- Sound in air ≈ 343 m/s; light in vacuum = 3×10⁸ m/s
- v = fλ is the key wave equation — memorize it
- Period T = 1/f; frequency f = 1/T
- When wave enters new medium: frequency stays constant; speed and wavelength change
- Constructive interference → larger amplitude; destructive → smaller or zero

#### Practice Toss-Ups
- "For 10 points, what type of wave requires a medium to travel through?" — **Mechanical wave**
- "For 10 points, a wave has frequency 400 Hz and speed 340 m/s. What is its wavelength?" — **0.85 m**
- "For 10 points, what wave phenomenon occurs when two waves meet crest-to-crest?" — **Constructive interference**
- "For 10 points, what is the period of a wave that completes 50 cycles per second?" — **0.02 seconds**
