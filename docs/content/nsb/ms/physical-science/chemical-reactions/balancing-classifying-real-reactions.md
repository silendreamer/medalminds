#### Photosynthesis — Synthesis Reaction
6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Verify balance: Left: 6C, 18O (12 from CO₂ + 6 from H₂O), 12H | Right: 6C, 18O (6 from glucose + 12 from O₂), 12H ✓
Type: synthesis (multiple reactants → one complex product + O₂); also an endothermic reaction (absorbs light energy).

#### Cellular Respiration — Combustion-like
C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O

This is essentially the reverse of photosynthesis. Type: similar to combustion (fuel + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + energy).

#### Rusting of Iron
4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃

Type: synthesis (two reactants → one product). Rusting is a slow oxidation reaction.

#### Baking Soda + Vinegar
NaHCO₃ + CH₃COOH → CH₃COONa + H₂O + CO₂

Type: double replacement. The CO₂ gas bubbles are the visible evidence of reaction.

#### Review Questions
1. Balance: __ Fe + __ O₂ → __ Fe₂O₃, and identify the reaction type.
2. In photosynthesis, what are the reactants and what are the products?
3. When baking soda reacts with vinegar, you observe bubbling. What gas is produced, and what type of reaction is occurring?
