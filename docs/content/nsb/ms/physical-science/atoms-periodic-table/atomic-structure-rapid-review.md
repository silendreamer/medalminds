#### Particle Summary Table
| Particle | Charge | Mass (amu) | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proton | +1 | 1 | Nucleus |
| Neutron | 0 | 1 | Nucleus |
| Electron | −1 | ~0 (1/1836) | Electron cloud |

#### Key Facts: Models
- Dalton: solid sphere (indivisible)
- Thomson: plum pudding (electrons in positive "pudding")
- Rutherford: nuclear model (dense positive nucleus, mostly empty space)
- Bohr: planetary model (electrons in energy levels/shells)
- Modern: quantum/probability cloud model

#### Isotope & Ion Quick-Check
- Same element, different neutron count → isotopes
- Atom loses electrons → cation (positive)
- Atom gains electrons → anion (negative)
- Atomic mass on PT = weighted average of isotopes
