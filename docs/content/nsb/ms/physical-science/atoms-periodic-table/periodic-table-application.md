#### Predicting Properties from Group
Knowing a group tells you an element's likely behavior:
- **Alkali metals (Group 1)**: All react vigorously with water to produce hydrogen gas and a metal hydroxide. Reactivity increases down the group (Fr is most reactive). Lithium reacts mildly; potassium ignites; cesium explodes.
- **Halogens (Group 17)**: All react to gain one electron and form −1 ions. F₂ is most reactive (will react with almost everything); I₂ is least reactive of the stable halogens.
- **Noble gases (Group 18)**: Full outer shell → nearly zero reactivity. He, Ne, Ar essentially form no compounds.

#### Predicting Ion Charges
Groups tell you what charge ions will form:
- Group 1 → +1 ions (Li⁺, Na⁺, K⁺)
- Group 2 → +2 ions (Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺)
- Group 17 → −1 ions (F⁻, Cl⁻, Br⁻, I⁻)
- Group 16 → −2 ions (O²⁻, S²⁻)
- Group 15 → −3 ions (N³⁻, P³⁻)

#### The "Staircase" and Metalloids
The staircase line on the PT divides metals (left) from nonmetals (right). Elements touching the staircase (B, Si, Ge, As, Sb, Te, Po) are metalloids/semiconductors. Silicon (Si) is the most important semiconductor, used in nearly all computer chips.

#### Review Questions
1. Predict the charge of the ion formed by fluorine. How did you determine this?
2. Why are noble gases the least reactive elements on the periodic table?
3. Where are metalloids located on the periodic table, and what property makes silicon valuable in electronics?
