#### Bowl-Ready Facts
- Series: R_T = ΣRᵢ; same current; voltage divides; one break stops all
- Parallel: 1/R_T = Σ(1/Rᵢ); same voltage; current divides; one break doesn't stop others; R_T < smallest R
- Ammeter: series; voltmeter: parallel
- Household wiring: parallel (each device gets 120 V; independent control)
- Batteries in series: voltages add; batteries in parallel: current capacity adds, same voltage

#### Practice Toss-Ups
- "For 10 points, in what type of circuit does each component receive the same voltage?" — **Parallel circuit**
- "For 10 points, in a series circuit with a 9 V battery and three equal 3 Ω resistors, what is the current?" — **1 ampere**
- "For 10 points, what type of circuit is used in home electrical wiring?" — **Parallel**
- "For 10 points, two 6 Ω resistors in parallel. What is the combined resistance?" — **3 ohms**

2. Adding a third branch to a parallel circuit — does total resistance increase or decrease?
3. Why would connecting all holiday lights in series be a bad design choice?
