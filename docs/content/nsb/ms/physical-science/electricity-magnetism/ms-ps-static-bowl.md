#### Bowl-Ready Facts
- SI unit of charge: coulomb (C); electron charge: −1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C
- Coulomb's Law: F = kq₁q₂/r²; k ≈ 9×10⁹ N·m²/C²
- Inverse-square: 2× distance → 1/4 force; 3× distance → 1/9 force
- Electric field lines: away from positive; toward negative
- Grounding: neutralizes charge via Earth connection
- Triboelectric effect: electrons transfer by friction; sign depends on material

#### Practice Toss-Ups
- "For 10 points, what law describes the force between two electric charges as inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them?" — **Coulomb's Law**
- "For 10 points, what is the SI unit of electric charge?" — **Coulomb**
- "For 10 points, when a balloon is charged by rubbing on wool, what type of charge does it typically acquire?" — **Negative (it gains electrons)**
- "For 10 points, what process neutralizes a charged object by connecting it to Earth?" — **Grounding**

#### Review Questions
1. Two charges repel with 16 N of force at distance d. What force at distance 2d?
2. Name three methods of charging an object.
3. In an electric field diagram, which direction do field lines point near a positive charge?
