#### Core Equations
| Equation | Variables |
|---|---|
| V = IR | Ohm's Law |
| P = IV | Power (basic) |
| P = I²R | Power (current + resistance) |
| P = V²/R | Power (voltage + resistance) |
| E = Pt | Energy from power × time |

#### Quick Mixed Problems
1. V = 18 V, R = 9 Ω → I = 2 A; P = IV = 2×18 = 36 W
2. I = 5 A, R = 4 Ω → V = 20 V; P = I²R = 25×4 = 100 W
3. P = 800 W, V = 120 V → I = 800/120 ≈ 6.67 A; R = V/I = 120/6.67 ≈ 18 Ω
4. 500 W device × 2 h = 1 kWh? → 500 W × 2 h = 1000 Wh = 1 kWh ✓
