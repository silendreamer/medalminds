#### Core Facts
- Like poles repel; unlike attract; monopoles don't exist
- Earth's field: generated by liquid iron core; deflects solar wind; poles offset from geographic poles
- Oersted: current creates magnetic field; right-hand rule
- Electromagnet: solenoid + iron core; stronger with more turns, more current
- Faraday: changing magnetic field → induced current → generator principle
- Transformer: step-up (V increases, I decreases); step-down (V decreases, I increases)

#### Mixed Problems
1. A solenoid has 50 turns and carries 2 A. To double the field strength, you could: (a) double the current, or (b) double the number of turns. Which changes are correct? (Both — each doubles strength)
2. A transformer: primary = 200 turns, secondary = 50 turns, input 120 V → output = 120 × 50/200 = **30 V** (step-down)
3. What converts electrical energy to mechanical energy? (Motor) Mechanical to electrical? (Generator)

#### Review Questions

2. Why does an electromagnet stop working when the current is switched off?
3. What is Lenz's Law (in simple terms): the induced current opposes the change that caused it — which direction does the induced current flow when a magnet moves into a coil?
