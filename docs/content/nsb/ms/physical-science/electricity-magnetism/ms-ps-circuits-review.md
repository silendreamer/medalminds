#### Common Mistakes
1. Thinking parallel resistance adds like series — it doesn't! 1/R_T = Σ(1/Rᵢ).
2. Thinking current splits equally in parallel — it splits by inverse resistance (more current through smaller R).
3. Forgetting that adding parallel branches REDUCES total resistance.
4. Confusing ammeter (series) with voltmeter (parallel).

#### Summary Problems
1. R₁=6Ω, R₂=6Ω in series, 12V battery → R_T=12Ω, I=1A, V_each=6V
2. R₁=6Ω, R₂=6Ω in parallel, 12V battery → R_T=3Ω, I_total=4A, I_each=2A
3. More devices in parallel → total R decreases → more current drawn → could trip breaker

#### Review Questions
1. Three 9 Ω resistors in parallel. What is the total resistance?
2. Two bulbs in series with a 6 V battery. Each has resistance 3 Ω. What is the voltage across each?
3. In which circuit type does a blown fuse protect all connected devices simultaneously?
