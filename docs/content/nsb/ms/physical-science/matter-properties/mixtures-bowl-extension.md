#### Toss-Up Patterns

**Pattern 1:**
*"For 10 points, what type of mixture has a uniform composition throughout, such as saltwater or air?"*
Answer: **Homogeneous mixture** (or solution). Buzz on "uniform composition."

**Pattern 2:**
*"What is the approximate percentage of nitrogen in Earth's atmosphere?"*
Answer: **78%**. Buzz immediately — this is quick recall. Common wrong: "21%" (that's oxygen).

**Pattern 3:**
*"What separation technique uses the different boiling points of substances to separate a liquid mixture?"*
Answer: **Distillation**.

**Pattern 4:**
*"Bronze is an alloy of copper and what other metal?"*
Answer: **Tin**. Brass = copper + zinc. Don't confuse them.

#### Quick-Fire Facts
- Water = compound (H₂O), not a mixture
- Air = mixture (N₂ + O₂ + Ar + CO₂ + others), not a compound
- Steel = alloy = homogeneous mixture
- Saturated = max solute dissolved; supersaturated = more than max (unstable)
- Chromatography separates by how components interact with stationary/mobile phase

#### Review Questions
1. Steel is a mixture of iron and carbon. What type of mixture is it?
2. What does the term "supersaturated" mean, and what happens if you drop a crystal into a supersaturated solution?
3. Name the two most abundant gases in Earth's atmosphere and their approximate percentages.
