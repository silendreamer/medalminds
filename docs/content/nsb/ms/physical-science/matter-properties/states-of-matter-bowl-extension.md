#### Top Toss-Up Patterns

**Pattern 1 — Phase change naming:**
*"For 10 points, what is the term for the phase change in which a solid converts directly to a gas?"*
Answer: **Sublimation**. Buzz when you hear "solid" + "directly" + "gas." Don't wait for "dry ice."

**Pattern 2 — Most common state:**
*"What state of matter makes up the Sun and most of the observable universe?"*
Answer: **Plasma**. Buzz on "Sun" or "most common state in the universe."

**Pattern 3 — Temperature at absolute zero:**
*"For 10 points, what temperature in Celsius corresponds to absolute zero, the theoretical point at which all particle motion ceases?"*
Answer: **−273°C** (accept −273.15°C). Buzz when you hear "absolute zero" and "Celsius."

**Pattern 4 — Ice density anomaly:**
*"What property of water explains why ice floats on liquid water, an anomaly not seen in most substances?"*
Answer: **Ice is less dense than liquid water** (due to hydrogen bonding forming an open lattice structure). Buzz when you hear "ice floats" + "why" or "anomaly."

#### Common Traps to Avoid
- Don't confuse **heat** and **temperature** — heat is energy transferred, temperature is average KE.
- Don't say temperature rises continuously during heating — it plateaus during phase changes.
- Plasma ≠ hot gas — plasma is ionized (electrons removed from atoms).

#### Review Questions
1. What phase change name applies when water vapor (gas) becomes frost (solid) on a cold surface?
2. A student says "the Sun is a very hot gas." Why is this technically incorrect?
3. At what Kelvin temperature does water boil?
