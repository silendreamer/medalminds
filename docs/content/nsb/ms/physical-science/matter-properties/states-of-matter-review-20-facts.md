#### The 20 Facts

1. Solids have definite shape AND definite volume.
2. Liquids have definite volume but NO definite shape.
3. Gases have NEITHER definite shape nor definite volume.
4. Plasma is the most common state of matter in the universe.
5. Melting = solid → liquid (absorbs heat).
6. Freezing = liquid → solid (releases heat).
7. Evaporation/boiling = liquid → gas (absorbs heat).
8. Condensation = gas → liquid (releases heat).
9. Sublimation = solid → gas directly (dry ice example).
10. Deposition = gas → solid directly (frost example).
11. During a phase change, temperature stays constant.
12. 0 K = absolute zero = −273°C (coldest possible temperature).
13. Kelvin = Celsius + 273.
14. Water boils at 100°C / 212°F.
15. Water freezes at 0°C / 32°F.
16. Ice is LESS dense than liquid water (ice floats).
17. Heat transfers by conduction, convection, or radiation.
18. Radiation is the only heat transfer that doesn't need a medium.
19. Temperature measures AVERAGE kinetic energy of particles.
20. Thermal energy is the TOTAL kinetic energy of all particles.

#### Review Questions
1. What is the phase change called when a solid goes directly to a gas? Name one example.
2. What is absolute zero in Celsius?
3. How does the density of ice compare to liquid water, and why is this unusual?
