#### Quick Classification Tree
Matter → Pure Substance or Mixture?
- Pure Substance → Element or Compound?
  - Element: one type of atom (O₂, Fe, He)
  - Compound: fixed ratio of elements (H₂O, NaCl, CO₂)
- Mixture → Homogeneous or Heterogeneous?
  - Homogeneous (solution): uniform (saltwater, air, alloys)
  - Heterogeneous: non-uniform (salad, granite, blood)

#### Solubility Factors Recap
- Temperature ↑ → solid solubility usually ↑, gas solubility ↓
- Pressure ↑ → gas solubility ↑ (important for carbonation)
- "Like dissolves like" — polar in polar, non-polar in non-polar

#### Separation Match-Up
| Mixture | Best Method |
|---|---|
| Sand + water | Filtration |
| Saltwater | Evaporation or distillation |
| Alcohol + water | Distillation |
| Iron filings + sand | Magnetism |
| Ink dyes | Chromatography |

#### Review Questions
1. Classify each: NaCl, bronze, O₂, saltwater, CO₂, granite.
2. A student dissolves as much sugar as possible in warm water, then cools it slowly without disturbing it. What type of solution results?
3. Which separation method would you use to recover the sugar from a sugar-water solution?
