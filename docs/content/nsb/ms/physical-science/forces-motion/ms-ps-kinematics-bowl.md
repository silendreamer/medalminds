#### Toss-Up Pattern Recognition
Bowl kinematics toss-ups often start with: "For 10 points, a car accelerates from rest to..." or "For 10 points, what is the SI unit for..." Listen for trigger words: *vector* → think velocity/force/displacement; *scalar* → think speed/distance/mass; *meters per second squared* → acceleration; *constant speed in a circle* → centripetal acceleration, not zero.

#### Practice Toss-Ups
- "For 10 points, what quantity is defined as the rate of change of velocity?" — **Acceleration**
- "For 10 points, a cyclist travels 300 m in 15 s. What is her speed in meters per second?" — **20 m/s**
- "For 10 points, what is the acceleration due to gravity at Earth's surface, in meters per second squared?" — **9.8 (accept 10)**
- "For 10 points, what term describes a vector quantity that includes both speed and direction?" — **Velocity**

#### Review Questions
1. What is the SI unit of acceleration?
2. A ball is dropped and falls for 3 s (g = 10 m/s²). What speed does it reach?
3. Name two scalar quantities and two vector quantities.
