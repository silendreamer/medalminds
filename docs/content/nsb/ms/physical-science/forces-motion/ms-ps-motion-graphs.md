#### Position-Time Graphs
The slope of a position-time (x-t) graph equals velocity. Steep slope = fast; gentle slope = slow. Positive slope = moving in positive direction; negative slope = moving in negative direction; horizontal line = stationary. A curved line means changing velocity (acceleration). A straight line means constant velocity (zero acceleration). Bowl questions sometimes show you a graph and ask: "Which segment shows the greatest speed?" — find the steepest slope.

#### Velocity-Time Graphs
The slope of a velocity-time (v-t) graph equals acceleration. Positive slope = speeding up; negative slope = slowing down; horizontal line = constant velocity (zero acceleration). The area under the curve equals displacement. A v-t graph showing a horizontal line at v = 5 m/s for 10 s means the object moved 5 × 10 = 50 m. Science Bowl sometimes combines x-t and v-t questions for the same motion.

#### Matching Graphs to Motion Descriptions
"A car starts from rest, accelerates to 20 m/s over 4 s, maintains that speed for 6 s, then decelerates to rest over 2 s." Translate this to a v-t graph: line from (0,0) to (4,20); horizontal at 20 from t=4 to t=10; line from (10,20) to (12,0). Then find total displacement: area = triangle + rectangle + triangle = ½(4)(20) + 6(20) + ½(2)(20) = 40 + 120 + 20 = 180 m.

#### Review Questions
1. On a position-time graph, what does a horizontal line mean? What does a line with negative slope mean?
2. A velocity-time graph shows v = 8 m/s for 5 seconds. What is the displacement?
3. Draw a rough sketch of the velocity-time graph for an object thrown straight up and falling back down.
