#### High-Yield Bowl Facts
- 6 simple machines: lever, inclined plane, wedge, screw, pulley, wheel and axle
- Lever classes: 1st (fulcrum middle) — crowbar; 2nd (load middle) — wheelbarrow; 3rd (effort middle) — tweezers
- Pulley MA = number of rope segments supporting load
- Inclined plane IMA = length/height
- Efficiency = (AMA/IMA) × 100% = (output work/input work) × 100%
- Work = Force × Distance; simple machines don't reduce total work

#### Practice Toss-Ups
- "For 10 points, a wheelbarrow is an example of what class of lever?" — **Second class**
- "For 10 points, what is the mechanical advantage of a pulley system with 4 rope segments?" — **4**
- "For 10 points, what simple machine is described as an inclined plane wrapped around a cylinder?" — **Screw**
- "For 10 points, if you use 100 J of work to do 80 J of useful work, what is the efficiency?" — **80%**
