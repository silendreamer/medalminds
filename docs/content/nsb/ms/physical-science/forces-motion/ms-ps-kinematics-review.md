#### Common Bowl Traps
Trap 1: "Constant speed = zero acceleration" — FALSE if direction changes (circular motion). Trap 2: "Average speed = (v1 + v2)/2" — only true if acceleration is constant; otherwise use total distance/total time. Trap 3: "Displacement = distance" — only true for straight-line motion with no backtracking. Trap 4: Confusing m/s and km/h — always check units. Trap 5: Forgetting that velocity must include direction; speed does not.

#### High-Yield Facts
- g ≈ 9.8 m/s² ≈ 10 m/s² (Earth's surface)
- Speed of sound in air: ~343 m/s (20°C)
- Speed of light in vacuum: 3×10⁸ m/s
- v = d/t; a = Δv/t; d = ½at² (from rest)
- Scalar: speed, distance, mass, time, temperature
- Vector: velocity, acceleration, force, displacement

#### Review Questions
1. A car turns a corner at a constant 15 m/s. Is it accelerating? Explain.
2. What scalar quantity always equals or is less than the magnitude of displacement on any trip?
3. An object falls from rest. Using g = 10 m/s², what is its speed after 5 s?
