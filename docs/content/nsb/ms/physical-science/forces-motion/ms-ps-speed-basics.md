#### Defining Speed
Speed is the rate at which an object moves — how much distance it covers in a given amount of time. The formula is simple: speed = distance ÷ time, or v = d/t. Speed is always a positive number, measured in units like meters per second (m/s), kilometers per hour (km/h), or miles per hour (mph). In Science Bowl, meters per second is the standard SI unit. If a car travels 120 meters in 10 seconds, its speed is 120 ÷ 10 = 12 m/s.

#### Units and Conversions
The SI unit for speed is meters per second (m/s). When you see km/h, multiply by 1,000 and divide by 3,600 to convert to m/s (or multiply by 0.278). For example, 72 km/h = 72 × 1000 / 3600 = 20 m/s. Science Bowl questions often give distance in kilometers and time in hours, then ask for the answer in m/s — always check your units before calculating.

#### Average vs. Instantaneous Speed
Average speed covers the whole trip: total distance ÷ total time. A car that drives 60 km in the first hour and 90 km in the next hour has an average speed of 150 km / 2 h = 75 km/h, even though it wasn't always moving at 75 km/h. Instantaneous speed is the speed at one specific moment — what a speedometer shows. Science Bowl problems usually deal with average speed unless they mention a specific instant.

#### Solving Speed Problems Step by Step
To solve v = d/t problems: (1) Identify what's given and what's unknown. (2) Write the formula. (3) Plug in numbers with units. (4) Calculate and include the unit in your answer. Example: A cyclist travels 500 m in 25 s. Speed = 500/25 = 20 m/s. If you know speed and time, find distance: d = v × t. If you know speed and distance, find time: t = d/v. Rearranging is a core skill.

#### Real-World Connections
Speed is everywhere: weather radar clocks raindrops, police radar guns measure vehicles, GPS chips update your location every second. Sprinters reach about 10 m/s; sound travels at 343 m/s; light travels at 3×10⁸ m/s. Knowing these benchmarks helps you check whether an answer is reasonable. If you calculate a walking speed of 50 m/s, something went wrong.

#### Review Questions
1. A train travels 900 km in 6 hours. What is its average speed in km/h?
2. If a ball rolls at 5 m/s for 8 seconds, how far does it travel?
3. What is the difference between average speed and instantaneous speed?
