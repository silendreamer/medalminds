#### High-Yield Facts
- **Biomagnification** = toxin concentration increases up the food chain (classic example: **DDT and bald eagles**)
- DDT banned in U.S. = **1972**; U.S. Endangered Species Act = **1973**
- **Eutrophication** = algal bloom from excess nutrients → oxygen depletion → fish kill
- Gulf of Mexico dead zone caused by **fertilizer runoff** from Mississippi River basin
- **Coral bleaching** = coral expels zooxanthellae due to thermal stress
- Current CO₂ level ~**420 ppm** (pre-industrial ~280 ppm)
- Ocean pH has fallen from ~8.2 to ~8.1 = **26% increase in acidity** (logarithmic scale)
- Bald eagle recovered due to DDT ban and **Endangered Species Act**
- California condor went extinct in the **wild in 1987**; recovered through captive breeding

#### Bowl-Style Toss-Up Stems
- "This process causes toxins like DDT to become more concentrated at each successive trophic level…" → **biomagnification**
- "This phenomenon, caused by excess nutrient runoff into water bodies, leads to algal blooms and oxygen depletion…" → **eutrophication**
- "This process, in which warm ocean temperatures cause coral to expel their symbiotic algae, is increasing in frequency due to climate change…" → **coral bleaching**
- "This 1973 U.S. law prohibits the take of listed species and requires protection of critical habitat…" → **Endangered Species Act**

#### Common Traps
- Bioaccumulation = buildup in ONE organism; biomagnification = increases UP the food chain (across trophic levels)
- Eutrophication causes fish kills through **oxygen depletion** (NOT direct toxicity)
- Coral bleaching = coral EXPELS its algae (doesn't die immediately; bleached coral can recover if temperature drops)

#### Review Questions
1. What is the difference between bioaccumulation and biomagnification?
2. What caused the Gulf of Mexico dead zone, and what is the ecological mechanism?
3. Why did DDT harm bald eagle reproduction specifically, rather than killing eagles directly?
