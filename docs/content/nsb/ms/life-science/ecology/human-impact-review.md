#### Cause-and-Effect Chain
Trace the cause-and-effect chain: Farmer applies excess fertilizer → rain washes fertilizer into stream → creek drains into lake → [complete the chain with correct ecological terms and describe the endpoint].

#### Multiple Stressors
A coral reef is experiencing: (1) warming ocean temperatures, (2) increased fishing pressure, (3) agricultural runoff from a nearby island. For each stressor, identify the specific ecological harm it causes. Then explain how multiple simultaneous stressors can have effects greater than each stressor alone (*synergistic effects*).

#### Conservation Prioritization
You have limited funds to protect biodiversity. You can: (a) protect 100 acres of tropical rainforest, (b) restore 100 acres of temperate grassland, or (c) clean up 1 mile of urban stream. Argue for which option provides the greatest conservation value, using principles from ecology.

#### Review Questions
1. Define biomagnification and explain why top predators are most affected by environmental toxins.
2. What is the enhanced greenhouse effect, and how is it different from the natural greenhouse effect?
3. Name three threatened species that have been successfully recovered through conservation efforts.
