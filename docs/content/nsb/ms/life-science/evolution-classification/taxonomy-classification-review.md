#### Classification Hierarchy Practice
Humans are classified as: Domain Eukarya → Kingdom Animalia → Phylum Chordata → Class Mammalia → Order Primates → Family Hominidae → Genus *Homo* → Species *sapiens*. Using this as a model, write the likely genus and species name for a fictitious organism from the genus Panthera (lions, tigers, leopards). What does it mean for two organisms to share the same genus?

#### Domain Sorting
Sort these organisms into the correct domain: (1) E. coli bacterium, (2) methane-producing archaea from a cow's gut, (3) a mushroom, (4) an amoeba, (5) a thermophilic archaea from a hot spring, (6) a fern. Domains: Bacteria, Archaea, Eukarya.

#### Vertebrate Class Identification
Identify the vertebrate class for each: (1) lays eggs on land, has scales, ectothermic; (2) has moist skin, begins life in water, uses gills then lungs; (3) has feathers and a wishbone, endothermic; (4) has fur, nurses young with milk; (5) lives entirely in water, breathes with gills, has scales.

#### Review Questions
1. What information is contained in a binomial name? How is it formatted?
2. How do Archaea differ from Bacteria at the molecular level?
3. Why did modern molecular biology require reorganization of the classification system?
