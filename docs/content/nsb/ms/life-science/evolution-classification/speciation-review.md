#### Sorting Mechanisms
Classify each scenario as allopatric or sympatric speciation: (1) A river changes course, splitting a population of lizards in two; (2) Two populations of fish in the same lake specialize on different food sources; (3) A volcanic island emerges and is colonized by insects from a nearby island; (4) A plant hybrid spontaneously doubles its chromosomes and can no longer breed with either parent.

#### Isolation Barrier Types
For each pair, identify whether the isolation is pre-zygotic or post-zygotic, and name the specific mechanism: (1) Two frog species live in the same pond but breed at different times of year; (2) A horse and donkey mate and produce a sterile mule; (3) Two bird species have different courtship songs and don't recognize each other's calls as mating signals.

#### Deep Synthesis
Explain how geographic isolation, genetic variation, natural selection, and reproductive isolation work together over time to produce two species from one.

#### Review Questions
1. What is the difference between pre-zygotic and post-zygotic reproductive isolation? Give an example of each.
2. Why is polyploidy a mechanism of sympatric speciation?
3. How does adaptive radiation produce many species from one ancestor?
