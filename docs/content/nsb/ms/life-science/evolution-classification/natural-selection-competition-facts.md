#### High-Yield Facts
- Darwin published *On the Origin of Species* in **1859**
- Darwin and **Alfred Russel Wallace** independently developed the theory of natural selection
- The Galápagos finches are also called **Darwin's finches**
- The peppered moth (*Biston betularia*) is the classic example of **industrial melanism**
- **Artificial selection** = human-directed breeding; the evidence Darwin used to support natural selection
- Fitness = **reproductive success**, not strength
- Natural selection acts on **phenotype** (expressed traits), which reflects underlying **genotype**
- Lamarck's incorrect idea = **inheritance of acquired characteristics**

#### Bowl-Style Toss-Up Stems
- "This scientist, who traveled on the HMS Beagle, developed the theory of natural selection…" → **Charles Darwin**
- "The peppered moth is a classic example of this type of natural selection, driven by industrial pollution…" → **industrial melanism** (or directional selection)
- "This term describes an organism's ability to survive and reproduce in its environment…" → **fitness**
- "Darwin used this human practice — selective breeding of animals and plants — as evidence for natural selection…" → **artificial selection**

#### Common Traps
- Don't confuse *evolution* (population-level change over generations) with *adaptation* (a specific heritable trait)
- Wallace is often the answer when the question says "independently developed" or "co-discoverer"
- "HMS Beagle" → Darwin; "Lamarck" → acquired characteristics (wrong theory)

#### Review Questions
1. Who co-developed the theory of natural selection with Darwin?
2. What is industrial melanism, and which organism is the classic example?
3. Why does natural selection act on phenotype rather than genotype?
