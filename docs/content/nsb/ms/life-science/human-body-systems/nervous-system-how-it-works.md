#### Organization of the Nervous System
The nervous system has two main divisions: the central nervous system (CNS) — brain and spinal cord — processes information and commands. The peripheral nervous system (PNS) — all nerves outside the CNS — carries information to and from the CNS. The PNS is further divided into the somatic nervous system (voluntary control of skeletal muscles) and autonomic nervous system (involuntary control of heart, digestion, glands).

#### The Neuron
Neurons are the functional cells of the nervous system. Parts: (1) Cell body (soma) — contains nucleus and metabolic machinery. (2) Dendrites — branching extensions that receive signals from other neurons or sensory cells. (3) Axon — long projection that carries the signal away from the cell body. (4) Myelin sheath — fatty insulation that speeds signal conduction. (5) Axon terminals (synaptic knobs) — where signals pass to the next neuron.

#### The Action Potential
Neurons communicate using electrical signals called action potentials. At rest, the inside of a neuron is negatively charged (~-70 mV) relative to the outside — the resting membrane potential. When stimulated above threshold, ion channels open: Na⁺ rushes in (depolarization), then K⁺ rushes out (repolarization). This wave of charge reversal travels down the axon at speeds up to 120 m/s. The signal is all-or-nothing: either it fires fully or not at all.

#### The Synapse
At the synapse, the action potential reaches the axon terminal and triggers release of neurotransmitters — chemical messengers — into the synaptic cleft. Neurotransmitters bind to receptors on the dendrite of the next neuron, triggering a new signal if the combined input exceeds threshold. Common neurotransmitters: acetylcholine (muscle contraction, learning), dopamine (reward, movement), serotonin (mood), GABA (inhibitory).

#### Reflex Arcs
A reflex is an automatic, rapid response that bypasses the brain. Sensory neuron → interneuron (in spinal cord) → motor neuron → muscle. The knee-jerk reflex and pulling your hand from a flame are examples. The brain is notified after the reflex already happened.
