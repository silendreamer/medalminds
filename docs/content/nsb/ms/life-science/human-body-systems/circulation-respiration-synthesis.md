#### High-Yield Fact List

**Circulatory:**
1. Heart has 4 chambers: right atrium, right ventricle, left atrium, left ventricle
2. Arteries = Away from heart; Veins = toward heart (Vein = inVard)
3. Pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood; pulmonary vein carries oxygenated blood
4. Capillaries = site of actual gas/nutrient exchange
5. Hemoglobin = iron-containing protein in RBCs that carries O₂
6. Blood pressure: systolic/diastolic; normal ≈ 120/80
7. Plasma = ~55% of blood; contains proteins, nutrients, hormones

**Respiratory:**
8. Alveoli = site of gas exchange in lungs
9. Gas exchange occurs by diffusion (high → low concentration)
10. Diaphragm contracts during inhalation; relaxes during exhalation
11. Trachea → bronchi → bronchioles → alveoli
12. Surfactant prevents alveoli collapse
13. CO₂ concentration drives breathing rate (detected by brainstem)
14. Emphysema destroys alveoli; chronic bronchitis inflames airways
15. Cilia sweep mucus upward in respiratory tract

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "These tiny air sacs in the lungs are the site of gas exchange between air and blood." → **Alveoli**
2. "Name the dome-shaped muscle below the lungs that controls breathing." → **Diaphragm**
3. "This iron-containing protein in red blood cells binds oxygen for transport." → **Hemoglobin**
4. "Blood flowing from the right ventricle to the lungs and back is called this type of circulation." → **Pulmonary circulation**
5. "Unlike other arteries, this vessel carries deoxygenated blood away from the heart." → **Pulmonary artery**

#### Review Questions
1. What is the difference between pulmonary and systemic circulation?
2. What protein makes red blood cells able to carry oxygen?
3. Where exactly does gas exchange occur in the lungs?
