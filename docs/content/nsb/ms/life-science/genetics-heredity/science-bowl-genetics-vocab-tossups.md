#### Top 15 Bowl-Tested Genetics Terms

1. **Allele** — alternate form of a gene
2. **Dominant** — expressed in heterozygote
3. **Recessive** — expressed only in homozygote
4. **Homozygous** — two identical alleles
5. **Heterozygous** — two different alleles; carrier
6. **Genotype** — allele combination
7. **Phenotype** — observable trait
8. **Codominance** — both alleles fully expressed (AB blood type)
9. **Incomplete dominance** — blended phenotype (pink snapdragons)
10. **Polygenic** — many genes, one trait (height, skin color)
11. **Sex-linked** — gene on X chromosome; males more affected
12. **Carrier** — heterozygous for recessive, shows dominant phenotype
13. **Pedigree** — family chart tracking trait inheritance
14. **F1 generation** — first filial; offspring of parental cross
15. **Law of Segregation** — alleles separate during gamete formation

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems

1. "An organism that has two different alleles for a trait is said to be this." → **Heterozygous**
2. "Mendel's First Law states that alleles separate during gamete formation. Name this law." → **Law of Segregation**
3. "In ABO blood typing, a person with genotype IᴬIᴮ has this blood type." → **AB**
4. "In snapdragons, crossing red and white plants produces pink offspring. This pattern is called this." → **Incomplete dominance**
5. "Traits controlled by more than two genes, such as height or skin color, are called this." → **Polygenic traits**

#### Review Questions
1. What is the Law of Independent Assortment, and when does it NOT apply?
2. Distinguish codominance from incomplete dominance with one example of each.
3. "For 10 points — a woman is a carrier for hemophilia. What is her genotype?" (Hint: X-linked recessive; answer: XᴴX)
