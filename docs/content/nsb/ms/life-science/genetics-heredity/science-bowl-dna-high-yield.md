#### Top 15 High-Yield DNA Facts for Bowl

1. DNA double helix described by **Watson and Crick** in **1953** (X-ray crystallography by Franklin)
2. DNA bases: **A-T** and **G-C** pairs (hydrogen bonds hold them together: A-T = 2 bonds, G-C = 3 bonds)
3. RNA uses **uracil (U)** instead of thymine (T)
4. Replication enzyme: **DNA polymerase** (builds); **helicase** (unzips); **ligase** (seals)
5. Replication is **semiconservative**
6. Transcription makes **mRNA** in the **nucleus**
7. Translation makes **protein** at **ribosomes**
8. **Codon** = 3-base mRNA sequence; **anticodon** = matching tRNA sequence
9. **Start codon** = AUG (codes for methionine); **stop codons** = UAA, UAG, UGA
10. tRNA carries **amino acids**; rRNA is structural part of ribosome
11. Gene = segment of DNA encoding a **protein or functional RNA**
12. Human genome: **~3 billion base pairs**, **46 chromosomes**, **~20,000–25,000 genes**
13. **PCR** amplifies small DNA samples (used in forensics)
14. **CRISPR-Cas9** is a gene-editing tool
15. **DNA polymerase** works in **5' to 3' direction** only

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems

1. "This molecule carries the genetic code from the nucleus to the ribosome for translation." → **mRNA**
2. "Described by Watson and Crick in 1953, this molecule stores hereditary information in a double helix." → **DNA**
3. "This enzyme unwinds the DNA double helix during replication." → **Helicase**
4. "A three-base sequence on mRNA that codes for a specific amino acid is called this." → **Codon**
5. "This type of replication results in each new DNA molecule containing one original strand." → **Semiconservative replication**

#### Common Traps
- "RNA polymerase" ≠ "DNA polymerase" — RNA polymerase makes mRNA during transcription; DNA polymerase builds new DNA during replication
- Translation happens at ribosomes, NOT in the nucleus
- mRNA uses U, not T — if a question gives a DNA sequence and asks for the mRNA copy, switch T→U and complement the rest

#### Review Questions
1. "For 10 points — name the enzyme that joins Okazaki fragments during DNA replication." (Answer: DNA ligase)
2. What base pairs with Guanine in DNA? In RNA?
3. Where does translation occur, and what molecule initiates it?
