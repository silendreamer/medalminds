#### Problem Set

**Problem 1 (Monohybrid):** In cats, black coat (B) is dominant over gray coat (b). Cross Bb × bb. What fraction of offspring will be gray?
Answer: 1/2 (50%) — Punnett square gives Bb, Bb, bb, bb → 2 black : 2 gray

**Problem 2 (Testcross):** A black cat (unknown genotype) × gray cat (bb) produces 3 black and 3 gray kittens. What is the black cat's genotype?
Answer: Bb — if it were BB, all offspring would be black

**Problem 3 (Homozygous dominant × recessive):** TT × tt. What fraction of offspring will show the recessive phenotype?
Answer: 0% — all offspring are Tt (dominant phenotype)

**Problem 4 (Monohybrid ratio):** From Aa × Aa, how many out of 40 expected offspring will be aa?
Answer: 1/4 × 40 = 10 offspring

**Problem 5 (Dihybrid):** AaBb × AaBb. What fraction will be AABB?
Answer: P(AA) × P(BB) = 1/4 × 1/4 = 1/16

**Problem 6 (Dihybrid phenotype):** AaBb × AaBb. What fraction will show dominant phenotype for BOTH genes?
Answer: 9/16

**Problem 7 (Codominance):** Iᴬi × Iᴮi. What fraction of offspring will have blood type AB?
Answer: 1/4 — Punnett gives Iᴬ Iᴮ, Iᴬi, iIᴮ, ii → 1 AB, 1 A, 1 B, 1 O

**Problem 8 (X-linked):** Carrier mother (XᴴX) × normal father (XY). What fraction of sons will have hemophilia?
Answer: 1/2 of sons — cross gives XᴴX, XX, XᴴY, XY; 1/2 of sons are XᴴY (affected)
