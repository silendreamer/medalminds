#### Core Facts Checklist
- Positive charge: lost electrons; Negative charge: gained electrons
- Only electrons move between objects (not protons)
- Like charges repel; unlike attract
- Coulomb's Law: F ∝ q₁q₂; F ∝ 1/r² (inverse-square)
- Grounding: connect to Earth to neutralize charge
- Charging methods: friction (electrons transfer), conduction (direct contact), induction (no contact)

#### Mixed Problems
1. A balloon is rubbed on wool and becomes negatively charged. What happened to the wool? (Became positively charged — lost electrons)
2. Two charges are 1 m apart with force 9 N. At 3 m, force = ? (9/9 = 1 N — inverse-square)
3. Lightning is a discharge between cloud and ground. Which type of charge accumulates at the cloud base? (Negative — repels electrons at ground surface, leaving positive charges on ground)
4. Name one industrial use of static electricity. (Electrostatic precipitator, photocopier, spray painting)

#### Review Questions

2. Why is grounding important for electronic equipment?
