#### Bowl-Ready Facts
- Oersted (1820): current → magnetic field; right-hand rule
- Faraday (1831): changing magnetic field → induced EMF; generator principle
- Motor: electrical → mechanical; Generator: mechanical → electrical
- Transformer equation: V₁/V₂ = N₁/N₂ (power conserved: P = IV)
- Electromagnet: solenoid + iron core; on/off control
- Earth's magnetosphere: generated by liquid iron outer core; protects from solar wind
- Auroras: charged particles exciting atmospheric molecules at poles

#### Practice Toss-Ups
- "For 10 points, what scientist discovered in 1820 that a current-carrying wire creates a magnetic field?" — **Hans Christian Oersted**
- "For 10 points, what device converts mechanical energy into electrical energy using electromagnetic induction?" — **Generator**
- "For 10 points, a transformer has 10 turns on the primary and 100 on the secondary, connected to 6 V. What is the output voltage?" — **60 volts**
- "For 10 points, what is the name for the region of space around Earth where its magnetic field dominates?" — **Magnetosphere**
