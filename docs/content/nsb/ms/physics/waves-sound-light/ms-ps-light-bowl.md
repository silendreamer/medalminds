#### Bowl-Ready Facts
- Speed of light in vacuum: c = 3×10⁸ m/s
- Index of refraction: n = c/v; water ≈ 1.33; glass ≈ 1.5; diamond ≈ 2.42
- Law of reflection: angle of incidence = angle of reflection (from normal)
- Refraction: entering denser medium → bends toward normal
- Total internal reflection → fiber optics
- Convex lens converges; concave diverges
- Dispersion: prism separates white light (violet bends most, red least)

#### Practice Toss-Ups
- "For 10 points, what phenomenon explains why a straw appears bent in a glass of water?" — **Refraction**
- "For 10 points, light travels from glass into air. If the angle exceeds the critical angle, what occurs?" — **Total internal reflection**
- "For 10 points, what is the approximate speed of light in a vacuum?" — **3×10⁸ m/s (or 3×10⁸ meters per second)**
- "For 10 points, what color of visible light has the longest wavelength?" — **Red**

#### Review Questions
1. Light bounces off a mirror with an angle of reflection of 40°. What was the angle of incidence?
2. Which bends more when entering glass: red light or violet light?
3. Name the technology that uses total internal reflection to transmit data as pulses of light.
