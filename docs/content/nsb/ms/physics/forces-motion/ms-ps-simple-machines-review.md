#### Rapid Review Table

| Machine | IMA Formula | Key Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Lever | effort arm / resistance arm | 3 classes based on fulcrum position |
| Inclined plane | length / height | longer ramp = lower force needed |
| Pulley | # of support ropes | fixed pulley MA = 1 (direction only) |
| Wheel & axle | R_wheel / R_axle | steering wheel, doorknob |
| Wedge | length / width | a moving inclined plane |
| Screw | 2πr / pitch | inclined plane wrapped around cylinder |

#### Mixed Problems
1. A 4 m ramp rises 0.5 m. IMA = 4/0.5 = **8**. To push a 160 N box up, ideal effort = 160/8 = **20 N**.
2. Scissors: **first class** lever (fulcrum between effort and load).
3. A pulley with 5 rope segments has MA = **5**. To lift 500 N: effort = 500/5 = **100 N**.
4. Efficiency of a lever with IMA 6 and AMA 4.5: (4.5/6) × 100 = **75%**.

#### Review Questions
1. What is the IMA of an inclined plane that is 10 m long and 2 m high?
2. A wheelbarrow is what class of lever?
3. How does a screw convert rotational motion into linear force?
