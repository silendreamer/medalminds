#### Key Facts for Bowl
- Newton (N) = kg·m/s²; weight (N) = mass (kg) × g (m/s²)
- 1st Law: equilibrium → zero net force; inertia proportional to mass
- 2nd Law: F_net = ma; rearrange to find any of three variables
- 3rd Law: equal magnitude, opposite direction, different objects — ALWAYS
- g on Earth ≈ 9.8 m/s² (use 10 in bowl); g on Moon ≈ 1.6 m/s²

#### Toss-Up Practice
- "For 10 points, what law states that for every action there is an equal and opposite reaction?" — **Newton's Third Law**
- "For 10 points, a 3 kg object has a net force of 12 N applied to it. What is its acceleration?" — **4 m/s²**
- "For 10 points, what term describes an object's resistance to changes in its state of motion?" — **Inertia**
- "For 10 points, what is the weight, in newtons, of a 50 kg person on Earth (g = 10 m/s²)?" — **500 N**

#### Review Questions
1. In a third-law pair, if one force is 30 N east, what is the other?
2. What is the net force on a 7 kg object accelerating at 3 m/s²?
3. Name the scientist who formulated the three laws of motion.
