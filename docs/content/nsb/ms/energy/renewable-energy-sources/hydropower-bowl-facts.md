#### Can't-Miss Facts
- Hydropower: world's largest renewable electricity source (~16% of global electricity)
- U.S. hydropower: ~6% of electricity, ~38% of renewable electricity
- Largest U.S. hydro plant: Grand Coulee Dam, Washington (6.8 GW)
- World's largest power plant of any type: Three Gorges Dam, China (22.5 GW)
- Pumped storage: ~95% of all U.S. grid-scale energy storage
- Hydropower capacity factor: ~40–42%
- Power equation: P = ρgQh
- Tidal energy: predictable based on Moon's gravity (unlike wind/solar)
- World's largest tidal barrage: La Rance, Brittany, France (240 MW, 1966)
- Klamath River dams: largest dam removal in U.S. history (2023)

#### Bowl Toss-Up Stems
*"For 10 points, what type of hydropower facility uses surplus nighttime electricity to pump water uphill, then generates electricity during peak demand?"* → Pumped-storage hydropower
*"For 10 points, the Three Gorges Dam, the world's largest power plant, is located in what country?"* → China
*"For 10 points, tidal energy is driven primarily by the gravitational pull of what celestial body?"* → The Moon

#### Review Questions
1. What share of U.S. renewable electricity does hydropower provide?
2. What makes pumped-storage hydropower important for the modern electricity grid?
3. Name the world's largest tidal barrage and its country.
