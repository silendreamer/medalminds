#### Can't-Miss Facts
- Geothermal heat sources: radioactive decay in mantle + primordial planetary formation heat
- Geothermal gradient: ~25–30°C per km of depth
- World's largest geothermal facility: the Geysers, Northern California (1,520 MW, dry steam)
- Countries leading in geothermal electricity: Iceland, Kenya, Philippines, New Zealand, United States
- Iceland heating: ~90% of homes heated by geothermal district heating
- Biomass carbon neutral: CO₂ released = CO₂ absorbed during plant growth (if sustainably managed)
- E10: 10% ethanol, 90% gasoline; standard U.S. gasoline blend
- E85: 85% ethanol; used in flex-fuel vehicles
- Biogas: methane produced by anaerobic digestion of organic waste
- Enhanced Geothermal Systems (EGS): inject water into hot dry rock to create artificial geothermal resource

#### Bowl Toss-Up Stems
*"For 10 points, what term describes the increase in temperature with depth below Earth's surface, averaging 25–30°C per kilometer?"* → Geothermal gradient
*"For 10 points, what gasoline blend containing 10% ethanol is the standard fuel sold at most U.S. gas stations?"* → E10 (or 10% ethanol blend)
*"For 10 points, what country meets approximately 66% of its primary energy needs from geothermal energy, which directly heats 90% of its homes?"* → Iceland

#### Review Questions
1. What are the two sources of Earth's geothermal heat?
2. What is biogas, and from what processes is it produced?
3. Name two countries that generate a high fraction of their electricity from geothermal energy.
