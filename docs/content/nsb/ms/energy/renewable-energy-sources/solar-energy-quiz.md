#### Question Set
1. What material is most commonly used in solar panels?
2. A 400-watt solar panel is exposed to 1,000 W/m² of sunlight and has an area of 2 m². What is its efficiency?
3. What does "passive solar" mean?
4. Which DOE lab leads U.S. renewable energy research, and where is it located?
5. What is the advantage of CSP with molten salt storage over standard photovoltaic solar?
6. What has happened to solar panel prices over the past 50 years?
7. What is the solar capacity factor, and how does it compare to nuclear?
8. Name one emerging solar technology beyond standard silicon panels.

#### Answers
1. Silicon
2. 400 W ÷ (1,000 W/m² × 2 m²) = 400 ÷ 2,000 = 20%
3. Using building design (orientation, windows, thermal mass) to heat and cool without active mechanical systems
4. NREL — National Renewable Energy Laboratory, Golden, Colorado
5. CSP with molten salt can store heat and generate electricity hours after sunset; PV needs external batteries for storage
6. Fallen about 99% (from ~$76/W in the 1970s to ~$0.25/W today)
7. ~25%; nuclear is ~93%
8. Perovskite cells, bifacial panels, agrivoltaics, multi-junction concentrator cells
