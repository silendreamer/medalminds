#### Can't-Miss Facts
- Photovoltaic effect: sunlight knocks electrons loose in semiconductor → electricity
- Most common PV material: silicon
- Photovoltaic effect discovered by: Edmond Becquerel (1839)
- Solar capacity factor: ~25% (varies by location and technology)
- Solar constant (outside atmosphere): ~1,361 W/m²
- NREL (National Renewable Energy Laboratory): Golden, Colorado; DOE lab for renewable energy research
- CSP types: parabolic trough, power tower (heliostats), dish/Stirling
- Molten salt storage: allows CSP to generate electricity hours after sunset
- Solar panel efficiency: ~15–23% (standard to premium monocrystalline)
- Fastest growing electricity source globally: solar PV

#### Bowl Toss-Up Stems
*"For 10 points, what DOE national laboratory in Golden, Colorado is the primary U.S. research center for solar and other renewable energy technologies?"* → National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL)
*"For 10 points, what effect, named after its discovery in the 1800s, allows solar panels to convert sunlight directly into electricity?"* → Photovoltaic effect
*"For 10 points, what type of solar thermal system uses a field of mirrors focused on a central receiver tower?"* → Power tower (or central receiver / heliostat field)

#### Review Questions
1. Where is NREL located, and what is its mission?
2. What is the solar constant, and why is it important?
3. Name two types of concentrated solar power designs.
