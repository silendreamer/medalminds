#### Can't-Miss Facts
- U.S. reactors: ~93 operating, ~20% of U.S. electricity, ~54% of U.S. carbon-free electricity
- Most common type: Pressurized Water Reactor (PWR)
- Fuel enrichment: natural U is 0.7% U-235; reactors need 3–5%
- Enrichment method: gas centrifuge (UF₆ spun at high speed)
- Spent fuel: stored in pools (wet) then dry casks; no U.S. permanent repository
- Yucca Mountain, Nevada: proposed permanent repository — not yet operational
- Chernobyl reactor type: RBMK (graphite-moderated, water-cooled, no containment building)
- DOE labs involved in nuclear: Oak Ridge (ORNL), Argonne (ANL), Idaho National Laboratory (INL), Sandia, Lawrence Livermore
- Nuclear lifecycle emissions: ~12 gCO₂eq/kWh (similar to wind)

#### Bowl Toss-Up Stems
*"For 10 points, what site in Nevada was designated as the U.S. permanent nuclear waste repository but has not opened due to political opposition?"* → Yucca Mountain
*"For 10 points, what percentage of U.S. electricity is generated by nuclear power?"* → About 20%
*"For 10 points, what DOE national laboratory in Tennessee is the largest science and energy laboratory in the U.S. and a leader in nuclear research?"* → Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL)

#### Review Questions
1. What share of U.S. carbon-free electricity does nuclear provide?
2. Why has the Yucca Mountain repository never opened?
3. Name three DOE national laboratories involved in nuclear research.
