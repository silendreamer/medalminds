#### Energy Poverty
Households without electricity can't power lights, refrigeration, water pumps, or communication (phones, internet). Work is limited to daylight hours. Education suffers — students can't study after dark. Health is compromised — no refrigeration for vaccines, no power for medical equipment, no clean cooking (indoor air pollution). Energy poverty perpetuates poverty cycles.

#### Clean Cooking and Health
~3 billion people cook with biomass (wood, dung) or coal on open fires. Indoor air pollution from cooking kills ~4 million people annually — more than malaria, tuberculosis, and HIV combined. Chronic exposure causes respiratory disease, heart disease, and cancer. Providing clean cooking (electricity, natural gas, clean biomass stoves) is a major health priority. This requires electricity access and stove distribution.

#### Economic Development and Electricity Correlation
There's a strong correlation between electricity consumption and GDP per capita. Countries with high electricity access have higher HDI (Human Development Index) than those without. Electricity enables economic development — factories need power, services need power, agriculture is mechanized. Expanding electricity access isn't just humanitarian; it's economically efficient.

#### Solar Mini-Grids and Decentralized Solutions
In remote areas, building centralized grid infrastructure is expensive and slow. Solar mini-grids (solar array + battery storage + local distribution) can provide electricity to 50-500 households cheaply and quickly. Cost: $500-1,000 per household connection (vs. $2,000+ for grid extension). Mini-grids are rapidly deployed in East Africa, providing the first electricity access to millions.

#### Leapfrogging Technology
Developing nations can leapfrog intermediate technologies. Rather than building fossil fuel infrastructure (expensive, locked in for decades), countries can deploy renewables directly. Cell phones leapfrogged landlines in Africa. Mobile payment systems leapfrogged banking infrastructure. Similarly, solar mini-grids can leapfrog centralized grids. This allows faster adoption of clean energy and reduces lock-in to fossil fuels.
