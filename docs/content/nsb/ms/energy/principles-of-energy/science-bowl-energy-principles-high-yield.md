#### 5 Practice Toss-Up Stems

1. "For 10 points — this scalar quantity, measured in joules, equals one-half times mass times velocity squared." → **Kinetic energy**

2. "For 10 points — name the three modes of heat transfer, all three required." → **Conduction, convection, and radiation**

3. "For 10 points — this law states that energy cannot be created or destroyed, only converted between forms." → **Law of Conservation of Energy**

4. "For 10 points — what quantity equals the rate of energy use and is measured in watts?" → **Power**

5. "For 10 points — a frictionless roller coaster has maximum potential energy at what location on the track?" → **The highest point**

#### Traps to Avoid
- "Heat" is not the same as "thermal energy" — heat is energy in transit
- Efficiency is ALWAYS less than 100% in real systems
- Watts measure power; joules measure energy — don't swap them

#### Review Questions
1. What is 1 kilowatt-hour in joules?
2. A bowl question asks for the "rate of energy transfer." What unit is the answer in?
3. Name one scenario where convection is the primary heat transfer mechanism.
