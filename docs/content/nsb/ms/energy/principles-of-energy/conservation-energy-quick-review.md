#### Core Principle Summary
Conservation of energy: Energy_initial = Energy_final (in a closed system). For mechanical energy problems: KE_i + PE_i = KE_f + PE_f (if frictionless). With friction: KE_i + PE_i = KE_f + PE_f + W_friction, where W_friction is energy converted to thermal. Always identify what forms exist at start and end before calculating.

#### Formula Flash Cards (Self-Drill)
- KE = ? → ½mv²
- GPE = ? → mgh
- Conservation (frictionless) → KE₁ + PE₁ = KE₂ + PE₂
- Power = ? → Work/time = J/s = W
- Efficiency = ? → (useful output/total input) × 100%
- 1 kWh = ? → 3.6 × 10⁶ J

#### Conceptual Questions (Answer Before Reading)
Q: A ball is dropped. At what fraction of the fall is KE = PE?
A: When height = half the initial height. At h/2: PE = mg(h/2), KE = mg(h/2). Equal.

Q: A pendulum released from 30 cm above the bottom swings. To what height does it rise on the other side (frictionless)?
A: Exactly 30 cm — total mechanical energy is conserved.

Q: Engine A is 30% efficient; Engine B is 60% efficient. Both use 1000 J of fuel. How much more useful work does B produce?
A: A produces 300 J, B produces 600 J. B produces 300 J more.
