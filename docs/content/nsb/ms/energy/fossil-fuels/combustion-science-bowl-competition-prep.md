#### Top Combustion Clue Words

- "Hydrocarbon + oxygen → CO₂ + H₂O" → **Combustion** (or "complete combustion")
- "Forms when insufficient oxygen present" → **Incomplete combustion** / **carbon monoxide**
- "Binds hemoglobin 200× more than oxygen" → **Carbon monoxide (CO)**
- "High-temperature combustion + nitrogen" → **Nitrogen oxides (NOₓ)**
- "Sulfur + oxygen in coal burning" → **Sulfur dioxide (SO₂)**
- "Acid rain precursor" → **SO₂ or NOₓ**
- "Fine particles less than 2.5 micrometers" → **PM₂.₅**
- "Secondary pollutant, forms from NOₓ + VOCs + sunlight" → **Ground-level ozone**
- "EPA's six regulated air pollutants" → Criteria air pollutants
- "Catalytic converter + CO/NOₓ/hydrocarbons" → produces CO₂, H₂O, N₂

#### 5 Practice Toss-Up Stems

1. "For 10 points — this colorless, odorless gas is produced by incomplete combustion of hydrocarbons and is toxic because it binds to hemoglobin about 200 times more readily than oxygen." → **Carbon monoxide (CO)**

2. "For 10 points — write the balanced equation for the complete combustion of methane (CH₄)." → **CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O**

3. "For 10 points — this gaseous compound, produced when sulfur impurities in coal react with oxygen during combustion, is the primary precursor of acid rain." → **Sulfur dioxide (SO₂)**

4. "For 10 points — this 1990 amendment to the Clean Air Act established a cap-and-trade system that dramatically reduced SO₂ emissions from coal power plants." → **Acid Rain Program** (or the Clean Air Act Amendments of 1990)

5. "For 10 points — ground-level ozone, a secondary air pollutant, forms when nitrogen oxides react with what class of compounds in the presence of sunlight?" → **Volatile organic compounds (VOCs)**

#### Review Questions
1. What makes carbon monoxide (CO) particularly dangerous to human health?
2. Write the formula for methane and the balanced complete combustion reaction.
3. Is ground-level ozone a primary or secondary pollutant? Explain why.
