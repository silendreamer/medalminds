#### Greenhouse Gas Quick Reference
| Gas | Source | GWP (20-yr vs CO₂) | Atmospheric lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| CO₂ | Combustion, cement | 1 (baseline) | 100s of years |
| CH₄ | Natural gas leaks, livestock | ~80 | ~12 years |
| N₂O | Agriculture, combustion | ~270 | ~114 years |
| HFCs | Refrigerants | 100s to 1000s | Decades |

GWP = Global Warming Potential (how much warming per unit mass vs. CO₂)

#### Climate Policy Timeline
- **1988**: IPCC established (UN scientific body, assesses climate science)
- **1997**: Kyoto Protocol (first binding international emissions treaty — U.S. did not ratify)
- **2009**: Copenhagen Accord (non-binding emissions pledges)
- **2015**: Paris Agreement (limit warming to 1.5–2°C; 195+ countries signed)
- **2021**: Glasgow Climate Pact (COP26; accelerated coal phase-down)
- **2022**: U.S. Inflation Reduction Act (~$369B in clean energy tax credits — largest U.S. climate investment)

#### 10 Environmental Impact Facts to Know
1. Pre-industrial CO₂: ~280 ppm → Current (2023): ~420 ppm
2. CO₂ is primary long-term greenhouse gas from fossil fuels
3. CH₄ is ~80× more potent than CO₂ over 20 years (but shorter-lived)
4. 1.2°C warming above pre-industrial as of 2023
5. Ice cores show CO₂ correlation with temperature over 800,000 years
6. Ocean acidification: CO₂ + H₂O → carbonic acid (H₂CO₃)
7. CCS can capture 90% of CO₂ from point sources
8. DAC cost target: below $100/ton CO₂ (DOE Carbon Negative Shot)
9. Paris Agreement: limit warming to well below 2°C, pursue 1.5°C
10. IPCC reports synthesize global climate science for policymakers
