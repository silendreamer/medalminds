#### 15 Highest-Yield Coal Facts

| Clue Pattern | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Formed during the Carboniferous" | Coal (or coal deposits) |
| "Highest carbon content coal" | Anthracite |
| "Most abundant U.S. coal type" | Bituminous |
| "Partially decomposed plant matter, first stage" | Peat |
| "Carbon intermediate for steelmaking" | Coke |
| "Primary greenhouse gas from coal combustion" | Carbon dioxide (CO₂) |
| "Acid rain precursor from coal" | Sulfur dioxide (SO₂) |
| "Removes SO₂ using limestone" | Scrubber (or flue gas desulfurization) |
| "Captures fly ash electrostatically" | Electrostatic precipitator |
| "Mercury control in coal plants" | Activated carbon injection |
| "Acid mine drainage cause" | Pyrite oxidation |
| "2008 Tennessee coal ash disaster" | Kingston Fossil Plant |
| "DOE coal research office" | Office of Fossil Energy and Carbon Management |
| "Coal's approximate efficiency in U.S. power plants" | ~35% |
| "World's largest coal reserves" | United States |

#### 4 Practice Toss-Up Stems

1. "For 10 points — this hardest grade of coal, with a carbon content exceeding 86 percent, burns very cleanly and is found primarily in Pennsylvania." → **Anthracite**

2. "For 10 points — this product is made by heating bituminous coal without oxygen and is essential as a reducing agent in the production of steel." → **Coke**

3. "For 10 points — this gaseous compound, produced when sulfur in coal reacts with oxygen during combustion, is the primary cause of acid rain." → **Sulfur dioxide (SO₂)**

4. "For 10 points — this geologic period, lasting from approximately 359 to 299 million years ago, is named for the vast coal deposits formed from its dense swamp forests." → **Carboniferous period**

#### Review Questions
1. What is the carbon content range of anthracite coal?
2. Name the DOE office that funds carbon capture and fossil fuel research.
3. During a toss-up, you hear "removed from flue gas using limestone." What do you answer?
