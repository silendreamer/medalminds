#### High-Yield Climate and Pollution Facts for Toss-Ups

| Clue | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Primary greenhouse gas from fossil fuels" | Carbon dioxide (CO₂) |
| "GWP of ~80 over 20 years" | Methane (CH₄) |
| "Scientific body assessing global climate" | IPCC |
| "2015 agreement, limit warming to 1.5–2°C" | Paris Agreement |
| "CO₂ concentration before industrialization" | ~280 ppm |
| "Current CO₂ concentration (~2023)" | ~420 ppm (or "over 400 ppm") |
| "CO₂ in water forms this acid" | Carbonic acid (H₂CO₃) |
| "DOE office for fossil energy research" | Office of Fossil Energy and Carbon Management |
| "Removes CO₂ from air directly" | Direct Air Capture (DAC) |
| "DOE cost target for DAC" | Below $100 per ton CO₂ (Carbon Negative Shot) |
| "2022 U.S. climate investment law" | Inflation Reduction Act |

#### 5 Practice Toss-Up Stems

1. "For 10 points — this international agreement signed in 2015 by more than 190 countries set a goal of limiting global warming to 1.5 to 2 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels." → **Paris Agreement**

2. "For 10 points — this technology captures carbon dioxide directly from the atmosphere, rather than at a point source, and is a focus of the DOE's Carbon Negative Shot initiative." → **Direct Air Capture (DAC)**

3. "For 10 points — name the United Nations scientific body, established in 1988, that synthesizes climate research and issues assessment reports for policymakers." → **IPCC (Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change)**

4. "For 10 points — methane's global warming potential over a 20-year timeframe is approximately how many times greater than carbon dioxide?" → **80 times (approximately)**

5. "For 10 points — this chemical reaction, in which carbon dioxide dissolves in seawater to form carbonic acid, is lowering the pH of the world's oceans." → **Ocean acidification**

#### Review Questions
1. What is the IPCC, when was it established, and what is its function?
2. A bowl question asks about "the primary greenhouse gas from burning fossil fuels" — what do you answer?
3. What is the DOE's Carbon Negative Shot, and what cost target does it set?
