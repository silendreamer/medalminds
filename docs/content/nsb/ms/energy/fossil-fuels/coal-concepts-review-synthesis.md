#### 20 Essential Coal Facts
1. Coal forms from compressed, transformed plant matter (fossil fuel)
2. Most coal formed during the Carboniferous period (~359–299 Ma)
3. Coal formation: peat → lignite → bituminous → anthracite
4. Anthracite: highest carbon content (~97%), most energy-dense
5. Bituminous coal: most common type used in U.S. electricity generation
6. Coking coal → coke → used in steelmaking
7. Surface mining used for shallow deposits; underground for deep seams
8. Coal produces ~35% efficiency in typical U.S. power plants
9. C + O₂ → CO₂ (combustion, primary GHG from coal)
10. S + O₂ → SO₂ (sulfur dioxide → acid rain)
11. Scrubbers remove SO₂ using limestone (CaCO₃)
12. Electrostatic precipitators capture fly ash
13. Mercury bioaccumulates in fish through coal emissions
14. Acid mine drainage: pyrite + air + water → sulfuric acid
15. Kingston spill (2008) → led to first federal coal ash rules (2014)
16. U.S. has world's largest proven coal reserves
17. Coal generated ~17% of U.S. electricity in 2023 (down from ~50% in 2008)
18. Shale gas revolution and renewable cost declines drove coal's decline
19. DOE Office of Fossil Energy and Carbon Management oversees coal R&D
20. 1 ton bituminous coal ≈ 25 million BTU of thermal energy
