#### Triangle Area and Heron's Formula
Triangle area = ½ × base × height. If base = 5 and height = 8, area = 20. If sides are known but not height, use Heron's formula: Area = √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)] where s = (a+b+c)/2 (semi-perimeter). Example: sides 3, 4, 5 give s = 6, Area = √[6(3)(2)(1)] = √36 = 6.

#### Coordinate Geometry
Given vertices of a polygon, area can be computed using the shoelace formula: Area = ½|x₁(y₂-y_n) + x₂(y₃-y₁) + ... + x_n(y₁-y_(n-1))|. For a triangle with vertices (0,0), (4,0), (2,3): Area = ½|0(0-3) + 4(3-0) + 2(0-0)| = ½|12| = 6.

#### Optimization: Maximum Area
Given a perimeter, maximize area. For a rectangle: P = 2L + 2W = constant. Area = L × W. To maximize, set L = W (square). A square with perimeter 20 has sides 5, area 25. Any other rectangle with perimeter 20 has less area. Calculus shows this: dA/dL = P/2 - 2L = 0 when L = P/4.

#### Pythagorean Theorem Applications
In a right triangle with legs a, b and hypotenuse c: a² + b² = c². Example: 3-4-5 triangle (3² + 4² = 5²). This finds missing sides and distances. In coordinate geometry, distance from (0,0) to (3,4) is √(3² + 4²) = 5.

#### 3D Geometry
Volume of rectangular prism: V = L × W × H. Surface area: SA = 2(LW + LH + WH). For a cube with side s: V = s³, SA = 6s². Sphere with radius r: V = 4πr³/3, SA = 4πr². These formulas generalize 2D area concepts to 3D.

#### Review Questions
1. What is the area of a triangle with base 10 and height 6?
2. Use Heron's formula to find the area of a triangle with sides 5, 6, 7.
3. What rectangle maximizes area for a given perimeter?
