#### Dividing by 5
Any number ÷ 5 = (number × 2) ÷ 10. So 340 ÷ 5 = 680 ÷ 10 = 68. Or 87 ÷ 5 = 174 ÷ 10 = 17.4. This converts division by 5 into multiplication by 2 and division by 10 (easy).

#### Dividing by 25 and 125
Since 25 = 100/4 and 125 = 1000/8: 400 ÷ 25 = (400 × 4) ÷ 100 = 1,600 ÷ 100 = 16. And 1,000 ÷ 125 = (1,000 × 8) ÷ 1,000 = 8. These conversions use the reciprocal relationship.

#### Canceling in Fractions
Before multiplying fractions, cancel common factors. (24/35) × (35/48): cancel 24 with 48 (divide both by 24) and 35 with 35 (both become 1). Result: 1/2. Without canceling: (24 × 35) ÷ (35 × 48) = 840/1,680 = 1/2 (more steps). Canceling is much faster.

#### Fractions and Percentages
10% = 1/10, 25% = 1/4, 50% = 1/2, 20% = 1/5. Converting fractions to percentages: 3/8 = 0.375 = 37.5%. These conversions help estimate quickly. What is 25% of 60? It's 1/4 of 60 = 15. What is 37.5% of 80? It's 3/8 of 80 = 30.
