#### Identifying Variables and Unknowns
Problem: "Sarah is 3 years older than twice Tom's age. If Sarah is 25, how old is Tom?" Let T = Tom's age, S = Sarah's age. Given: S = 2T + 3 and S = 25. Unknown: T. Solve: 25 = 2T + 3, so 2T = 22, T = 11.

#### Units and Conversions
Problem: "A car travels 60 miles per hour for 2.5 hours. How far does it travel?" Distance = rate × time = 60 mph × 2.5 h = 150 miles. Pay attention to units — they must be compatible. If rate is in m/s and time in hours, convert first.

#### Setting Up Equations from Descriptions
Problem: "A rectangle's width is 4 less than its length. Perimeter is 56. Find dimensions." Let L = length, W = width. Given: W = L - 4 and 2L + 2W = 56. Substitute: 2L + 2(L - 4) = 56, so 4L - 8 = 56, 4L = 64, L = 16. Then W = 12.

#### Multiple-Step Problems
Problem: "You buy 3 items at $5 each, a 10% discount is applied, and you pay 8% tax. What's your final cost?" Start: 3 × $5 = $15. After discount: $15 × 0.9 = $13.50. After tax: $13.50 × 1.08 = $14.58. Multiple steps require careful tracking.

#### Checking Reasonableness
After solving, substitute back into the original problem. If Tom is 11 and Sarah is 25, check: is 25 = 2(11) + 3? Yes, 25 = 22 + 3 ✓. If you got Tom = 15, check: is 25 = 2(15) + 3? No, 25 ≠ 33. This catches errors.

#### Review Questions
1. Translate: "The sum of two numbers is 20, and their difference is 4."
2. How would you solve that system of equations?
3. What's the rectangle's length if width = length - 4 and perimeter = 56?
