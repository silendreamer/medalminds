#### Formula Recall Check
Quickly state: Distance formula = ___. Midpoint formula = ___. Slope formula = ___. Slope-intercept form = ___. Perpendicular slopes have product = ___. Answers: √[(Δx)²+(Δy)²], ((x₁+x₂)/2,(y₁+y₂)/2), Δy/Δx, y=mx+b, −1.

#### Mixed Problem Set
1. Line 1: y = 3x + 5. Line 2: y = 3x − 2. Are these parallel, perpendicular, or neither? *Same slope (3), different y-intercepts → **parallel***.
2. Find the distance from the origin to point (5, 12). *√(25+144) = √169 = **13***.
3. The vertices of a square are at (0,0), (4,0), (4,4), (0,4). Find the length of a diagonal. *Diagonal = √(4²+4²) = √32 = 4√2 ≈ 5.66*.
4. A segment has midpoint (2,−1) and one endpoint (−1, 3). Find the other endpoint. *(2+x₂)/2 = 2 → x₂=5. (3+y₂)/2 = −1 → y₂=−5. Answer: **(5,−5)***.

#### Graph Interpretation
From a graph, you see a line passing through (0, −4) and (3, 2). Find: (a) slope = (2−(−4))/(3−0) = 6/3 = **2**. (b) equation: y = **2x − 4**. (c) x-intercept: 0 = 2x−4 → x = **2**, point (2,0). These three pieces of information are always recoverable from any two points on the line.

#### Review Questions
1. Find the perimeter of a triangle with vertices A(0,0), B(4,0), C(0,3).
2. A line has slope 2 and passes through (3, 5). Write its equation in slope-intercept form.
3. What is the distance between (−2, 3) and (4, −5)?
