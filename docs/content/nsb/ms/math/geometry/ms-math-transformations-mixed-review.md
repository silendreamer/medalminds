#### Transformation Quick Reference
Translation: (x+a, y+b) — slides, no change in size/orientation. Reflection over x-axis: (x,−y). Over y-axis: (−x,y). Over y=x: (y,x). Rotation 90° CCW: (−y,x). 180°: (−x,−y). 270° CCW: (y,−x). Dilation by k from origin: (kx,ky). Rigid (size preserved): translation, reflection, rotation. Non-rigid: dilation.

#### Mixed Problems
1. Triangle with vertices (1,2), (3,2), (2,5). Translated by (−3, 1). Find new vertices. *Subtract 3 from each x, add 1 to each y: **(−2,3), (0,3), (−1,6)***.
2. Point P(4, −3). Reflect over x-axis, then reflect over y-axis. Final location? *After x-axis reflection: (4,3). After y-axis reflection: **(−4,3)**. [Same as 180° rotation!]*
3. A dilation centered at origin with scale factor 2.5 maps (4,6) to ___. *(10, 15)*.
4. Which transformation would map △ABC onto △A'B'C' if all angles are preserved and sizes are identical? *Any rigid motion — translation, reflection, or rotation (or a composition).*

#### Identifying Transformations
Pre-image vertex A(2,5); image A'(−5,2). What transformation occurred? Test: reflection over y=x: (x,y)→(y,x). Applying: (2,5)→(5,2) ✗. Rotation 90° CCW: (x,y)→(−y,x). Applying: (2,5)→(−5,2) ✓. **Rotation 90° counterclockwise** about the origin.

#### Review Questions
1. A figure is reflected over the line y = x. If a vertex was at (3, −7), where is its image?
2. A square has vertices at (0,0), (2,0), (2,2), (0,2). After a 180° rotation about the origin, what are the new vertices?
3. True or false: a dilation always produces a figure congruent to the original.
