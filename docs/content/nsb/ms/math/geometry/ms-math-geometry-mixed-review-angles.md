#### Quick Recall: Essential Formulas
Run through these before timed practice: Interior angle sum of n-gon = (n−2)×180°. Each angle of regular n-gon = (n−2)×180°/n. Exterior angle of regular n-gon = 360°/n. Triangle angle sum = 180°. Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c² (right triangles only). Supplementary angles sum to 180°; complementary to 90°. Alternate interior angles (parallel lines) are equal.

#### Mixed Problem Set A
1. A regular polygon has interior angles of 140°. How many sides does it have? *Solution:* (n−2)×180/n = 140 → 180n−360 = 140n → 40n = 360 → n = **9 sides**.
2. A right triangle has a hypotenuse of 13 and one leg of 5. Find the other leg. *Solution:* 5² + b² = 13² → 25 + b² = 169 → b² = 144 → b = **12**.
3. Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. A co-interior angle pair measures (5x + 15)° and (3x + 5)°. Find x. *Solution:* Co-interior angles are supplementary: 5x+15+3x+5 = 180 → 8x+20 = 180 → x = **20**.

#### Mixed Problem Set B
1. A rhombus has one angle of 65°. Find all four angles. *Solution:* Opposite angles are equal; consecutive angles are supplementary. Angles: 65°, 115°, 65°, 115°.
2. In △ABC, ∠A = 3x, ∠B = 2x, ∠C = x. Find all three angles. *Solution:* 6x = 180 → x = 30. Angles: 90°, 60°, 30°. This is a right triangle!
3. How many diagonals does a hexagon have? *Solution:* n(n−3)/2 = 6(3)/2 = **9 diagonals**.

#### Common Mistakes to Avoid
(1) Using the Pythagorean theorem on non-right triangles — always confirm there's a right angle first. (2) Confusing interior and exterior angles. (3) Forgetting that "regular" means BOTH all sides equal AND all angles equal. (4) Applying parallel-line rules to non-parallel lines — check that the lines are explicitly stated as parallel.

#### Timed Practice Strategy
For Science Bowl geometry questions: first, identify what type of angle or polygon problem it is (takes 2–3 seconds). Then recall the relevant formula or relationship. Set up the equation. Solve. In toss-up format, you must interrupt when confident — practice recognizing key clue words: "regular polygon," "parallel lines," "right triangle," "exterior angle."

#### Review Questions
1. What is the exterior angle of a regular pentagon?
2. A right triangle has legs in the ratio 3:4. If the hypotenuse is 20, find both legs.
3. Two angles are supplementary. The larger is 4 times the smaller. Find both angles.
