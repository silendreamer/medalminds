#### The 5 Most Common Bowl Genetics Problem Patterns

**Pattern 1:** "Two heterozygotes crossed — what fraction recessive?" → Always 1/4
**Pattern 2:** "Heterozygote × recessive crossed — what fraction recessive?" → Always 1/2
**Pattern 3:** "What blood type is genotype IᴬIᴮ?" → AB (codominant)
**Pattern 4:** "Dihybrid AaBb × AaBb — phenotype ratio?" → 9:3:3:1
**Pattern 5:** "What is the probability of getting aa from Aa × aa?" → 1/2

Recognize the pattern before picking up pencil. These 5 cover 70% of bowl genetics questions.

#### Speed Techniques
- For dihybrid probability: multiply monohybrid fractions (don't draw 4×4 grid)
- For ratios: memorize 3:1 (monohybrid heterozygous cross), 1:1 (heterozygous × recessive), 9:3:3:1 (dihybrid)
- For X-linked: write X alleles explicitly (Xᴴ, X) to avoid confusion

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems

1. "Two pea plants, both Tt, are crossed. What fraction of offspring will be homozygous recessive?" → **1/4**
2. "A cross between a heterozygous individual and a homozygous recessive produces half dominant and half recessive offspring. Name this cross type." → **Testcross**
3. "In a dihybrid cross AaBb × AaBb, what fraction of offspring are expected to show dominant phenotypes for both traits?" → **9/16**
4. "The ABO blood type system, where genotype IᴬIᴮ produces both A and B antigens, is an example of what inheritance pattern?" → **Codominance**
5. "This cross type is used to determine the unknown genotype of a dominant-phenotype organism." → **Testcross**

#### Review Questions
1. Solve quickly: What fraction of offspring from Aa × Aa will be Aa (heterozygous)?
2. What phenotype ratio results from a monohybrid cross between two heterozygotes?
3. From AaBb × AaBb, what is the probability of offspring with genotype aabb?
