#### Biome Identification Challenge
Match each description to the correct biome: (1) Largest terrestrial biome, dominated by conifers, long cold winters; (2) Near equator, >200 cm rain/year, highest biodiversity; (3) <25 cm precipitation/year, extreme temperature swings, cacti and succulents; (4) Short growing season, permafrost, no trees, mosses and lichens; (5) Seasonal rainfall, grasses with scattered trees, large mammal migrations.

Answers: (1) Taiga; (2) Tropical rainforest; (3) Desert; (4) Tundra; (5) Savanna.

#### Climate → Biome
If you know: temperature = −15°C in winter, 10°C in summer; precipitation = 20 cm/year, mostly as snow → what biome? If you know: temperature = 20°C year-round; precipitation = 50 cm in summer, 5 cm in winter → what biome?

#### Permafrost Alert
Why is permafrost thawing a concern for climate scientists? How does this create a positive feedback loop? Which biome is most affected?

#### Review Questions
1. Rank these biomes from least to most annual precipitation: tundra, tropical rainforest, temperate grassland, desert.
2. What is the rain shadow effect, and how does it affect biome distribution?
3. Why is the tropical rainforest the most biodiverse biome despite covering only 6% of Earth's land?
