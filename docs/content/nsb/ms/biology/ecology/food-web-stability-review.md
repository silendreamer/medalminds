#### Scenario Analysis
A fishery harvests large quantities of anchovies for fish meal (used in agriculture and aquaculture). Anchovies eat phytoplankton and are eaten by tuna, dolphins, seabirds, and large fish. Predict the ecological consequences of drastically reducing anchovy populations. Use food web, keystone species, and energy pyramid concepts in your answer.

#### Energy Pyramid Problem
Draw a simple energy pyramid for a forest ecosystem: oak trees → caterpillars → warblers → hawks. If oaks have 800,000 kcal: (a) How much energy do caterpillars have? (b) How much do warblers have? (c) How much do hawks have? (d) Why can't this forest support many more trophic levels?

#### Keystone Species Removal
List the cascading effects that would likely follow the removal of wolves from a temperate forest where wolves eat deer, deer eat seedlings of trees, trees provide habitat for songbirds, and songbirds control insect populations.

#### Review Questions
1. Why does removing one species from a food web often have broader consequences than expected?
2. Using the 10% rule, explain why there are always more herbivores than carnivores in an ecosystem.
3. What is a trophic cascade? Describe one real-world example.
