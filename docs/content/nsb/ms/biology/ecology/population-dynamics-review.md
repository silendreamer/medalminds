#### Growth Curve Classification
Classify each scenario as producing J-curve (exponential) or S-curve (logistic) growth, and explain: (1) A bacterium culture in a petri dish with unlimited nutrients for the first 6 hours; (2) A deer population on an island with limited food and some predators; (3) A bacterium culture approaching the petri dish's maximum carrying capacity.

#### Limiting Factor Analysis
A rabbit population has grown to 10× its normal density. List three density-dependent limiting factors that would increase as a result. How would each factor tend to reduce the population back toward carrying capacity?

#### Predator-Prey Reasoning
Lynx populations in the Canadian boreal forest peaked in 1990, 1999, and 2009. Snowshoe hare populations peaked in about 1987, 1996, and 2006. (a) What is the approximate cycle length? (b) Why do hare peaks precede lynx peaks? (c) What would you predict for the lynx population in 2010 (the year after the 2009 peak)?

#### Review Questions
1. What is carrying capacity, and what happens to population growth as a population approaches it?
2. What is the difference between density-dependent and density-independent limiting factors? Give an example of each.
3. Explain why exponential growth cannot be sustained indefinitely.
