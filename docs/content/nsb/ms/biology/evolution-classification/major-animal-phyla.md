#### Invertebrate and Vertebrate Phyla

Animals are organized into large groups called **phyla**. The biggest division separates **vertebrates** (animals with backbones, like fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds, and mammals) from **invertebrates** (animals without backbones). Invertebrates make up the vast majority of animal species on Earth. Some major invertebrate phyla include Porifera (sponges), Cnidaria (jellyfish and corals), Platyhelminthes (flatworms), Nematoda (roundworms), Annelida (segmented worms), Arthropoda (insects, spiders, crustaceans), Mollusca (snails, squids, clams), and Echinodermata (starfish, sea urchins).

#### Body Symmetry and Organization

**Body symmetry** is a key way to classify animals. **Radial symmetry** means the body parts are arranged like spokes on a wheel around a central point—you see this in jellyfish and sea anemones. **Bilateral symmetry** means the body has a left and right side that mirror each other—this is how most complex animals, including humans and insects, are organized. **Asymmetrical** animals, like sponges, have no clear symmetry at all. Symmetry often reflects how an animal moves and feeds.

#### Arthropod Characteristics

**Arthropods** are the most numerous animal phylum and include insects, spiders, scorpions, crustaceans (crabs, lobsters, shrimp), and centipedes. They share several **characteristic traits**: a hard outer skeleton called an **exoskeleton**, segmented bodies, and jointed legs. Insects have six legs, spiders have eight, and crustaceans typically have ten or more. The exoskeleton protects the animal and provides attachment points for muscles.

#### Mollusks and Other Key Phyla

**Mollusks** include snails, slugs, squids, and clams. Many mollusks have a soft body protected by a hard shell, and some have a muscular foot for movement. **Annelids** (segmented worms like earthworms) have bodies divided into repeating ring-like segments, which lets them move with flexibility. **Echinoderms** like starfish have a water-filled system inside their bodies that helps them move and feed.
