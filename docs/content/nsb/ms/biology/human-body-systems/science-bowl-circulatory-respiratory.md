#### Most-Tested Terms with Clue Words

| Term | Clue words in stem |
|---|---|
| Alveoli | "gas exchange," "air sacs," "lungs," "tiny," "oxygen into blood" |
| Hemoglobin | "oxygen transport," "red blood cells," "iron," "protein" |
| Diaphragm | "breathing," "inhalation," "dome-shaped muscle," "below lungs" |
| Capillaries | "exchange," "one cell thick," "smallest blood vessels" |
| Pulmonary artery | "deoxygenated," "from heart to lungs," "right ventricle" |

| Surfactant | "prevents alveoli collapse," "surface tension," "premature babies" |

#### Top 5 Trap Questions
1. "Pulmonary arteries carry which type of blood?" → Trap: most arteries carry oxygenated — but pulmonary artery carries **deoxygenated**
2. "Veins carry blood toward or away from the heart?" → Toward (not always deoxygenated — pulmonary veins carry O₂-rich blood)
3. "Does gas exchange require active transport or diffusion?" → **Diffusion** (passive, no ATP)
4. "Does the diaphragm contract or relax during inhalation?" → **Contracts** (students often guess relax)
5. "Where is blood pressure highest?" → **Arteries** (not veins)

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "This condition involves the buildup of fatty plaques inside artery walls." → **Atherosclerosis**
2. "Consisting of ~55% of blood volume, this straw-colored liquid carries proteins, nutrients, and hormones." → **Plasma**
3. "This lung disease, commonly caused by smoking, destroys alveoli and reduces gas exchange surface area." → **Emphysema**
4. "These one-way structures in veins prevent blood from flowing backward." → **Valves**
5. "This hormone, released by the kidneys in response to low oxygen, stimulates red blood cell production." → **Erythropoietin (EPO)**

#### Review Questions
1. Why are capillaries ideal for gas and nutrient exchange (two reasons)?
2. What happens to blood pressure during systole vs. diastole?
3. Name one way the body responds to chronic low oxygen levels (such as at high altitude).
