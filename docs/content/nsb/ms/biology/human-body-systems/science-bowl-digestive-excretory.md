#### Top 15 Facts

1. Main site of digestion AND absorption = **small intestine**
2. Site of water reabsorption = **large intestine (colon)**
3. Enzyme that digests starch in mouth = **salivary amylase**
4. Enzyme that digests protein in stomach = **pepsin** (active only in acid)
5. **Bile** emulsifies fats; produced by **liver**, stored in **gallbladder**
6. Pancreas secretes **amylase, lipase, protease, and bicarbonate**
7. Villi increase surface area of **small intestine** for absorption
8. Nephron = functional unit of kidney
9. Kidney hormone = **erythropoietin (EPO)** → stimulates RBC production
10. **ADH** = more water reabsorption → concentrated urine
11. Glucose in urine = sign of **diabetes** (or failure of tubule reabsorption)
12. **Urea** = main nitrogenous waste; formed from amino acid metabolism
13. Cirrhosis = liver scarring; causes **jaundice**
14. **Peptic ulcers** caused primarily by H. pylori bacteria
15. Dialysis replaces kidney filtration when kidneys fail

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "These finger-like projections lining the small intestine increase surface area for nutrient absorption." → **Villi**
2. "Produced by the liver and stored in the gallbladder, this substance emulsifies dietary fats." → **Bile**
3. "This hormone, released by the posterior pituitary, signals the kidneys to reabsorb more water." → **Antidiuretic hormone (ADH)**
4. "Name the functional filtration unit of the kidney." → **Nephron**
5. "This nitrogenous waste, formed from protein metabolism, is the primary solute in urine." → **Urea**

#### Review Questions
1. In which organ does the majority of food absorption occur?
2. What is the role of the pancreas in both digestion and blood sugar regulation?
3. What does it mean when urine tests positive for protein?
