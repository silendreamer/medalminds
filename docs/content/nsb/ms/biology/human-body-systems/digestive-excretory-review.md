#### Must-Know Organ → Function Pairs
- Mouth: teeth (mechanical), salivary amylase (starch digestion)
- Stomach: HCl (kills bacteria, activates pepsin), pepsin (protein digestion)
- Small intestine: main site of digestion AND absorption, villi increase surface area
- Liver: produces bile; detoxifies; produces clotting factors
- Gallbladder: stores bile
- Pancreas: produces amylase, protease, lipase; neutralizes acid with bicarbonate
- Large intestine: water reabsorption; houses gut bacteria
- Kidneys: filter blood, produce urine, regulate blood pressure and fluid balance
- Nephron: functional unit; filtration → reabsorption → secretion → excretion

#### High-Yield Enzyme Table
| Enzyme | Made by | Substrate |
|---|---|---|
| Salivary amylase | Salivary glands | Starch → maltose |
| Pepsin | Stomach (chief cells) | Proteins → peptides |
| Lipase | Pancreas | Fats → fatty acids + glycerol |
| Pancreatic amylase | Pancreas | Starch → maltose |
| Protease (trypsin, etc.) | Pancreas | Proteins → amino acids |

#### Hormone-Function Pairs
- ADH (antidiuretic hormone): more water reabsorption in kidneys
- Insulin: lowers blood glucose; promotes glucose uptake by cells
- Glucagon: raises blood glucose; promotes glycogen breakdown
- Erythropoietin (EPO): stimulates RBC production (from kidneys)
