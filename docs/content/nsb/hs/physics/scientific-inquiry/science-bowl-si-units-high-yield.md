#### Top Bowl Facts
1. **Meter (m)** = SI unit of length.
2. **Kilogram (kg)** = SI unit of mass (NOT gram).
3. **Second (s)** = SI unit of time.
4. **Newton (N)** = kg·m/s² = SI unit of force.
5. **Joule (J)** = kg·m²/s² = N·m = SI unit of energy.
6. **Watt (W)** = J/s = SI unit of power.
7. **Pascal (Pa)** = N/m² = SI unit of pressure.
8. **Hertz (Hz)** = 1/s = SI unit of frequency.
9. **Coulomb (C)** = A·s = SI unit of electric charge.
10. **kilo = 10³, mega = 10⁶, giga = 10⁹, milli = 10⁻³, micro = 10⁻⁶, nano = 10⁻⁹**.
11. **Significant figures rule**: multiplication → use fewest sig figs.
12. **Dimensional analysis** = multiply by conversion factors = 1.
13. **7 SI base units**: m, kg, s, A, K, mol, cd.
14. The year ≈ **π × 10⁷ seconds** (3.15 × 10⁷ s).
15. **1 eV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J** (electron-volt to joule conversion).
