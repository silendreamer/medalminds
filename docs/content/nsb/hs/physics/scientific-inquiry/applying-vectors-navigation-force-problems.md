#### Navigation Problem: River Crossing
A boat travels at 4.0 m/s east relative to water. The river current is 3.0 m/s south. What is the boat's velocity relative to ground? Vₓ = 4.0 m/s, Vᵧ = −3.0 m/s. |V| = √(4² + 3²) = 5.0 m/s. Angle = arctan(3/4) = 36.9° south of east. If the river is 100 m wide, crossing time = 100/4 = 25 s. Drift south = 3.0 × 25 = 75 m.

#### Multi-Force Problem: Block on Incline
A 10 kg block sits on a 30° frictionless incline. Find the acceleration. Decompose gravity along and perpendicular to the slope: Component along slope = mg sin(30°) = 10 × 9.8 × 0.5 = 49 N (down slope). Perpendicular component = mg cos(30°) = 84.9 N (into slope, balanced by normal). Net force = 49 N → a = 49/10 = 4.9 m/s².

#### Three-Force Equilibrium
Three forces act on a point: **F₁** = 100 N east, **F₂** = 80 N north, **F₃** = ? (equilibrium). For equilibrium, **F₃** = −(**F₁** + **F₂**) = −(100î + 80ĵ) = −100î − 80ĵ N. Magnitude = √(100² + 80²) = 128 N, direction = 38.7° south of west.

#### Review Questions
1. An airplane heads north at 200 m/s but a crosswind pushes it east at 30 m/s. What is the actual velocity (magnitude and direction)?
2. A 5 kg block is on a 45° frictionless incline. What is the acceleration down the slope?
3. Two forces of 60 N and 80 N act at right angles on an object. What single force would produce equilibrium?
