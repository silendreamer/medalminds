#### Top Bowl Facts
1. Slope of position-time graph = velocity.
2. Slope of velocity-time graph = acceleration.
3. Area under velocity-time graph = displacement.
4. Area under force-displacement graph = work done.
5. Linearize T vs. L by plotting T² vs. L (pendulum).
6. Best-fit line minimizes sum of squared residuals.
7. Random residuals → correct model; systematic residuals → wrong model.
8. Standard error = σ/√n (halved by 4× more data).
9. Error bars represent ± measurement uncertainty.
10. Add % uncertainties when multiplying/dividing quantities.

#### Practice Toss-Ups
1. "For 10 points — on a velocity-time graph, what physical quantity does the area under the curve represent?" *(Displacement)*
2. "For 10 points — what is the slope of a position-time graph equal to?" *(Velocity)*
3. "For 10 points — to linearize a relationship of the form T equals 2π times the square root of L over g, you should plot T-squared versus what?" *(Length L)*

#### Review Questions
1. A P-t graph has a constant positive slope. What is the object doing?
2. The area under an F-t graph represents what quantity?
3. How can you tell from a residual plot that your model is incorrect?
