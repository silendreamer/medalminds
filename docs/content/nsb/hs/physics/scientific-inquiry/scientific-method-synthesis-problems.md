#### Problem 1: Design a Hooke's Law Experiment
You want to verify that spring force is proportional to extension (F = kx). Write: (a) a testable hypothesis, (b) independent and dependent variables, (c) two controlled variables, (d) potential sources of systematic error.
*Solution:* Hypothesis: "The extension of a spring is directly proportional to the applied force." Independent: mass added (which determines force F = mg). Dependent: spring extension (measured with a ruler). Controlled: same spring, room temperature, vertical orientation. Systematic error: ruler not perfectly aligned with spring; zero-extension mark measured incorrectly.

#### Problem 2: Analyzing a Table of Results
A student measures the period of a pendulum 5 times: 2.01, 1.98, 2.03, 2.00, 2.02 s. The accepted period is 2.00 s. Calculate: (a) the mean, (b) the percent error.
*Solution:* Mean = (2.01+1.98+2.03+2.00+2.02)/5 = 10.04/5 = 2.008 s. Percent error = |2.008−2.00|/2.00 × 100% = 0.4%.

#### Problem 3: Evaluate This Conclusion
"I dropped a marble from 1 m and 2 m. It took longer from 2 m. Therefore, distance is proportional to time in free fall." Is this conclusion valid?
*Solution:* No. The relationship between distance and time in free fall is d = ½gt² — distance is proportional to time squared, not time. Two data points are also insufficient to claim proportionality; a graph of multiple points is needed.

#### Review Questions
1. What graph would you plot to verify that d ∝ t² for free fall?
2. If all your measured values are 5% higher than the true value, what type of error is this?
3. Why can't two data points prove a proportional relationship?
