#### Must-Know Vector Facts
1. Scalars: mass, speed, distance, energy, time, temperature, work.
2. Vectors: displacement, velocity, acceleration, force, momentum, electric field.
3. |**R**| = √(Rₓ² + Rᵧ²); direction θ = arctan(Rᵧ/Rₓ).
4. Aₓ = A cos(θ), Aᵧ = A sin(θ) (θ from x-axis).
5. **A** · **B** = AB cos(θ) = AₓBₓ + AᵧBᵧ.
6. Work = **F** · **d** = Fd cos(θ).
7. Perpendicular vectors have dot product = 0.
8. Unit vectors: î (x), ĵ (y), k̂ (z), magnitude = 1.

#### Practice Problems
1. Find the resultant of **A** = 8 m/s at 0° and **B** = 6 m/s at 90°. *(10 m/s at 36.9°)*
2. **F** = 20 N, displacement = 5 m at 60° to **F**. Find work. *(50 J)*
3. Is work (W = Fd cos θ) a scalar or vector? *(Scalar)*

#### Review Questions
1. An object moves at velocity **v** = 3î + 4ĵ m/s. What is its speed?
2. A force **F** = 10 N acts perpendicular to a displacement. How much work is done?
3. Find the angle between **A** = î and **B** = ĵ. What is **A** · **B**?
