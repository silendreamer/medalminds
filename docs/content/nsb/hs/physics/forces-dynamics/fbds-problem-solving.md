#### The Five-Step Method
(1) Draw FBD for every object of interest. (2) Choose coordinate axes (positive directions). (3) Write ΣFx = max and ΣFy = may from the FBD. (4) List knowns and unknowns. (5) Solve algebraically, then substitute numbers.

#### Worked Example: Box on Floor
A 10 kg box: applied force 60 N at 37° above horizontal, kinetic friction 25 N. Find acceleration.
- Fx: 60cos37° − 25 = 10a → 60(0.8) − 25 = 10a → 48 − 25 = 10a → a = 2.3 m/s²
- Fy: N + 60sin37° − mg = 0 → N = 98 − 60(0.6) = 98 − 36 = 62 N.

#### Worked Example: Hanging Sign
A 5 kg sign hangs from one vertical chain. FBD: tension T upward, weight 49 N downward. Equilibrium: T − 49 = 0 → T = 49 N. Now the sign hangs from two chains, each vertical: each T = 24.5 N by symmetry.

#### Review Questions
1. A 15 kg crate slides across a floor. Applied force is 80 N horizontal, friction is 45 N. Draw the FBD and find acceleration.
2. A stoplight (mass 12 kg) hangs from a single vertical wire. Draw the FBD and find the wire tension.
3. A 20 kg block is pushed against a wall by a horizontal force of 150 N. The wall exerts friction upward. Draw the FBD and find the minimum friction force needed to prevent the block from sliding down.
