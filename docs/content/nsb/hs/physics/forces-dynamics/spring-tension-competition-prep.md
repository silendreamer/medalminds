#### Must-Know Formulas
- Hooke's Law: F = kx (magnitude) or F = −kx (with direction)
- Spring PE: PE = ½kx²
- Spring series: 1/k_eff = 1/k₁ + 1/k₂ (softer)
- Spring parallel: k_eff = k₁ + k₂ (stiffer)
- Launch speed from spring: v = x√(k/m)
- Tension at top of circular loop: T + mg = mv²/r → T = mv²/r − mg

#### Bowl Toss-Up Stems
- "For 10 points, what law states that the force exerted by a spring is proportional to its displacement from equilibrium?"
- "For 10 points, what is the spring constant of a spring that exerts 30 N when stretched 0.15 m?"
- "For 10 points, what quantity equals one-half times the spring constant times the square of displacement?"

#### Quick Calculations
- k = F/x (from Hooke's Law)
- k = mg/x (from equilibrium hang)
- PE = ½kx² — memorize this form; bowl questions often ask for PE directly

#### Review Questions
1. A spring with k = 400 N/m is stretched 0.25 m. Find (a) force exerted, (b) elastic PE stored.
2. A 3 kg mass hangs at rest from a spring stretched 0.06 m. Find k.
3. "For 10 points, named for an English physicist, what law describes the proportional relationship between spring force and extension?" (Answer: Hooke's Law)
