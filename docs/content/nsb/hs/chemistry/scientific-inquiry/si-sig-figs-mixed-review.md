#### Quick-Fire Unit Conversions
1. 4.5 × 10⁻⁶ m = _____ nm → 4500 nm (or 4.5 μm)
2. 25°C = _____ K → 298.15 K (≈ 298 K)
3. 2.00 atm = _____ mmHg → 1520 mmHg
4. 1.5 kg = _____ g → 1500 g (1.5 × 10³ g)
5. 350 mL = _____ L → 0.350 L
6. 500 kJ = _____ J → 5.0 × 10⁵ J

#### Sig Fig Classification
Count sig figs in each:
- 0.0050 → 2 (leading zeros not significant)
- 100.0 → 4 (trailing zeros after decimal = significant)
- 6.022 × 10²³ → 4 (the exponent doesn't count)
- 1500 → ambiguous (2, 3, or 4 — must use scientific notation to clarify)

#### Calculation Practice
1. 3.24 × 2.1 = 6.804 → round to **6.8** (2 sig figs, limited by 2.1)
2. 18.2 + 1.03 + 0.004 = 19.234 → round to **19.2** (1 decimal place, limited by 18.2)
3. 125.0 / 5.0 = 25.00 → round to **25** (2 sig figs, limited by 5.0)

#### Review Questions
1. A mass is measured as 25.3 g on a balance accurate to 0.1 g, and a volume as 4.2 mL from a graduated cylinder accurate to 0.1 mL. Calculate density to the correct sig figs: d = 25.3/4.2 = ?
2. Why is 100 mL of water NOT necessarily the same number of sig figs as 100. mL?
3. Convert 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² (gravitational constant) — how many sig figs does this have?
