#### Mixed Problem Set

**Unit conversions:**
1. 45.0 nm → m: 45.0 × 10⁻⁹ m = 4.50 × 10⁻⁸ m
2. 2.5 g/cm³ → kg/m³: 2.5 × 1000 = 2500 kg/m³
3. 320 kJ → cal: 320,000 J × (1 cal / 4.184 J) = 76,500 cal = 76.5 kcal

**Molar conversions:**
4. 9.0 g H₂O → mol: 9.0 / 18.02 = 0.50 mol
5. 0.50 mol H₂O → molecules: 0.50 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 3.0 × 10²³ molecules
6. 3.0 × 10²³ molecules H₂O → grams: (3.0 × 10²³ / 6.022 × 10²³) × 18.02 = 9.0 g ✓ (full circle)

**Stoichiometry:**
7. 4.0 g H₂ + excess O₂ → H₂O; grams of water? 4.0/2.02 × (2/2) × 18.02 = 35.7 g

#### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Forgetting to balance the equation before using mole ratios
- Using 22.4 L/mol outside of STP conditions
- Rounding intermediate answers (round only the FINAL answer)
- Flipping a conversion factor (5 kg → g: multiply by 1000, not divide)

#### Review Questions
1. A balloon contains 1.5 mol of He and 0.5 mol of Ne. What is the total mass of gas?
2. 0.250 mol of NaOH reacts with HCl (1:1 ratio). What volume of 0.500 M HCl solution is needed?
3. At STP, 11.2 L of an unknown gas has a mass of 32.0 g. What is the molar mass? What gas might it be?
