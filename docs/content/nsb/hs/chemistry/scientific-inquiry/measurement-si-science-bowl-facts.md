#### Top Bowl Facts: SI Units
1. SI base unit of mass = kilogram (kg); NOT the gram
2. SI base unit of temperature = kelvin (K); NOT Celsius
3. 0°C = 273.15 K; 0 K = absolute zero = −273.15°C
4. 1 L = 1 dm³ = 1000 cm³ = 1000 mL; 1 mL = 1 cm³
5. 1 atm = 760 mmHg = 760 torr = 101,325 Pa = 101.3 kPa
6. 1 cal = 4.184 J; 1 Cal (food) = 1000 cal = 4184 J
7. Density of water = 1.00 g/mL = 1.00 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³
8. Nano = 10⁻⁹; visible light wavelengths ≈ 400–700 nm

#### Top Bowl Facts: Significant Figures
9. Leading zeros: never significant
10. Captive zeros (between nonzeros): always significant
11. Trailing zeros + decimal point: significant
12. Trailing zeros without decimal: ambiguous
13. Multiplication/division: match fewest sig figs
14. Addition/subtraction: match fewest decimal places
15. Exact numbers and defined conversions have infinite sig figs

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "For 10 points — what is the SI base unit of amount of substance?" → **mole**
2. "For 10 points — what is the value of absolute zero in degrees Celsius?" → **−273.15°C** (accept −273°C)
3. "For 10 points — how many significant figures are in the number 0.00420?" → **3**

#### Review Questions
1. A question asks for pressure in pascals given 2.50 atm. What is the answer?
2. How many sig figs in 6.0230 × 10²³? What famous constant does this approximate?
3. True or false: the SI base unit of mass is the gram. Explain.
