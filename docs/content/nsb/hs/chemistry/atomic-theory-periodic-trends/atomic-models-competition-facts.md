#### Toss-Up Pattern Recognition
"For 10 points, this physicist's gold foil experiment, which he conducted with Geiger and Marsden, revealed that most atomic mass is concentrated in a small dense region." → **Rutherford**

"For 10 points, this model of the atom depicts electrons embedded in a diffuse sphere of positive charge, resembling fruit in a baked dessert." → **Thomson's plum pudding model**

"For 10 points, the equation Eₙ = −13.6 eV/n² describes electron energy in the model proposed by this Danish physicist." → **Niels Bohr**

#### The Must-Know Timeline
- 1803: Dalton — postulates, definite proportions
- 1897: Thomson — electron, cathode rays, plum pudding
- 1909: Rutherford — gold foil, nuclear model
- 1913: Bohr — quantized orbits, explains H spectrum
- 1924: de Broglie — wave-particle duality for matter
- 1926: Schrödinger — wave equation, orbitals
- 1927: Heisenberg — uncertainty principle
