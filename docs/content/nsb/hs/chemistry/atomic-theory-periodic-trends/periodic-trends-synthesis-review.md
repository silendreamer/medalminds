#### Trend Summary
| Property | Across Period (→) | Down Group (↓) |
|----------|------------------|----------------|
| Atomic radius | Decreases | Increases |
| IE₁ | Generally increases (2 exceptions) | Decreases |
| Electron affinity | Generally increases | Decreases |
| Electronegativity | Increases | Decreases |
| Metallic character | Decreases | Increases |

#### Quick Comparisons
- Largest atom in period 3: Na (leftmost, lowest Z_eff)
- Highest IE₁ in period 2: Ne (noble gas, fully filled shells)
- Most electronegative element: F
- Least electronegative metal: Cs (bottom-left of periodic table)

#### Review Questions
1. Arrange K, Ca, Ga, Ge in order of decreasing atomic radius.
2. Which element in period 2 has the highest first ionization energy, and why do noble gases typically have higher IE₁ than halogens?
3. Why does the metallic character of elements increase going down a group?
