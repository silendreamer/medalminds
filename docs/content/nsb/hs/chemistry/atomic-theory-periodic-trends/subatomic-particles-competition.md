#### Toss-Up Stems to Know
"For 10 points, this particle, discovered by James Chadwick in 1932, has no electric charge and resides in the atomic nucleus." → **neutron**

"For 10 points, the number of this type of subatomic particle uniquely determines an element's identity and is called the atomic number." → **proton**

"For 10 points, chlorine's atomic mass of approximately 35.45 amu is a weighted average because chlorine has two naturally occurring forms of this type." → **isotopes**

#### Key Numbers to Memorize
- Proton mass ≈ 1 amu = 1.673 × 10⁻²⁷ kg
- Electron mass ≈ 1/1836 amu = 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg
- Proton charge = +1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C
- Electron charge = −1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C
- Neutron charge = 0

#### Review Questions
1. Who discovered the neutron, and in what year?
2. An element has atomic number 19 and mass number 39. Give the element name and the number of neutrons.
3. Write a bowl-style toss-up question about isotopes of carbon.
