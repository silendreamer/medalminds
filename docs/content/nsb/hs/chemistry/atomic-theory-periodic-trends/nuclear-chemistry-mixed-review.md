#### Decay Equation Practice
Complete these nuclear equations:
1. ²²⁶₈₈Ra → ⁴₂He + ? → Answer: ²²²₈₆Rn
2. ¹³₇N → ⁰₋₁e + ? → Answer: ¹³₆C (beta decay; Z decreases by 1)
3. ²³⁵₉₂U + ¹₀n → ¹⁴¹₅₆Ba + ³⁶Kr + ? neutrons → 236 = 141 + 92 + 3×1; so 3 neutrons

#### Half-Life Calculation
I-131 (used in thyroid treatment) has half-life 8.02 days. A patient receives 12 mCi. How much remains after 24.06 days?
n = 24.06/8.02 = 3 half-lives. Remaining = 12 × (½)³ = 12/8 = 1.5 mCi.

#### Review Questions

2. A 40 g sample of a radioactive isotope decays to 5 g in 36 hours. What is the half-life?
3. Distinguish between nuclear fission and fusion in terms of the types of nuclei involved and the conditions required.
