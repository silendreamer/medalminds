#### Toss-Up Stems
"For 10 points, this property of atoms, measured in picometers, decreases across a period and increases down a group due to effective nuclear charge and electron shielding." → **atomic radius**

"For 10 points, this Nobel Prize-winning chemist developed the most widely used electronegativity scale, on which fluorine has a value of 4.0." → **Linus Pauling**

"For 10 points, the large jump between the first and second ionization energies of sodium reveals that it belongs to what group of the periodic table?" → **Group 1 (alkali metals)**

#### Numbers to Know
- F electronegativity: 4.0 (highest)
- Cs electronegativity: 0.7 (lowest for a metal)
- He IE₁: 2372 kJ/mol (highest)
- Fr IE₁: ~393 kJ/mol (lowest)

#### Review Questions
1. Which two elements in period 2 have lower first ionization energies than would be predicted by a smooth left-to-right increase?
2. A question asks which is larger: a K atom or a K⁺ ion. Give the answer and the reason.
3. Francium is in the bottom-left of the periodic table. Predict its approximate electronegativity and first ionization energy relative to Cs.
