#### Toss-Up Stems
"For 10 points, this rule states that electrons occupy separate orbitals within a sublevel before pairing, all with the same spin." → **Hund's rule**

"For 10 points, this principle, named for an Austrian physicist, states that no two electrons in an atom can have the same four quantum numbers." → **Pauli exclusion principle**

"For 10 points, chromium's ground-state electron configuration deviates from the expected [Ar] 4s² 3d⁴ to become [Ar] 4s¹ 3d⁵ due to the extra stability of this type of d sublevel." → **half-filled (d sublevel)**

#### Must-Know Exceptions
Only Cr ([Ar]4s¹3d⁵) and Cu ([Ar]4s¹3d¹⁰) are tested at the HS level. Know the reason: half-filled (Cr) and fully-filled (Cu) d sublevels are especially stable.

2. Give the quantum numbers (n, l, mₗ, mₛ) for the last electron added to a chlorine atom.
