#### Haber Process (Ammonia Synthesis)

The **Haber process** (also called the Haber-Bosch process) synthesizes ammonia from nitrogen and hydrogen: **N2 + 3H2 -> 2NH3**. The reaction is carried out at high pressure (150–300 atm) and moderate temperature (400–500 °C) over an **iron catalyst** with promoters such as K2O and Al2O3. Although lower temperatures favor the exothermic equilibrium product (Le Chatelier's principle), higher temperatures are used in practice to achieve an acceptable **reaction rate**. The ammonia produced is the primary industrial source of fixed nitrogen for fertilizers.

#### Contact Process (Sulfuric Acid Synthesis)

The **Contact process** manufactures **sulfuric acid (H2SO4)** in three key steps. First, sulfur is burned to give **SO2**: S + O2 -> SO2. Second, SO2 is oxidized to **SO3** over a **vanadium(V) oxide (V2O5) catalyst** at 450–600 °C: 2SO2 + O2 -> 2SO3. Third, SO3 is absorbed into concentrated H2SO4 to form **oleum (H2S2O7)**, which is then diluted with water to yield sulfuric acid. Direct absorption of SO3 into water is avoided because the reaction is violently exothermic. H2SO4 is the world's most widely produced industrial chemical, used in fertilizers, refining, and chemical synthesis.

#### Solvay Process (Sodium Carbonate Synthesis)

The **Solvay process** (ammonia-soda process) produces **sodium carbonate (Na2CO3, soda ash)** from brine (NaCl solution) and limestone (CaCO3). Carbon dioxide from calcined limestone and ammonia are passed through brine, precipitating **sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3)**. Heating decomposes it: 2NaHCO3 -> Na2CO3 + H2O + CO2. Ammonia is recovered from the ammonium chloride byproduct by treating it with slaked lime Ca(OH)2. Na2CO3 is essential for glass manufacturing, detergents, and paper production.

#### Comparison of Conditions

| Process | Product | Key Catalyst | Temperature | Pressure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Haber | NH3 | Fe (with K2O, Al2O3) | 400–500 °C | 150–300 atm |
| Contact | H2SO4 | V2O5 | 450–600 °C | ~1–2 atm |
| Solvay | Na2CO3 | None (NH3 recycled) | ~60–80 °C | ~1 atm |

Each process applies equilibrium and kinetic principles to maximize industrial yield at an economical rate.
