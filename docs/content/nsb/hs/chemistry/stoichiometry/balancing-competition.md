#### Bowl-Style Toss-Up #1
"Balance the equation: iron reacts with chlorine gas to form iron(III) chloride. 2Fe + ___Cl₂ → 2FeCl₃. What is the coefficient?" (Answer: 3.)

#### Bowl-Style Toss-Up #2
"In the combustion of ethanol (C₂H₅OH), what is the coefficient of oxygen gas?" C₂H₅OH + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 3H₂O. (Answer: 3.)

#### Systematic Redox Balancing
For redox, identify what's oxidized and reduced. Example: Fe + Cu²⁺ → Fe³⁺ + Cu. Fe loses 2 electrons, Cu²⁺ gains 2 electrons. Balance electrons to balance the equation: Fe + Cu²⁺ → Fe³⁺ + Cu (no extra coefficient needed).

#### Speed Trick
Combustion of CₙHₘ always produces n CO₂ and m/2 H₂O. The oxygen coefficient follows from balancing the right side. This pattern recognition saves time in bowl competitions.

#### Review Questions
1. For 10 points: Balance permanganate reduction in acidic solution. MnO₄⁻ + Fe²⁺ + H⁺ → Mn²⁺ + Fe³⁺ + H₂O
2. For 15 points: Disproportionation of Cl₂. Cl₂ + OH⁻ → Cl⁻ + ClO⁻ + H₂O
3. Bonus (20 points): P₄ + O₂ → P₄O₁₀ (tricky: white phosphorus burning)
