#### Law Identification Practice
Match the scenario to the law:
1. A sealed syringe is cooled — pressure stays the same, volume decreases → **Charles's Law**
2. A sealed rigid container is heated — volume stays same, pressure increases → **Gay-Lussac's Law**
3. Gas is compressed at constant temperature — volume decreases, pressure increases → **Boyle's Law**
4. Both P and T change while n is fixed → **Combined Gas Law**

#### Common Calculation Errors
- Using Celsius instead of Kelvin → Guaranteed wrong answer
- Mixing pressure units (atm on one side, kPa on other) → Off by 101.3×
- Solving for V₂ but forgetting to cancel units

#### Speed Practice
A gas: P₁ = 2 atm, V₁ = 3 L, T₁ = 300 K → P₂ = 1 atm, T₂ = 150 K. Find V₂.
V₂ = (P₁V₁T₂)/(T₁P₂) = (2 × 3 × 150)/(300 × 1) = 900/300 = **3 L**
(Both P halved and T halved — effects cancel → same volume)

#### Review Questions
1. A gas at 5.0 L, 2.0 atm, 400 K is changed to 1.0 atm and 200 K. Find new volume.
2. Convert 3.0 L of gas at 150°C and 2.0 atm to STP. Find liters at STP.
3. Which law is being applied: a balloon at constant temperature is carried deeper underwater and shrinks?
