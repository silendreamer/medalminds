#### Classification Quick-Fire
Classify each (element / compound / homogeneous mixture / heterogeneous mixture):
1. Pure water (H₂O) → compound
2. Bronze (Cu + Sn alloy) → homogeneous mixture (solution)
3. Helium gas → element
4. Milk → heterogeneous mixture (colloid)
5. Carbon dioxide → compound
6. Air (N₂, O₂, Ar, etc.) → homogeneous mixture
7. Soil → heterogeneous mixture
8. Diamond (pure C) → element

#### Property Classification
Physical (P) or Chemical (C):
- Melting point → P · Rusting → C · Odor → P · Toxicity → C
- Color → P · Flammability → C · Density → P · Reactivity with acid → C
- Boiling point → P · Decomposition temperature → C

#### Separation Technique Matching
Match the separation to its best technique:
- Sugar from water → evaporation/crystallization
- Alcohol from water → distillation
- Ink dye components → chromatography
- Sand from water → filtration
- Iron from sulfur → magnetic separation
- Blood cells from plasma → centrifugation

#### Review Questions
1. A student heats a green copper compound in a crucible and observes it turn black with gas production. Is this physical or chemical change? Name two evidence items.
2. Are the following intensive or extensive: specific gravity, volume, electrical conductivity, heat capacity of a 1-kg sample?
3. Why can homogeneous mixtures not be separated by filtration but can often be separated by distillation?
