#### Drawing Practice
Draw and evaluate Lewis structures for:
- HCN: H−C≡N (triple bond; C has 0 lone pairs; N has 1 lone pair; all octets satisfied; all formal charges = 0)
- SO₂: two resonance structures; S−O bond order = 1.5
- ClO₄⁻ (perchlorate): Cl central, 4 oxygen atoms; expanded octet on Cl; formal charges reduced with double bonds

#### Bond Length/Energy Predictions from Lewis Structure
Given NO vs. NO₂ vs. NO₃⁻: as bond order decreases (2 > 1.5 > 1.33), bond length increases and bond energy decreases.

#### Review Questions
1. Draw the Lewis structure for ozone (O₃) and predict the O−O bond length relative to O₂ (double bond, 121 pm) and H₂O₂ (single bond, 148 pm).
2. Calculate the formal charge on N in NH₃ and in NH₄⁺.
3. Explain why the carbonate ion (CO₃²⁻) has three equivalent C−O bond lengths of 129 pm, between a single bond (143 pm) and double bond (122 pm).
