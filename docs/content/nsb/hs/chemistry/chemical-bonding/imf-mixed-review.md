#### Complete Analysis
For each substance, list all IMFs and predict one physical property relative to a similar molecule:
- CH₃COOH (acetic acid): LDF + dipole-dipole + H-bonding → BP (118°C) much higher than similarly sized CH₃CH₃ (ethane, −89°C)
- C₆H₆ (benzene): LDF only (nonpolar ring) → BP 80°C; lower than toluene (methyl group makes slightly polar)
- KI: ion-ion forces in crystal (ionic bond) → very high MP (681°C)

#### Solubility Prediction
Predict miscibility with water: hexane (no, nonpolar vs polar water), acetone (yes, polar, some H-bond acceptance), NaOH (yes, ionic dissolves in water), gasoline (no, nonpolar mixture).

#### Review Questions
1. Why is acetic acid (CH₃COOH) a liquid at room temperature while similarly sized propanal (CH₃CH₂CHO) is also liquid, but acetic acid has a higher boiling point?
2. Which would be more soluble in hexane: CCl₄ or NaCl? Explain.
3. A student claims "since HF has stronger hydrogen bonds than H₂O, it should have a higher boiling point." Evaluate this claim — is it correct, and why or why not?
