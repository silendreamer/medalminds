#### Toss-Up Stems
"For 10 points, in the bonding of ethylene, the carbon-carbon double bond consists of one bond of this type, formed by head-on orbital overlap, and one pi bond formed by side-on p orbital overlap." → **sigma bond**

"For 10 points, carbon atoms in a C=C double bond have this hybridization, which produces a trigonal planar geometry and leaves one unhybridized p orbital." → **sp²**

"For 10 points, the hybridization of the central atom in water, with its four electron domains, is named for how many s and p orbitals combine." → **sp³**

#### Sigma/Pi Count Reference
| Bond order | # σ bonds | # π bonds |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Single | 1 | 0 |
| Double | 1 | 1 |
| Triple | 1 | 2 |

3. How many pi bonds are in a molecule with 4 double bonds and 2 triple bonds?
