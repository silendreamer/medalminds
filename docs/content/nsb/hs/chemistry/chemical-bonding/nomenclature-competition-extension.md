#### Toss-Up Stems
"For 10 points, the compound with formula Fe₂O₃, the main component of rust, is named what?" → **iron(III) oxide**

"For 10 points, the naming system that uses Roman numerals to indicate the charge of transition metal ions is named after this Swedish chemist." → **Alfred Stock** (the Stock system)

"For 10 points, this compound, with formula HNO₃, is a strong acid widely used in the production of fertilizers and explosives." → **nitric acid**

#### Must-Know Polyatomic Ions
| Ion | Name | Ion | Name |
|-----|------|-----|------|
| NO₃⁻ | nitrate | CO₃²⁻ | carbonate |
| NO₂⁻ | nitrite | HCO₃⁻ | bicarbonate (hydrogen carbonate) |
| SO₄²⁻ | sulfate | OH⁻ | hydroxide |
| SO₃²⁻ | sulfite | NH₄⁺ | ammonium |
| PO₄³⁻ | phosphate | ClO₄⁻ | perchlorate |
| CrO₄²⁻ | chromate | Cr₂O₇²⁻ | dichromate |

#### Review Questions
1. A toss-up describes "a polyatomic anion with formula SO₄²⁻." Name it.
2. Write the formula for iron(III) phosphate and explain how you determined the subscripts.
3. What is the difference between sulfate and sulfite ions in terms of formula and charge?
