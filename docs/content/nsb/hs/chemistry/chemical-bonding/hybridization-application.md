#### Hybridization in Organic Molecules
- Methane (CH₄): C is sp³ → tetrahedral, 109.5°, no π bonds
- Ethylene (C₂H₄): each C is sp² → planar molecule, 120°, one π bond
- Acetylene (C₂H₂): each C is sp → linear, 180°, two π bonds
- Benzene (C₆H₆): each C is sp² → planar ring, 120°, delocalized π system

#### Mixed Hybridization in One Molecule
Acetic acid (CH₃COOH): the CH₃ carbon is sp³ (4 single bonds); the carbonyl carbon (C=O) is sp² (3 domains including the C=O); the O in OH is sp³ (2 bonds + 2 lone pairs). Different carbons in the same molecule can have different hybridization.

#### Inorganic Examples
- SO₂: S is sp² (2 bonds + 1 lone pair = 3 domains)
- PCl₅: P is sp³d (5 domains)
- SF₆: S is sp³d² (6 domains)

#### Review Questions
1. What hybridization does the central carbon in formaldehyde (H₂C=O) have? What about the oxygen?
2. Explain why sp hybridization produces linear geometry.
3. A molecule has a central atom with 3 bonds and 1 lone pair. What is its hybridization, electron geometry, and molecular geometry?
