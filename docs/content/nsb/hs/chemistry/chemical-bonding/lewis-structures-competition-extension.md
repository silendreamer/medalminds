#### Toss-Up Stems
"For 10 points, in a Lewis structure, this quantity is calculated as the number of valence electrons minus lone pair electrons minus half the bonding electrons, and should be minimized." → **formal charge**

"For 10 points, the concept where a molecule's true electron distribution is described as an average of multiple valid Lewis structures is called what?" → **resonance**

"For 10 points, atoms of period 3 and beyond, like phosphorus and sulfur, can accommodate more than eight electrons around them by using what type of orbitals?" → **d orbitals (expanded octet)**

#### Must-Know Species
- O₃: 2 resonance structures; bond order 1.5; bent shape
- NO₃⁻: 3 resonance structures; N−O bond order 1.33
- SO₄²⁻: 4 equivalent S−O bonds; resonance-stabilized
- Benzene C₆H₆: 2 Kekulé structures; bond order 1.5; delocalized π system

#### Review Questions
1. For CO₂, explain why there is no need to invoke resonance even though it has two C=O bonds.
2. What is the formal charge on each atom in the Lewis structure of H₂O?
3. A molecule has 3 resonance structures each with one double bond. What is the bond order of those bonds?
