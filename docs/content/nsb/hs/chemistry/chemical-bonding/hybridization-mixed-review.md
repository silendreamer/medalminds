#### Full Analysis Practice
Analyze each molecule completely (Lewis structure → VSEPR → hybridization → σ/π bonds → polarity):
- CH₂Cl₂: sp³, tetrahedral, 4σ, 0π, polar (asymmetric)
- CO₂: sp, linear, 2σ, 2π, nonpolar (symmetric)
- H₂CO (formaldehyde): C is sp², trigonal planar, 3σ (2 C−H + 1 C=O σ) + 1π, polar

#### Review Questions
1. For acetylene (HC≡CH), describe: bond angles, hybridization of C, number of σ and π bonds, molecular polarity.
2. Why does increasing bond order (single → double → triple) progressively shorten the bond?
3. A compound has a carbon with sp³ hybridization, a carbon with sp² hybridization, and an oxygen with sp³ hybridization. Propose a simple formula that fits this description.
