#### Property Prediction Table
Fill in predicted properties (MP, conductivity in solution, state at RT):
- NaCl: high MP, yes conducts, solid
- CCl₄: low MP, no conducts, liquid
- SiO₂ (network covalent): very high MP, no conducts, solid
- NH₃: low MP, no conducts (but dissolves), gas → liquid at −33°C

#### Lattice Energy Ranking
Rank in order of increasing lattice energy: NaCl, MgO, NaF, CaO. Reasoning: Na⁺/Cl⁻ (1+/1−, large) < Na⁺/F⁻ (1+/1−, smaller F−) < Ca²⁺/O²⁻ (2+/2−) ≈ Mg²⁺/O²⁻ (2+/2−, Mg²⁺ is smaller so MgO > CaO). Order: NaCl < NaF < CaO < MgO.

#### Review Questions
1. Which would have the highest lattice energy: NaCl, MgO, or Al₂O₃? Explain.
2. Classify each as ionic, polar covalent, nonpolar covalent, or network covalent: SiC, HBr, K₂O, Xe.
3. A student predicts that NaF will have a higher melting point than NaCl. Is the student correct? Explain using lattice energy arguments.
