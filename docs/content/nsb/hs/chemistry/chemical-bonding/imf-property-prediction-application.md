#### Predicting Solubility: Like Dissolves Like
Polar solvents dissolve polar/ionic solutes; nonpolar solvents dissolve nonpolar solutes. Water (H-bonding) dissolves NaCl (ionic) because water molecules solvate Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions. Water dissolves ethanol (H-bonding between OH groups). Water does NOT dissolve hexane (nonpolar, only LDF) — immiscible layers form.

#### IMF in Biology
DNA double helix: held together by H-bonds between base pairs (A-T: 2 H-bonds; G-C: 3 H-bonds). DNA strands can be separated by heating (denaturation) — temperature required depends on G-C content (more H-bonds → higher melting temperature). Protein folding: secondary structure (alpha helices, beta sheets) maintained by H-bonds between backbone −NH and −C=O groups.

#### Comparison Problem
Compare boiling points: (a) pentane C₅H₁₂ (MW 72, nonpolar, only LDF) = 36°C vs. (b) butanol C₄H₉OH (MW 74, polar, H-bonding) = 118°C. Nearly equal MW, but butanol is 82°C higher — direct evidence of H-bonding's strength.

#### Review Questions
1. Predict which has the higher boiling point and explain: CH₃CH₃ (ethane) vs. CH₃F (fluoromethane). They have similar molar masses.
2. Why does oil not dissolve in water?
3. Explain why DNA's G-C base pairs are more thermally stable than A-T base pairs.
