#### What Are Functional Groups?
A **functional group** is a specific atom or arrangement of atoms within an organic molecule that determines its characteristic chemical reactions. The rest of the molecule — the **carbon skeleton** or **R group** — is largely inert. Functional groups give organic chemistry its predictability: molecules sharing the same group react similarly regardless of chain length.

#### Oxygen-Containing Functional Groups
**Alcohols** contain a hydroxyl group (-OH) bonded to a carbon (e.g., ethanol, CH3CH2OH); they undergo dehydration and oxidation reactions. **Ethers** have an oxygen bridging two carbons (R-O-R'), as in diethyl ether (CH3CH2-O-CH2CH3). **Aldehydes** bear a carbonyl group at the chain end (-CHO), while **ketones** have an internal carbonyl (C=O); both are susceptible to nucleophilic addition. **Carboxylic acids** (-COOH) are weakly acidic and form esters and amides through condensation reactions; acetic acid (CH3COOH) is a classic example.

#### Nitrogen and Halogen Groups
**Amines** (-NH2, -NHR, or -NR2) are derivatives of ammonia and act as bases due to the lone pair on nitrogen. **Amides** (-CONH2) link a carbonyl to a nitrogen and form the peptide bonds of proteins. **Alkyl halides** (R-X, where X = F, Cl, Br, I) are polar and undergo nucleophilic substitution (SN1/SN2) and elimination reactions; reactivity generally increases as F < Cl < Br < I.

#### Hydrocarbons as the Baseline
**Alkanes** (C-C single bonds) are the least reactive class — they resist most reagents except combustion and free-radical halogenation. **Alkenes** (C=C) and **alkynes** (C≡C) are far more reactive because the pi bond is available for **electrophilic addition** (e.g., addition of HBr across a double bond following Markovnikov's rule). **Aromatic** rings (benzene, C6H6) undergo **electrophilic aromatic substitution** rather than addition, preserving ring stability.

#### Review Questions
1. A compound with the molecular formula C2H5OH contains which functional group, and what type of reaction does it most readily undergo when treated with a strong acid at high temperature?
2. Explain why carboxylic acids are more acidic than alcohols, referencing the structural feature responsible for the difference in pKa.
3. An unknown organic compound decolorizes bromine water and produces a secondary alkyl bromide upon reaction with HBr. Identify the class of the starting material and state the regiochemical rule that governs product formation.
