#### Review Questions (Extended Set)
1. Explain "defense in depth" in nuclear reactor safety using three specific barriers between fuel and environment.
2. Three Mile Island (1979): primary coolant was lost, fuel partially melted, but no significant radiation was released to the environment. What safety feature prevented release?
3. Chernobyl (1986): the RBMK reactor had a positive void coefficient — as coolant boiled, reactivity increased. Explain why this made the reactor inherently less safe than a PWR.
4. Fukushima Daiichi (2011): the reactors shut down automatically after the earthquake, but three units melted down. What caused the meltdown if the chain reaction had stopped?
5. Modern reactor designs (AP1000, EPR) use "passive safety" — safety systems that require no active pumping or operator action. Give one example of a passive safety feature.
