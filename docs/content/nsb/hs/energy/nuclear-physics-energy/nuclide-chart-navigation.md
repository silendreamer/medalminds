#### Review Questions (Extended Set)
1. On a nuclide chart (N vs. Z), in which direction does alpha decay move a nucleus? Beta-minus? Beta-plus?
2. Why does the valley of stability curve away from the N = Z line for heavy nuclei?
3. A nucleus at (Z=55, N=82) lies above the valley of stability. Which decay mode would you predict, and why?
4. Explain why there are no stable isotopes with Z > 82 (lead).
5. Technetium (Z=43) has no stable isotopes despite being a relatively light element. Propose an explanation using nuclear structure concepts.
