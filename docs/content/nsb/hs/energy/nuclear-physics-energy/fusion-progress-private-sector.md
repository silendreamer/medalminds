#### Why Now? High-Temperature Superconductors (HTS)
Until recently, tokamak magnets used low-temperature superconductors (LTS) requiring liquid helium cooling to 4 K and limited to ~12 tesla field strength. New rare-earth barium copper oxide (REBCO) HTS tapes, cooled only to 20 K (much easier) with liquid nitrogen, can generate 20+ tesla fields. Stronger fields confine plasma much more effectively — plasma pressure scales as B⁴. This allowed a major redesign: smaller, cheaper tokamaks.

#### Commonwealth Fusion Systems: SPARC
Commonwealth Fusion Systems (CFS), MIT spinoff, is building SPARC — a compact tokamak (major radius 1.85 m, vs. ITER's 6.2 m) targeting Q > 2 by ~2025. If successful, they plan ARC — a fusion power plant demonstrating electricity generation by the 2030s. CFS demonstrated 20-tesla HTS magnets in September 2021, a key milestone.

#### Other Private Fusion Companies
- **TAE Technologies**: field-reversed configuration, uses proton-boron (aneutronic) fuel — no neutrons; would reduce material damage and activation
- **Helion Energy**: field-reversed configuration with direct energy conversion; Microsoft signed a power purchase agreement for 2028 delivery
- **Tri Alpha Energy → TAE**: decades of FRC research
- **General Fusion**: magnetized target fusion — plasma compressed by pistons
Over $6 billion invested in private fusion as of 2024. Optimists project grid power by 2035–2040.
