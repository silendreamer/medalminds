#### Review Questions (Extended Set)
1. A nucleus has Z = 90 and A = 230. Is it likely to be stable or unstable? Justify using N/Z ratio.
2. Using the concept of binding energy per nucleon, explain why uranium-235 releases energy when it fissions into barium-141 and krypton-92.
3. Why does helium-4 (an alpha particle) appear so often as a decay product of heavy nuclei?
4. Calculate the binding energy per nucleon for deuterium (²H, mass = 2.01410 u; proton = 1.00728 u; neutron = 1.00867 u).
5. Lead-208 is doubly magic (Z=82, N=126). Predict: would lead-207 or lead-209 be more or less stable than lead-208, and why?
