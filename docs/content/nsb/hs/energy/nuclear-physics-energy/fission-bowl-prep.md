#### High-Yield Facts
- Fissile isotopes: U-235, Pu-239 (thermal neutrons); U-233 (from Th-232 breeding)
- Energy per fission: ~200 MeV
- Most common reactor: PWR (pressurized water reactor)
- Moderator purpose: slow fast neutrons to thermal energies
- Control rod materials: boron, hafnium, silver-indium-cadmium
- Chernobyl reactor type: RBMK (graphite-moderated, light-water-cooled)
- Fukushima: BWR (boiling water reactor)
- US reactor count: ~93 operating reactors, providing ~20% of US electricity
- Nuclear capacity factor: >90% (highest of any major source)
- First controlled chain reaction: Chicago Pile-1, December 2, 1942, Fermi/Manhattan Project

#### Bowl Toss-Up Stems
- "For 10 points, this type of reactor uses pressurized water as both moderator and coolant…" → **Pressurized water reactor (PWR)**
- "For 10 points, this is the multiplication factor describing a self-sustaining chain reaction…" → **k = 1 (critical)**
- "For 10 points, this Manhattan Project experiment on December 2, 1942 achieved the first controlled nuclear chain reaction…" → **Chicago Pile-1**
