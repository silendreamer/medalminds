#### Review Questions (Extended Set)
1. Radon-222 (t½ = 3.82 days) accumulates in a sealed basement. If the initial activity is 400 Bq, what is the activity after 19.1 days?
2. A nucleus undergoes alpha decay followed by two beta-minus decays. If it started as ²³⁸₉₂U, what is the final nucleus?
3. A patient receives 0.5 Gy of neutron radiation (Q = 10) and 2 Gy of gamma radiation (Q = 1). What is the total dose equivalent in sieverts?
4. Why does iodine-131 accumulate in the thyroid gland, and how does this property make it useful for thyroid cancer treatment?
5. Explain why a 1 Ci source of alpha emitters is more dangerous if ingested than if held in the hand.
