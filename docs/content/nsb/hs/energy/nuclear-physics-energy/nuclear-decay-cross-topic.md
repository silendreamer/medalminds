#### Review Questions (Extended Set)
1. Mass-energy equivalence: an alpha particle (mass 4.0015 u) is emitted from Po-210. The total mass of reactants is 209.9829 u and products is 209.9787 u (excluding alpha). Calculate the mass defect in u and estimate the energy released (1 u = 931.5 MeV/c²).
2. How does uranium-238's half-life of 4.47 billion years make it useful as a geological clock?
3. The decay equation ²¹⁰₈₄Po → X + ⁴₂He. Identify element X (give symbol, Z, and A).
4. Why does the rate of nuclear decay not change with temperature, pressure, or chemical state — unlike chemical reaction rates?
5. A chain of decays produces a daughter with a much shorter half-life than the parent. Sketch how the activity of the parent and daughter change over time until secular equilibrium is reached.
