#### Why Apoptosis Matters
Apoptosis — programmed, orderly cell death — is essential for normal development and homeostasis. In human embryos, apoptosis sculpts fingers (by killing webbing cells) and eliminates autoreactive T cells. Adults remove ~100 billion cells daily via apoptosis. Insufficient apoptosis contributes to cancer and autoimmunity; excessive apoptosis causes neurodegeneration. Apoptosis differs from **necrosis** (accidental cell death): apoptotic cells shrink, chromatin condenses, DNA is laddered, and the cell fragments into "apoptotic bodies" without releasing inflammatory contents.

#### Intrinsic Pathway: The Mitochondrial Route
The intrinsic pathway is triggered by internal stress signals: DNA damage, oxidative stress, ER stress, growth factor withdrawal. The key decision is made at the mitochondrial outer membrane by the **Bcl-2 family**:
- **Pro-survival:** Bcl-2, Bcl-XL, Mcl-1 (keep mitochondria intact)
- **Pro-apoptotic:** Bax, Bak (form pores in the outer membrane)
- **BH3-only proteins:** Bad, Bim, Bid, Puma, Noxa (activated by stress; neutralize pro-survival Bcl-2 proteins)

When pro-apoptotic signals overcome pro-survival proteins, Bax and Bak oligomerize and permeabilize the outer mitochondrial membrane, releasing **cytochrome c** into the cytoplasm.

#### The Apoptosome and Caspase Cascade
Released cytochrome c binds **Apaf-1**, triggering the assembly of a wheel-shaped heptameric complex called the **apoptosome**. The apoptosome activates **caspase-9** (an initiator caspase), which activates **caspase-3 and caspase-7** (executioner caspases). Executioner caspases cleave hundreds of cellular proteins: structural proteins (lamins collapse the nucleus), DNase inhibitors (ICAD is cleaved, releasing CAD to fragment DNA), and cytoskeletal proteins.

#### Extrinsic Pathway: Death Receptors
External signals can also trigger apoptosis via **death receptors** (TNFR1, Fas/CD95) on the cell surface. Ligand binding (TNF, FasL) causes receptor clustering and recruitment of adaptor protein **FADD**, which recruits and activates **caspase-8** (initiator caspase). Caspase-8 then directly activates caspase-3, bypassing the mitochondria. In some cells, caspase-8 also cleaves Bid to Truncated Bid (tBid), amplifying the signal through the intrinsic pathway.
