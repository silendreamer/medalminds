#### Bowl Toss-Up Patterns
- "Phosphorylates Rb to release E2F" → **Cyclin D–CDK4/6**
- "Guardian of the genome, mutated in >50% of cancers" → **p53**
- "Named for a childhood eye tumor, regulates G1/S checkpoint" → **Rb (retinoblastoma protein)**
- "Knudson's two-hit hypothesis explains inheritance of mutations in this protein" → **Rb**
- "CDK inhibitor induced by p53 in response to DNA damage" → **p21**
- "Inhibits CDK4 and CDK6, commonly deleted in melanoma" → **p16 (INK4a)**
- "Chromosomal translocation causing CML, treated by imatinib" → **BCR-ABL**

#### Sample Toss-Ups
*"For 10 points — name the protein kinase complex that drives the G2-to-M transition, consisting of Cyclin B bound to CDK1."*
**Answer: MPF (maturation-promoting factor) / Cyclin B–CDK1**

*"For 10 points — this tumor suppressor protein, when unphosphorylated, sequesters the transcription factor E2F to prevent S phase entry."*
**Answer: Rb (retinoblastoma protein)**

#### Review Questions
1. What is MPF, and what cell cycle transition does it trigger?
2. A cell has a gain-of-function mutation in CDK4 and a loss-of-function mutation in p16. Predict the combined effect on Rb.
3. Why is a point mutation in RAS described as a dominant oncogenic mutation?
