#### Must-Know Apoptosis Facts
- Apoptosis = programmed cell death; necrosis = accidental cell death
- Intrinsic pathway: stress → Bcl-2 family decision → cytochrome c release → apoptosome → caspase-9 → caspase-3
- Extrinsic pathway: FasL/TNF → death receptor → FADD → caspase-8 → caspase-3
- Apoptosome components: cytochrome c + Apaf-1 + caspase-9
- DNA laddering: CAD cleaves DNA at nucleosomal intervals (180 bp multiples)
- "Eat me" signal: phosphatidylserine on outer leaflet
- Bcl-2 overexpression → t(14;18) → follicular lymphoma

#### Sample Toss-Ups
*"For 10 points — name the large protein complex formed from cytochrome c, Apaf-1, and caspase-9 that initiates caspase activation in the intrinsic apoptosis pathway."*
**Answer: apoptosome**

*"For 10 points — this adaptor protein binds the death domain of the Fas receptor and recruits caspase-8 to initiate extrinsic apoptosis."*
**Answer: FADD (Fas-associated death domain protein)**

*"For 10 points — give the class of anti-cancer drugs that includes venetoclax and works by mimicking BH3-only proteins to displace Bax from Bcl-2."*
**Answer: BH3 mimetics**

#### Review Questions
1. Name the four components of the DISC (death-inducing signaling complex) in extrinsic apoptosis.
2. What is the DNA fragmentation pattern in apoptotic cells, and which enzyme causes it?
3. Distinguish Bcl-2 (pro-survival) from Bax (pro-apoptotic) by their effect on the mitochondrial membrane.
