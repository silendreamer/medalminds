#### Must-Know Second Messengers
| Messenger | Made by | Acts on |
|---|---|---|
| cAMP | Adenylyl cyclase | PKA |
| IP₃ | Phospholipase C | ER Ca²⁺ channels |
| DAG | Phospholipase C | PKC (with Ca²⁺) |
| PIP₃ | PI3K | AKT |
| Ca²⁺ | IP₃ or voltage channels | Calmodulin, troponin |

#### Sample Bowl Toss-Ups
*"For 10 points — this lipid second messenger is produced by PI3K and recruits AKT to the plasma membrane."*
**Answer: PIP₃ (phosphatidylinositol 3,4,5-trisphosphate)**

*"For 10 points — this tumor suppressor phosphatase degrades PIP₃ and is commonly mutated in prostate and endometrial cancers."*
**Answer: PTEN**

*"For 10 points — this antibiotic from Easter Island bacteria inhibits mTOR and is used as an immunosuppressant."*
**Answer: rapamycin (sirolimus)**

#### Review Questions
1. Name the enzyme that produces cAMP and the enzyme that destroys it.
2. What two products result from PLC cleavage of PIP₂?
3. PTEN is classified as a tumor suppressor. Explain why, using PIP₃ levels.
