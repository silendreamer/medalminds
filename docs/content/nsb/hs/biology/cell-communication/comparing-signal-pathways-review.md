#### Pathway Identification Decision Tree
Identifying the correct pathway from a description:
1. Does the receptor have 7 transmembrane helices? → **GPCR**
2. Does the receptor dimerize and cross-phosphorylate on tyrosines? → **RTK**
3. Does the receptor have no intrinsic kinase but associates with JAKs? → **JAK-STAT**
4. Does ligand binding directly open an ion channel? → **Ligand-gated ion channel**
5. Does the hormone enter the cell and bind a nuclear receptor? → **Steroid/nuclear receptor**

#### The Central Role of Phosphorylation
Across all non-steroid pathways, **phosphorylation** is the universal currency of signal transduction. Kinases add phosphate groups; phosphatases remove them. The reversibility of phosphorylation makes it perfect for rapid signaling — a single enzyme can toggle a protein's activity on and off without synthesizing or degrading it.

#### Review Questions
1. A signaling molecule activates adenylyl cyclase without directly touching the kinase domain of a receptor. Which receptor type is most likely involved?
2. Compare how GPCR, RTK, and JAK-STAT pathways each activate gene transcription.
3. Why is phosphorylation a more efficient signaling mechanism than de novo protein synthesis?
