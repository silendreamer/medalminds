#### Bowl Toss-up Patterns
"For 10 points, what structure, formed from the ruptured ovarian follicle after ovulation, produces progesterone during the luteal phase?" → **corpus luteum**

"For 10 points, what hormone, secreted by the trophoblast of the early embryo, maintains the corpus luteum during early pregnancy and is detected by home pregnancy tests?" → **hCG** (human chorionic gonadotropin)

"For 10 points, what hormone surge from the anterior pituitary triggers ovulation on approximately day 14 of the menstrual cycle?" → **LH surge** (luteinizing hormone surge)

"For 10 points, what are the cells in the testes that produce testosterone?" → **Leydig cells** (interstitial cells)

"For 10 points, what embryonic stage, consisting of an inner cell mass and trophoblast, implants into the endometrium?" → **blastocyst**

#### High-Yield Facts
- Sperm produced at **~34–35°C** (testes must be in scrotum)
- Secondary oocyte arrested in **metaphase II** until fertilization
- Oogenesis: 1 ovum + 2–3 polar bodies per meiosis
- Germ layers: ecto (skin/nervous), meso (muscle/bone), endo (gut/lungs)
- Folic acid prevents **neural tube defects**

#### Review Questions
1. A bowl question asks: "What extraembryonic membrane forms a fluid-filled sac that cushions the developing fetus?" → What is the answer?
2. Why does the combined oral contraceptive pill prevent pregnancy? Name the two hormones it contains and the mechanism.
3. Trace sperm from formation to ejaculation, naming all structures (seminiferous tubules → epididymis → vas deferens → ejaculatory duct → urethra).
