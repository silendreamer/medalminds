#### Vaccine Types
**Live attenuated**: weakened pathogen (MMR, varicella, yellow fever) — strong immune response, long-lasting, risk in immunocompromised. **Inactivated (killed)**: pathogen killed (influenza injection, polio Salk) — safer, weaker response, needs boosters. **Subunit**: specific proteins (HepB, HPV) — very safe, weaker response. **Toxoid**: inactivated toxin (tetanus, diphtheria) — generates anti-toxin antibodies. **mRNA vaccines** (COVID-19 mRNA vaccines): mRNA encoding antigen → host cells produce antigen → immune response; no DNA, no live pathogen; mRNA degrades quickly.

#### Herd Immunity
When enough of a population is immune (via infection or vaccination), pathogen transmission chains are broken even among unvaccinated individuals. Threshold depends on R0 (basic reproduction number): measles (R0 ~15) requires ~93–95% immunity; COVID-19 original strain (R0 ~2–3) requires ~50–67%. Herd immunity protects immunocompromised individuals who cannot be vaccinated.

#### Immune Evasion and Autoimmunity
HIV infects **CD4+ helper T cells** (via CD4 receptor and CCR5/CXCR4 coreceptors) → destroys the very cells that coordinate adaptive immunity → AIDS (acquired immunodeficiency syndrome). Influenza undergoes **antigenic drift** (point mutations in hemagglutinin/neuraminidase → gradual change → annual vaccine reformulation) and **antigenic shift** (reassortment of RNA segments between strains → sudden major change → pandemic potential). **Autoimmunity**: self-reactive lymphocytes survive self-tolerance checkpoints (thymus negative selection for T cells) → attack self-tissues. Type 1 diabetes (beta cells), multiple sclerosis (myelin), rheumatoid arthritis (synovial joints), lupus (SLE, DNA/nuclear antigens).

#### Review Questions
1. Why must the influenza vaccine be reformulated each year while the measles vaccine provides decades of protection?
2. Explain why HIV infection eventually leads to susceptibility to opportunistic infections like Pneumocystis pneumonia.
3. What is the difference between antigenic drift and antigenic shift in influenza, and which is more likely to cause a pandemic? Why?
