#### Bowl Toss-up Patterns
"For 10 points, what Japanese biologist proposed the neutral theory of molecular evolution in 1968?" → **Motoo Kimura**

"For 10 points, what term describes genes in different species that descended from a single ancestral gene through speciation and typically retain similar function?" → **orthologs**

"For 10 points, what term describes genes within the same organism that arose by gene duplication within a lineage?" → **paralogs**

"For 10 points, what ratio of non-synonymous to synonymous substitution rates is used to detect positive selection?" → **dN/dS ratio** (greater than 1 indicates positive selection)

"For 10 points, what immune system in bacteria and archaea incorporates short viral DNA sequences to recognize future infections, and has been co-opted as a genome editing tool?" → **CRISPR** (CRISPR-Cas)

#### Quick Distinctions
- Neutral mutations → fixed by **genetic drift** (not selection)
- Synonymous substitutions accumulate **faster** than non-synonymous in functional genes
- CRISPR guide RNA → complementary to target sequence; PAM (5'-NGG-3') required
- Orthologs: across species; paralogs: within one organism

#### Review Questions
1. What does a dN/dS ratio of 0.1 indicate about the selection pressure on a gene? What does dN/dS = 3.5 indicate?
2. Name the two scientists awarded the 2020 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for developing CRISPR-Cas9 as a genome editing tool.
3. In CRISPR editing, what happens to DNA at the cut site if repaired by NHEJ rather than HDR?
