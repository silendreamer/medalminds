#### Classic Bowl Questions
"For 10 points, what British naturalist co-discovered natural selection independently of Darwin, prompting Darwin to publish *On the Origin of Species*?" → **Alfred Russel Wallace**

"For 10 points, what type of natural selection occurred in peppered moths during the Industrial Revolution, shifting the population toward darker phenotypes?" → **directional selection**

"For 10 points, what term describes the random change in allele frequency in a small population due to chance sampling?" → **genetic drift**

"For 10 points, what phenomenon occurs when a small group separates from a larger population, carrying only a subset of the parent population's alleles?" → **founder effect**

#### Common Traps
- "Survival of the fittest" → fitness = reproductive success, not physical survival
- Evolution is not "goal-directed" — organisms don't evolve "in order to" do something
- Lamarck proposed use/disuse and inheritance of acquired characteristics — this is **wrong**
- Mutation rates are too low to significantly change allele frequencies in one generation — drift and selection are faster

#### Review Questions
1. What is the key difference between how Darwin and Lamarck explained the evolution of the giraffe's long neck?
2. Why is "survival of the fittest" a somewhat misleading phrase?
3. Name one scientist who contributed to the Modern Synthesis (neo-Darwinism) beyond Darwin.
