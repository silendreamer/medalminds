#### Bowl Toss-up Patterns
"For 10 points, what term describes heritable traits that increase an organism's reproductive success in a given environment?" → **adaptation**

"For 10 points, what type of selection favors both phenotypic extremes and can split a population into two distinct groups?" → **disruptive selection**

"For 10 points, what 19th-century biologist proposed the incorrect idea that organisms pass traits acquired during their lifetime to offspring?" → **Jean-Baptiste Lamarck** (Lamarckian inheritance)

"For 10 points, what is the name for the loss of alleles from a population due to random chance, especially prominent in small populations?" → **genetic drift**

"For 10 points, what transitional fossil, discovered in 2004, showed features of both fish and tetrapods including a flat head and proto-limbs?" → **Tiktaalik**

#### Distinguishing Key Terms
- Fitness = reproductive success (not physical fitness)
- Adaptation = heritable trait increasing fitness (not a response to environment)
- Natural selection acts on **phenotype**; evolution changes **genotype** frequencies
- Genetic drift is **random**; natural selection is **non-random**
- Bottleneck = sudden population crash; Founder effect = small group colonizes new area

#### Review Questions
1. A bowl question asks about the "non-random process that changes allele frequencies based on reproductive success." What is the answer?
2. What distinguishes the founder effect from the bottleneck effect?
3. Name two transitional fossils and the evolutionary transition each documents.
