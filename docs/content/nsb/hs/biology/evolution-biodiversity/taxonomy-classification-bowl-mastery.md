#### Bowl Toss-up Patterns
"For 10 points, what Swedish naturalist developed the hierarchical classification system and binomial nomenclature in the 18th century?" → **Carl Linnaeus** (Carolus Linnaeus)

"For 10 points, what are the three domains of life, as established using ribosomal RNA analysis?" → **Bacteria, Archaea, Eukarya**

"For 10 points, what term describes a group that includes an ancestor and all of its descendants?" → **clade** (or monophyletic group)

"For 10 points, what term describes a shared derived character used to define a clade in cladistic analysis?" → **synapomorphy**

"For 10 points, what molecular technique uses a standardized short gene region to identify species from a tissue sample?" → **DNA barcoding**

#### Mnemonics and High-Yield Facts
- Taxonomic levels: Domain, Kingdom, Phylum, Class, Order, Family, Genus, Species → "Dear King Philip Came Over For Good Soup"
- Archaea: more closely related to **Eukarya** than to Bacteria
- Carl Woese discovered Archaea using **rRNA** (ribosomal RNA) sequences in 1977
- Binomial nomenclature: Genus (capital) + species (lowercase), italicized

#### Review Questions
1. What is the correct format for writing a scientific name? Give an example.
2. Which domain is most closely related to Eukarya based on molecular data?
3. Name the 8 levels of the Linnaean hierarchy from most to least inclusive.
