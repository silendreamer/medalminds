#### Bowl Toss-up Patterns
"For 10 points, what mode of speciation occurs when a geographic barrier separates a population into two isolated groups?" → **allopatric speciation**

"For 10 points, what reproductive isolating mechanism is demonstrated by the sterile offspring of a horse-donkey cross?" → **hybrid sterility** (postzygotic isolation)

"For 10 points, what process, common in plants, involves a doubling of the entire genome and can cause instant reproductive isolation?" → **polyploidy**

"For 10 points, what term describes the rapid diversification of one ancestral species into many species filling available ecological niches?" → **adaptive radiation**

#### Key Distinctions
- Prezygotic isolation prevents mating/fertilization; postzygotic occurs after
- Allopatric = geographic barrier; sympatric = no barrier
- Polyploidy → most common in **plants** (wheat, cotton, bananas)
- Mule is an example of **hybrid sterility** (postzygotic)
- Darwin's finches = classic **adaptive radiation** example

#### Review Questions
1. A bowl question asks: "What type of isolation prevents two populations from mating because they breed in different seasons?" → What category and specific type is the answer?
2. What crop plant is an allohexaploid that arose through hybridization and polyploidy?
3. Name two examples of adaptive radiation.
