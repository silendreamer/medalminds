#### Top Bowl Facts: Geologic Time
- Earth's age: **4.6 billion years** (4.6 Ga)
- Precambrian = **88%** of Earth history
- Cambrian explosion: **~541 Ma** — rapid appearance of most animal body plans
- End-Permian extinction: **~252 Ma** — largest mass extinction (~96% marine species)
- K-Pg extinction: **~66 Ma** — Chicxulub impact; end of non-avian dinosaurs
- We live in: Phanerozoic Eon → Cenozoic Era → Quaternary Period → Holocene Epoch
- The "Great Dying" = Permian-Triassic extinction
- Geologic time unit hierarchy: Eon → Era → Period → Epoch → Age

#### Toss-Up Stem Patterns
*"For 10 points, what era is characterized by the age of reptiles, including the period when non-avian dinosaurs dominated terrestrial ecosystems?"* → **Mesozoic Era**

*"For 10 points, the largest mass extinction in Earth history, which eliminated approximately 96 percent of marine species at the end of this period, marks the boundary between the Paleozoic and Mesozoic eras."* → **Permian** (or Permian-Triassic boundary)

*"For 10 points, what explosion of multicellular animal diversity, occurring approximately 541 million years ago at the base of the Paleozoic Era, saw the rapid appearance of most major animal body plans?"* → **Cambrian explosion**

#### Review Questions
1. What eon, era, period, and epoch do we currently live in?
2. Which two mass extinctions are associated with large igneous province (flood basalt) volcanism?
3. A bowl question asks about the "age of fishes" — which period does this refer to?
