#### Top Bowl Facts
- Mid-Atlantic Ridge: separates North America from Europe (divergent)
- Mariana Trench: deepest point on Earth, ~11 km; oceanic-oceanic subduction
- San Andreas Fault: Pacific-North American transform, ~46 mm/yr
- Himalayas: India-Asia collision; highest peak Everest 8,849 m
- Ring of Fire: ~75% of active volcanoes; ~90% of earthquakes
- Hotspot creates volcanic chain: Hawaii-Emperor; Yellowstone; Iceland
- Seafloor spreading confirmed by: symmetric magnetic anomaly stripes
- Harry Hess: proposed seafloor spreading (1960); Vine-Matthews-Morley confirmed it magnetically (1963)

#### Toss-Up Stems
*"For 10 points, what process occurs at mid-ocean ridges where magma wells up between separating plates to create new oceanic crust?"* → **Seafloor spreading**

*"For 10 points, the Pacific Plate moves over a stationary mantle plume, creating the volcanic island chain whose youngest member is the Big Island."* → **Hawaiian hotspot** (accept Hawaiian-Emperor chain)

*"For 10 points, what type of plate boundary is marked by the San Andreas Fault, where the Pacific Plate slides northwestward relative to the North American Plate?"* → **Transform boundary** (accept transform fault, strike-slip)

#### Review Questions
1. Name the deepest oceanic trench and the plates responsible for it.
2. What evidence did Harry Hess use to propose seafloor spreading?
3. In a bowl question about "the theory that unifies earthquakes, volcanoes, and mountain building," what is the answer?
