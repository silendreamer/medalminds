#### Top 20 High-Yield Facts

1. **Moho depth**: ~35 km continental / ~7 km oceanic
2. **Moho discovered by**: Andrija Mohorovičić, 1909
3. **Gutenberg discontinuity**: mantle-outer core, 2,900 km — S-waves cannot pass
4. **Lehmann discontinuity**: outer-inner core, 5,150 km — discovered by Inge Lehmann, 1936
5. **Inner core state**: SOLID (pressure exceeds melting point)
6. **Outer core composition**: liquid iron-nickel alloy
7. **Outer core function**: generates Earth's magnetic field (geodynamo)
8. **Asthenosphere**: partially molten upper mantle, ~100–350 km, enables plate motion
9. **Lithosphere**: rigid plates = crust + uppermost mantle
10. **Continental crust rock type**: granite (felsic; SIAL = Silicon + Aluminum)
11. **Oceanic crust rock type**: basalt (mafic; SIMA = Silicon + Magnesium)
12. **Mantle rock type**: peridotite (ultramafic)
13. **P-wave shadow zone**: 103°–142° from epicenter
14. **S-wave shadow zone**: beyond 103° (S-waves can't enter liquid outer core)
15. **Deepest borehole**: Kola Superdeep Borehole, Russia, ~12.2 km
16. **Earth's average density**: 5.5 g/cm³ (surface rocks are ~2.7 → dense interior)
17. **Inner core temperature**: ~5,000–6,000°C
18. **Geothermal gradient (crust)**: ~25–30°C/km
19. **Mantle fraction of Earth's volume**: ~84%
20. **Differentiation**: heavy Fe/Ni sank to core, light Si/Al rose to surface, early in Earth's history

#### Common Bowl Traps
- "Shadow zone" → specify P-wave (103°–142°) vs. S-wave (beyond 103°); they're different
- Inner core is SOLID, outer core is LIQUID — don't swap them
- Moho = crust-mantle boundary; Gutenberg = mantle-core boundary; don't confuse
- Lehmann discontinuity was discovered by a woman (Inge Lehmann) — bowls sometimes test this

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "For 10 points, name the seismologist who discovered the inner-outer core boundary in 1936." *(Inge Lehmann)*
2. "For 10 points, name the boundary between Earth's mantle and outer core, located at approximately 2,900 kilometers depth." *(Gutenberg discontinuity)*
3. "For 10 points, what state of matter is Earth's outer core, and what important phenomenon does its motion generate?" *(Liquid; Earth's magnetic field / geodynamo)*
4. "For 10 points, name the partially molten layer of the upper mantle upon which tectonic plates slide." *(Asthenosphere)*
5. "For 10 points, why does the inner core remain solid despite temperatures exceeding 5,000 degrees Celsius?" *(Immense pressure raises the melting point of iron above the actual temperature)*

#### Review Questions
1. A bowl question asks "name the boundary where S-waves disappear." What is the answer and at what depth is it located?
2. Which layer generates Earth's magnetic field, and why does that layer have the property needed to do so?
3. What is the Kola Superdeep Borehole and why is it significant in the study of Earth's interior?
