#### Top 20 Facts

1. Wegener named the supercontinent **Pangaea** (proposed 1912)
2. Southern supercontinent = **Gondwana** (Africa, S. America, India, Australia, Antarctica)
3. Northern supercontinent = **Laurasia** (N. America, Europe, Asia)
4. Seafloor spreading proposed by **Harry Hess**, 1962
5. Magnetic anomaly stripes explained by **Vine, Matthews, and Morley**, 1963
6. Mid-ocean ridges = divergent boundaries; form new oceanic crust
7. Subduction zones = convergent boundaries; destroy old oceanic crust
8. Oldest oceanic crust: ~200 Ma; oldest continental: ~4.0 Ga (Acasta Gneiss)
9. Mariana Trench = deepest point on Earth (~11 km); produced by Pacific-Philippine convergence
10. San Andreas Fault = right-lateral transform; Pacific-North American boundary
11. Himalayas = India-Eurasia continental collision; began ~50 Ma; still rising
12. Hawaii hot spot: Pacific Plate moves ~9 cm/yr northwest over it
13. Bend in Hawaii-Emperor chain at 43 Ma = change in Pacific Plate direction
14. Dominant driving force = **slab pull** (dense cold slab sinks, pulls rest of plate)
15. Fast spreading ridge = East Pacific Rise (~15 cm/yr); slow = Mid-Atlantic (~2.5 cm/yr per side)
16. Glossopteris: fossil fern found on 5 continents; key Wegener evidence
17. Mesosaurus: freshwater reptile fossil found only in S. America and Africa
18. East African Rift = active continental rifting (may become ocean in ~10 Ma)
19. Island arcs form above subducting oceanic plates (Japan, Aleutians, Philippines)
20. GPS now directly measures plate motions (confirmed ~cm/yr rates)

#### Practice Toss-Up Stems
1. "For 10 points, name the scientist who proposed seafloor spreading in 1962." *(Harry Hess)*
2. "For 10 points, what is the deepest point in the ocean, and in which ocean is it located?" *(Mariana Trench / Challenger Deep; Pacific Ocean)*
3. "For 10 points, name the fossil fern found on five widely separated continents, which served as key evidence for continental drift." *(Glossopteris)*
4. "For 10 points, what supercontinent began breaking apart approximately 180 million years ago?" *(Pangaea)*
5. "For 10 points, name the type of plate motion that is considered the dominant driving force of plate tectonics." *(Slab pull)*

#### Review Questions
1. Who proposed seafloor spreading, who explained the magnetic anomaly stripes, and when did each of these developments occur?
2. Name three specific examples of transform faults worldwide.
3. Why do island arcs form parallel to ocean trenches, and what type of volcanism do they produce?
